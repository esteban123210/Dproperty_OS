## Positioning

Gesti68 is a compact urban project designed around personalization, central San Francisco living and unusually complete small-unit functionality. Although marketed as “studios,” the plans show a separated bedroom, two bathrooms, living/dining area, kitchen, laundry and one parking space.

The principal differentiators are parking for every residence, double glazing, 2.75 m internal height, customizable Italian-material packages and a location between Calle 50 and Vía Porras.
