## Positioning

Sky Parc II is a standardized two-bedroom product in Costa del Este, combining an efficient 85 m² layout with high-rise amenities and a clear view-based price structure. Models A/B face Avenida Centenario; models C/D face Parque Felipe Motta and command the strongest premiums.

The product is suited to small families, couples requiring a second room, executives and investors who prefer a simple, comparable unit format.
