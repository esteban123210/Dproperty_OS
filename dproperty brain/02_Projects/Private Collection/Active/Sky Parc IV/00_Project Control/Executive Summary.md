## Positioning

Sky Parc IV is presented as the next evolution of Urbania’s Sky Parc line, using the prior projects’ market history as proof of concept. Its broad product ladder allows the project to target first-time buyers and investors through compact one-bedroom units while also serving couples and families with larger two- and three-bedroom residences.
