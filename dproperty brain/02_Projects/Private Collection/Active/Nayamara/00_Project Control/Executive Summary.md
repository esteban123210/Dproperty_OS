## Positioning

Nayamara is positioned as a private waterfront sanctuary in Amador built around calm, privacy, curated materials and a slower rhythm of life. The project uses the jaguar as a symbol of quiet strength and organizes the experience through three spatial moments: The Umbral arrival, Collection Homes and The Zenith social/wellness levels.

Its strongest commercial differentiators are the waterfront setting, three view families, extensive upper-level wellness programme, terraces and a product range that spans compact one-bedroom units to larger two-bedroom homes.

## Location thesis

Nayamara sits on the Amador waterfront near the convention center, cruise port, Casco Antiguo, Cinta Costera, restaurants and central Panama City. The investment thesis combines scarcity of waterfront land, tourism/leisure infrastructure and differentiated views, but must be tested against delivery timing, maintenance and rental rules.
