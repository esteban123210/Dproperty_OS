## Positioning

Cavarosa Signature Residences is a waterfront luxury project inspired by Italian coastal caves, Mediterranean beach-club culture, yachting, fashion and highly curated hospitality. The architecture is a primary differentiator: a curved, recognizable tower with continuous terraces, large arched openings, formal landscaped arrival and direct relationship with Amador’s waterfront promenade.

The likely buyer profile is a high-income end user, second-home buyer or international investor seeking design identity, terraces, sea views and a resident club rather than a standardized tower product.

## Main project components

### Signature Residences

Private homes facing the water, designed around uninterrupted views, detailed interiors and terraces.

### Boardwalk Gallery

The lower tower combines private interior spaces with an open-air plaza and hospitality/restaurant-style frontage. Public versus resident-only access, commercial ownership and operating costs are not established.

### Club Cavarosa

Resident club across the top levels with Mediterranean hospitality positioning.

## Location thesis

Amador combines waterfront recreation, tourism infrastructure, convention demand, cruise activity, marina/yachting appeal and proximity to Casco Antiguo and central Panama City. The project’s value thesis depends heavily on protected views, waterfront rights, club operation and luxury-market absorption.
