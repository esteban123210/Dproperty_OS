## Positioning

Dovle Selva combines compact double-height residences with technology, renewable-energy claims, nature, content-production spaces, coworking and hotel-permit investment functionality. It is positioned as a “new world” residential product for buyers who value flexibility, productivity and sustainability.

The sales strategy must distinguish between conventional residential units and units legally authorized for hotel/short-term operation. Hotel-permit benefits cannot be generalized to the full building without unit-level legal confirmation.
