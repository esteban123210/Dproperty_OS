## Positioning

Boreal is a wellness-led urban residence built around the idea of living calmly while remaining in the center of Panama City. The brochure emphasizes natural light, cross ventilation, functional proportions, warm materials, integrated vegetation and communal spaces that support work, movement, recovery and social life.

The strongest buyer profiles are likely urban professionals, couples, downsizers and families who value Obarrio’s centrality but want a more composed residential environment than a conventional city tower.
