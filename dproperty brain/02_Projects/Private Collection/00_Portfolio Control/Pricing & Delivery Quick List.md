## Notes on data quality (per Missing Information Tracker)

- All price figures above are **dated stacking-plan/price-list snapshots** (BIOMA 2026-05-28, Sky Parc II undated, Nayamara 2026-07-13, Gesti68 2026-06-05, Playa Escondida - Torre 200 2026-07-24) and must be reconfirmed before any client-facing quote.
- Nayamara: 124 of 161 stack positions are marked "Reserved," not counted as sold or priced; the 37-unit price stats above reflect only the visibly priced positions.
- Gesti68: stats reflect only the 37 currently **available** units out of 80 total (38 sold, 5 reserved).
- Playa Escondida - Torre 200: stats reflect 88 of 104 visible positions (16 reserved, excluded).
- Sky Parc II: only 19 of 152 total units have a supplied price (12.5% of inventory); the other 133 have unknown status.
- No project in this list has an official developer payment plan, delivery date, or signed Dproperty commission agreement on file.
