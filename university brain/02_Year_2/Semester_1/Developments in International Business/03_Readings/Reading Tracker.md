## Reading-note extraction rule
For every required reading, capture only what will help with cases or the exam:
- **Question addressed**
- **Core thesis**
- **Framework / mechanism**
- **Variables / dimensions**
- **Key evidence**
- **Managerial implication**
- **Boundary conditions / critique**
- **Links to other readings**
- **One-sentence exam retrieval cue**
