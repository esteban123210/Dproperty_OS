## Mechanism and managerial relevance

Export-credit insurance helps exporters bridge long payment periods and liquidity gaps while lowering lender risk. The buyer gains longer repayment terms and potentially lower financing cost; the exporter gains financing and competitiveness; the bank gains reduced exposure. Atradius Dutch State Business acts for the Dutch government. Underwriting combines financial, environmental/social and compliance assessment. The lecture connects export finance with climate transition, critical materials, innovation and strategic autonomy. Its charts and statistics refer to the supplied September 2026 presentation.
