## Session 1 conceptual chain

1. The world is neither fully global nor purely domestic: it is **semi-globalized**.
2. Cross-border activity still faces distance, institutional, informational, cultural, and political frictions.
3. Those frictions create a **liability of foreignness** for foreign firms.
4. To compete abroad, firms require **firm-specific advantages (FSAs)** sufficient to offset those disadvantages.
5. Cross-border market transactions also create **transaction costs**.
6. When internal organization is more efficient than arm's-length market exchange, firms may **internalize** activities.
7. Combining **Ownership, Location, and Internalization** advantages explains when FDI and multinational enterprise activity become attractive.
