SHOPEE HITTING THE PAUSE BUTTON ON INTERNATIONAL EXPANSION

Condensed Case Session 7

Pangarkar, N., and Yadav, N. (2022). Shopee: Hitting the Pause Button on International Expansion? Ivey Publishing.

## PART I WHAT IS THIS CASE REALLY ABOUT

In May 2022, Shopee had to decide whether a period of rapid international expansion had created a stronger global platform or had stretched the company beyond what its economics, management capacity and local legitimacy could support. The immediate warning signs were its withdrawals from France and India only months after entry, widening losses in e-commerce and a sharp fall in the share price of parent company Sea Limited.

The case is not simply a choice between growth and profitability. Shopee had already shown that international expansion could work. It had built a leading position in Southeast Asia and Taiwan and had gained strong traction in Brazil. The difficult question was why the same mobile-first, social and gamified platform produced durable positions in some markets but only expensive experiments in others.

Management therefore needed to separate three types of evidence. First, did a market generate real customer and seller value rather than subsidized activity? Second, could Shopee transfer its capabilities while adapting logistics, regulation and stakeholder relationships locally? Third, did the market have a credible path to positive contribution before cash, management attention and investor patience ran out?

The case ends without a final answer. Shopee still held more than US$9 billion in cash and cash equivalents in early 2022, but the financial market had become less willing to reward growth without proof of sustainable value. The next phase required more selective geography, clearer entry and exit gates and a better distinction between regional scale and simultaneous entry into distant markets.

THE CASE IN ONE SENTENCE Shopee proved that its platform could travel, but France and India showed that demand growth alone cannot justify expansion unless country economics, organizational capacity and local legitimacy are also strong.

## PART II THE COMPANY AND ITS WINNING FORMULA

## Sea Limited and the internal funding model

Forrest Li founded Garena in 2009 with Gang Ye and David Chen. Garena began as a digital games business and launched Shopee in 2015. The group was renamed Sea Limited in 2017 and listed on the New York Stock Exchange that year, raising US$884 million. Tencent was an early investor and still owned 18.7 percent after selling part of its stake in January 2022.

By 2022, Sea operated three connected businesses. Garena produced and published games; Shopee operated e-commerce marketplaces; and SeaMoney provided digital payments and financial services. This portfolio supported cross-business learning and ecosystem effects, but it also created a funding dependency. Digital entertainment was the group's only profitable division and financed losses in e-commerce and financial services. The model worked while gaming profits, cash reserves and investor confidence were strong. It became more fragile when Free Fire faced regulatory pressure and Sea's share price fell.

Business | Strategic role | Case implication

Garena | Profitable gaming engine and source of users, digital capabilities and cash. | Its success helped fund Shopee; the Free Fire ban in India showed that cross-business political risk could damage the whole group.

Shopee | Mobile-first marketplace connecting consumers and sellers through payments, logistics and marketing services. | It delivered very fast revenue growth but remained deeply loss-making.

SeaMoney | Payments and financial services supporting transactions and ecosystem depth. | It could strengthen platform stickiness, but it was also loss-making and required investment.

## How Shopee competed

Shopee described itself as a scalable marketplace rather than a traditional retailer. It connected buyers and sellers and monetized the platform through advertising, transaction fees and value-added services such as logistics. Its strategy combined four elements that had worked especially well in Southeast Asia:

Mobile first Shopee designed the platform around consumers who accessed the Internet and shopped through smartphones. By 2020, most transactions were mobile.

Hyper-local execution Local offices and teams adapted product categories, campaigns and operations to each country instead of imposing one identical regional offer.

Social and gamified engagement Community features and games increased user acquisition, time spent and retention. In Brazil, mini-games and coupons reinforced the platform's appeal.

Seller enablement Shopee gave sellers tools to manage sales, payments, inventory and deliveries, while promotional support and low fees encouraged participation.

These features created rapid adoption, but they did not guarantee profitable growth. Free shipping, zero or low commissions, discounts and advertising-led monetization could make downloads and orders grow before the market generated enough revenue to cover logistics, promotion and local overhead.

## PART III INTERNATIONAL EXPANSION AND RETRENCHMENT

## The sequence of expansion

Period | Expansion step | What it demonstrated

2015 onward | Shopee built scale in Southeast Asia and Taiwan and overtook earlier entrant Lazada in regional leadership. | The platform could expand quickly where mobile behavior, market knowledge and organizational routines were relatively reusable.

2019 | Localized launch in Brazil. | A distant-region entry could succeed when Shopee had time to localize and build a new market base.

2021 | Mexico, Argentina, Chile and Colombia; then Poland, Spain and France; India in November. | The company increased geographic distance and the number of simultaneous learning problems.

March 2022 | France and India closed within the same month. | Fast entry generated information and some traction, but did not prove sustainable market positions.

## Southeast Asia and Taiwan the proven regional base

Shopee became the largest pan-regional e-commerce platform in Southeast Asia and Taiwan despite entering after Alibaba-backed Lazada. Its local teams, mobile design, seller tools and social engagement matched the region's conditions. This is the strongest evidence that international expansion strengthened Shopee's competitive position. It built scale, brand recognition, data, seller networks and operating knowledge across multiple markets rather than succeeding in only one home country.

## Brazil a successful distant-market experiment

Shopee entered Brazil in 2019 and became the country's most-downloaded shopping app within two years. The entry benefited from several reinforcing factors: Free Fire already had a large local audience; Shopee used games and coupons; it attracted small businesses through free shipping and very low commissions; and COVID-19 accelerated e-commerce adoption. Morgan Stanley estimated that the share of Latin American consumers using Shopee rose from 6 percent in 2020 to 37 percent in 2021.

Brazil matters because it prevents an overly simple conclusion that Shopee should remain only in nearby Asian markets. Geographic distance did not make success impossible. However, Brazil also differs from the rushed 2021 wave: Shopee had more time to learn and localize before using it as a possible base for wider Latin American expansion.

## India strong demand but a weak operating and political foundation

India looked attractive. Internet users exceeded 830 million in 2021, online sales were growing rapidly and forecasts expected the e-commerce market to reach roughly US$99-130 billion by 2024-25. Shopee entered in November 2021 with free delivery, no seller commission and a product mix concentrated in fashion, mobile accessories and home products. Because it lacked its own logistics network, it relied on partners such as Delhivery and had slower delivery than some competitors.

Demand indicators were impressive. By January 2022 the app had been downloaded more than 20 million times; by February it reportedly had more than 21 million monthly active users and processed ten million orders a day. Yet this growth was not sufficient to protect the business. Trader association CAIT accused Shopee of violating foreign-investment rules, facilitating Chinese goods and using predatory practices. Other allegations involved fake products, data security and the structure of the Indian entity. The Competition Commission rejected the predatory-pricing complaint, but the wider legitimacy problem remained.

The political shock came when India banned Free Fire and other apps associated with China on national-security grounds. Sea insisted that it was Singaporean and did not store Indian user data in China, but the ban erased more than US$16 billion from Sea's market capitalization and showed how perceptions of ownership and geopolitical affiliation could affect the full corporate portfolio. Shopee closed India on March 28, 2022, only about four months after launch.

## France limited differentiation in a mature crowded market

Shopee entered France in October 2021 and exited in early March 2022, describing the venture as a preliminary pilot. France offered a large market but also approximately 120,000 active e-commerce sites, strong international competitors and many local alternatives. Unlike India, the case does not identify a major political confrontation as the main cause. France is therefore evidence that institutional acceptance alone is insufficient: Shopee also needed a distinctive local value proposition and credible economics.

## PART V BALANCED EVALUATION OF INTERNATIONAL PERFORMANCE

## What expansion achieved

Shopee's internationalization was not a simple failure. It created a leading Southeast Asian platform, overcame a first-mover disadvantage against Lazada, established a credible position in Taiwan and showed in Brazil that its capabilities could be adapted beyond Asia. These positions increased platform scale, seller and customer reach, data, brand recognition and the option to develop payments, logistics and advertising services around the marketplace.

Expansion also generated valuable learning. France tested Shopee's ability to differentiate in a mature European market. India exposed political, regulatory, identity and stakeholder risks that downloads and orders did not reveal. Exiting early limited further losses. These benefits matter, but they justify costs only if the company records the learning and improves later market selection and entry design.

## What expansion had not yet proved

The case does not demonstrate that Shopee's complete international footprint created sustainable value. E-commerce losses widened as revenue grew, several distant markets were entered almost simultaneously and two high-profile entries were closed within months. Sea's cash position provided time, but cash availability is not evidence that an investment is economically attractive. Nor are app downloads, gross merchandise value or order volume sufficient when growth is supported by free shipping, low fees and discounts.

Market cluster | Evidence of strength | Main unresolved issue | Case-based assessment

Southeast Asia and Taiwan | Regional leadership, reusable routines, integrated ecosystem and the largest revenue base. | Shopee still needed to improve e-commerce profitability and reduce subsidy dependence. | Strongest evidence that internationalization built a durable competitive position.

Brazil | Most-downloaded shopping app, strong consumer adoption and rapid Latin American revenue growth. | Low fees and free shipping may have bought share; country-level profit data are absent. | Promising new regional base, but sustainable value still required economic proof.

Other Latin America | Brazil offered a platform for expansion and the region grew quickly. | Entries were recent and transferability from Brazil was not yet demonstrated. | Continue selectively and scale country by country.

France | Large addressable market and a low-cost pilot generated quick information. | Crowded market, weak differentiation and no evidence of viable economics. | Disciplined exit, but not evidence of a stronger lasting position.

India | Very rapid adoption, more than 21 million monthly active users and high order volume. | Weak legitimacy, regulatory and geopolitical exposure, partner-dependent logistics and uncertain unit economics. | Demand success did not become a sustainable right or ability to operate.

## The central diagnosis

Shopee used one growth system across very different market types: rapid launch, aggressive promotion, seller acquisition and mobile engagement. That system was powerful for proving demand. It was weaker as a method for proving durable value because it could produce large usage numbers before the company understood regulatory acceptance, competitive differentiation, logistics quality and country contribution. The required correction was not to abandon internationalization, but to change its pace, sequence and evidence gates.

## TEAM A POSITION

Core thesis. International expansion strengthened Shopee by creating regional scale, a leading Southeast Asian platform and a promising Latin American base. France and India were bounded experiments rather than proof that the overall strategy failed.

Best evidence Shopee overtook Lazada in Southeast Asia and Taiwan; Brazil became the country's most-downloaded shopping app; Latin American revenue grew more than sixfold between 2019 and 2021.

Why the costs can be justified Entry spending bought users, sellers, data, brand recognition and local learning. Early withdrawal from weak markets prevented experiments from becoming permanent commitments.

Theory support Kim shows that rapid regional expansion can create value when capabilities are reusable. Henisz allows Team A to argue that local seller, regulator and partner relationships can become valuable political and social capital.

Main vulnerability Team A must prove durable economics. Growth, downloads and market share do not show that customer acquisition, free shipping and localization costs will be recovered.

## TEAM B POSITION

Core thesis. Shopee expanded faster and farther than its management systems and economics could support. The 2021 wave increased losses and complexity without proving a durable advantage in the new markets.

Best evidence E-commerce losses widened to US$2.767 billion in 2021; France and India closed after only a few months; Sea's share price fell and the business remained dependent on gaming profits.

Why the footprint weakened performance Multiple simultaneous entries fragmented attention and required promotion, logistics, localization and compliance investments before any distant-market model was proven.

Theory support Kim predicts that fast inter-regional expansion can destroy value when learning is compressed. Henisz explains why India's stakeholder conflict and weak local legitimacy prevented strong demand from becoming durable cash flow.

Main vulnerability Team B should not treat every entry as a failure. Southeast Asia and Brazil show that expansion created real strategic positions, and rapid exit from weak pilots can be a sign of discipline.

## NON DEBATING TEAM ASSIGNMENT

Briefly evaluate Shopee's internationalisation performance. What evidence should management use to determine whether its international expansion is creating sustainable value, and what should Shopee prioritise over the next 3-5 years?

## Recommended balanced answer

Shopee's internationalization performance was mixed but strategically valuable. It clearly strengthened the company's position in Southeast Asia and created a promising second regional base in Brazil. However, the rapid inter-regional expansion of 2021 did not justify its full costs: France and India were exited quickly, e-commerce losses widened and high demand in India did not produce a durable license to operate. The correct conclusion is therefore selective consolidation, not global retreat or renewed expansion at the previous pace.

## Evidence management should use to test sustainable value

Evidence area | Measures to track | Why it matters

Country economics | Contribution margin after promotions, shipping, payment, returns, local staff and headquarters allocation; cash burn; time to break even. | Shows whether growth creates value rather than only activity.

Demand quality | Repeat purchase, cohort retention, order frequency, organic traffic, customer-acquisition payback and reduced dependence on discounts. | Separates durable customer behavior from subsidized downloads and orders.

Seller ecosystem | Active and retained sellers, seller concentration, local assortment, advertising uptake, fulfilment quality and seller satisfaction. | A marketplace needs both sides of the platform to remain attractive.

Capability transfer | How much technology, gamification, payments, marketing and operating routines can be reused without expensive redesign. | Tests whether Shopee has a scalable advantage in the target market.

Stakeholder legitimacy | Regulatory readiness, policy stability, disputes, trader-group opposition, consumer trust, data concerns and partner support. | Determines whether Shopee can continue operating and convert demand into cash flow.

Organizational capacity | Local leadership depth, logistics and service capability, compliance resources, management attention and number of simultaneous launches. | Prevents expansion speed from outrunning the company's ability to learn and coordinate.

Portfolio learning | Milestones met, cost and speed of exit, lessons transferred to later entries and evidence that one market creates a regional platform. | Makes pilots valuable even when management decides not to scale them.

## Priorities for the next 3-5 years

Consolidate Southeast Asia and Taiwan Improve contribution margins and deepen the Shopee, SeaMoney and logistics ecosystem where the company already has scale, knowledge and stronger stakeholder relationships.

Build Latin America as a cluster around Brazil Treat Brazil as a base for transferable regional capabilities, but require each additional country to prove demand quality, legitimacy and economics before scaling.

Use staged entry in distant markets Begin with limited pilots, local partnerships and explicit learning objectives. Increase commitment only after predetermined commercial and nonmarket milestones are met.

Make stakeholder readiness an entry gate Map regulators, trader groups, sellers, logistics and payment partners, data concerns and political sensitivities before launch. Give local teams ownership of these relationships.

Restore capital discipline and use clear decision gates Protect cash through country-level loss limits and customer-acquisition payback targets. Scale when retention, unit economics and stakeholder support improve together; maintain while the evidence improves; exit when structural barriers persist.

## Suggested one pager storyline

Start with the verdict Expansion built real regional advantages, but the 2021 distant-market wave did not yet create sustainable value.

Show the contrast Southeast Asia and Brazil demonstrate potential; France and India reveal failures of fit, pace, economics and legitimacy.

Define sustainable value Use financial, customer, seller, capability, stakeholder and organizational evidence rather than downloads or revenue alone.

End with the strategy Consolidate the core, build a Latin American cluster and use gated pilots for any new distant market.

## PART VII LINKAGE TO SESSION 7 READINGS

## Article 1 Kim 2020 What is the best pace of expansion

Kim provides the geography-and-speed logic. Fast expansion can work inside a familiar region because knowledge and capabilities are more reusable. Fast expansion across distant regions is more dangerous because regulation, customers, partners and organizational routines must be learned at the same time. Shopee's Southeast Asian growth fits the first pattern. The simultaneous 2021 entries into Latin America, Europe and India fit the warning about time-compression diseconomies and management complexity. Brazil shows that a distant region can still succeed when entry is sequenced and localization has time to develop.

Use in the assignment. Assess pace by cluster rather than labeling Shopee simply fast or slow. Ask whether the country can reuse proven capabilities, whether the local organization has learned enough and whether several entries can be coordinated without degrading performance.

## Article 2 Henisz Dorobantu and Nartey 2014 Stakeholder engagement and financial value

Henisz and colleagues explain why legal market entry and customer demand are not enough. Firms also need a practical social license to operate: cooperation from regulators, communities, partners and other actors who can delay or disrupt the business. India is the clearest application. Shopee generated enormous user and order growth but faced trader opposition, FDI allegations, product-authenticity concerns, data-security fears and geopolitical spillover from the Free Fire ban. These conflicts raised the probability that the expected cash flows could not be realized.

Use in the assignment. Track stakeholder cooperation, policy credibility and the operational consequences of conflict alongside financial metrics. France also sets a boundary: the absence of intense stakeholder conflict does not make a market attractive when competitive fit and economics are weak.

Where should Shopee expand? | Prefer regional clusters where capabilities travel; treat distant regions as experiments. | Prefer markets where policy is credible and stakeholders can support a durable right to operate. | A market must pass both capability-transfer and legitimacy tests.

How fast should Shopee expand? | Move faster in proven clusters and more slowly across distant regions. | Relationships and social capital take time and cannot be created only through spending. | Scale only after learning, stakeholder support and operating economics are visible.

What proves sustainable value? | Performance improves when pace matches distance and organizational capacity. | Cooperation protects execution and reduces the discount on expected cash flows. | Use contribution, retention, seller health, transferability, legitimacy and execution evidence together.

Assignment focused conclusion. Shopee should not return to one global expansion speed. Over the next three to five years it should move faster only inside proven regional clusters, while treating new distant markets as staged options. Commitment should rise only when customer and seller behavior, country economics, local stakeholder support and organizational capacity all show that the platform can create durable value.
