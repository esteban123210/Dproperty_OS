# DIB Quest

A standalone, offline study game for the uploaded Developments in International Business course pack.

## Open and play

Download `DIB-Quest.html` and open it in a modern browser. GitHub displays HTML source rather than running this file. No installation, account, server or network access is required for the game. Links to canonical repository notes require internet access, but their text is also embedded in the game.

1. Choose a grade ambition. All tracks are selectable immediately.
2. Open any of the seven weeks and read its topic briefings.
3. Play a session mission or a mixed ten-question raid. Choose an answer, commit it and inspect the reasoning. Options shuffle; repeated cleared questions do not farm XP.
4. Use Repair mistakes for wrong or hinted answers. A hinted attempt is guided practice and does not clear a quiz skill.
5. At Mastery, write and review the topic challenges and the four practice-exam responses.

These are study ambitions, not calibrated grade predictions or an official university marking rubric. Course completion reflects practice coverage, not independently demonstrated mastery. The exam also requires interpretation and writing that multiple-choice questions cannot grade.

## Feedback and writing

Every quiz has an explanation and links to the underlying source. Higher-level study briefs add mechanisms, empirical evidence, comparisons and boundary conditions.

Topic writing challenges target 80–180 words; the supplied mock sheet specifies 250–500 words per question and 120 minutes total. Drafts must reach the minimum before the guide and five-criterion rubric open. Written milestones require a self-assessed score of at least 8/10 with no criterion missing. They are explicitly self-reviewed, not automatically graded or lecturer-approved. Use external feedback to validate your judgment. The optional timer is for practice and runs while the page is open; it is not a saved examination session.

## Progress and backup

Progress and drafts stay in browser local storage when available. Changing browser or moving the file may change the storage origin. The game never uploads your answers. Use Save / restore to download a JSON backup or show/select backup text. The text option works when a browser blocks downloads. Restore a file or pasted backup after confirmation. Reset is also confirmed.

## Coverage

The campaign contains 47 topics, 141 quiz questions, 51 writing challenges and 58 embedded knowledge files. Its seven weeks include readings, foundational lecture concepts and case-transfer practice. The course outline excludes direct examination of debated cases; these are labelled as transfer laboratories.

Missing source material remains missing: Session 7 slides, the Lindner & Puck textbook chapters, announced AI guest-talk materials and an official mock answer key. Figure images remain in canonical repository notes; the standalone library preserves the knowledge-file text and figure references, not every image. Original publications, AI-labelled condensed aids and submitted work retain distinct provenance.
