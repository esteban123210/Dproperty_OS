# Exam Strategy and Framework Index

## Exam facts
- **Weight:** 60%
- **Format:** Open book
- **Question style:** Short answer and/or essay
- **Source base:** Slides and readings
- **Cases:** In-class / debated cases are not directly examined
- **Pass requirement:** Exam grade must be at least 5.5

## What the exam is actually testing
1. **Comprehension** - explain the theory accurately.
2. **Application** - use a framework to diagnose a new business situation.
3. **Comparison** - distinguish competing IB strategies or perspectives.
4. **Judgment** - make a recommendation and justify it under uncertainty.

## Open-book strategy
Open book is only useful if retrieval is fast. Do not build long reading summaries as the primary study asset. Build a searchable index around **questions and frameworks**.

### For every framework, capture
- **Framework name**
- **Author / source**
- **Problem it solves**
- **Core dimensions / variables**
- **Mechanism**
- **Managerial implication**
- **Boundary conditions**
- **Useful comparison**
- **One-sentence retrieval cue**

## Likely comparison families
Prepare explicit compare/contrast notes for:
- Market attractiveness **vs** distance.
- Country selection **vs** entry-mode selection.
- Low-control entry modes **vs** JVs **vs** full ownership.
- Cultural distance **vs** institutional/political distance.
- Traditional political risk **vs** geopolitical risk.
- Host-country risk **vs** bilateral/network geopolitical exposure.
- Standardization/global integration **vs** localization/local responsiveness.
- Faster expansion **vs** capability-constrained expansion.
- Traditional MNE logic **vs** digital/AI-enabled MNE logic.

## Answer architecture for an essay question
### 1. Diagnose
Identify the actual strategic problem and the relevant cross-border dimension.

### 2. Select
Choose 1-3 frameworks that directly address the problem.

### 3. Apply
Use facts from the exam case to populate the framework dimensions.

### 4. Compare
Explain why an alternative framework or strategy would produce a different conclusion.

### 5. Recommend
State what management should do, the trade-off accepted, and the conditions under which the recommendation holds.
