# Session 06 - MNEs and Artificial Intelligence

**Instructor:** Harald Puhr

## Learning objective
Understand how recent developments in big data and artificial intelligence are reshaping international business and how firms respond to these challenges.

## Discussion case
- Cotte, J., & Chandrasekhar, R. (2025). *Acorai AB: Marketing 'listening to your heart with AI'*.

## Concepts to master
- AI as a change in information and decision-making capability.
- Data quality and cross-country data asymmetry.
- Algorithmic governance and augmented decision-making.
- Digital service multinationals.
- Scaling digital capabilities internationally.
- Country differences in data, regulation, infrastructure, and adoption.

## Pre-class questions
- Which internationalization frictions does AI reduce, and which does it amplify?
- What happens when decision quality depends on uneven local data?
- How should governance change when AI contributes to strategic decisions?
- Which advantages of digital firms travel across borders more easily than traditional firm advantages?
- Where must an AI-enabled business localize despite digital scalability?

## Imported course knowledge as of 8 October 2026

AI changes both international decision-making and operations. Human bounded rationality creates judgment and present-state biases; augmentation can ease information and processing constraints but introduces biased data, objectives, benefits and interaction. Governance and local data validation remain essential. Digital MNEs, born globals, direct and indirect network effects and complementors challenge assumptions about scaling while Hennart tests what existing theory already explains.
