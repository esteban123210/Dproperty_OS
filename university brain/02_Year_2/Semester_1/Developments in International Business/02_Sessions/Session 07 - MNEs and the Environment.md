# Session 07 - MNEs and the Environment

**Instructor:** Arad Ahi

## Learning objective
Assess how the extent, pace, and pattern of internationalization shape MNE performance over time, and evaluate how firm capabilities, stakeholder relationships, and externalities influence value creation.

## Discussion case
- Pangarkar, N., & Yadav, N. (2022). *Shopee: Hitting the pause button on international expansion?*

## Required readings
- Kim, H. (2020). What's the best pace of expansion? *Harvard Business Review, 98*(3), 29-29.
- Henisz, W. J., Dorobantu, S., & Nartey, L. J. (2014). Spinning gold: The financial returns to stakeholder engagement. *Strategic Management Journal, 35*(12), 1727-1748. Required sections: Introduction; The valuation of stakeholder management; Discussion.

## Supplementary readings
- Kim, H., Wu, J., Schuler, D. A., & Hoskisson, R. E. (2020). Chinese multinationals' fast internationalization: Financial performance advantage in one region, disadvantage in another. *Journal of International Business Studies, 51*(7), 1076-1106.
- Lu, J. W., & Beamish, P. W. (2004). International diversification and firm performance: The S-curve hypothesis. *Academy of Management Journal, 47*(4), 598-609.
- Lindner & Puck (2025), Chapters 3.4, 14.

## Concepts to master
- Pace of international expansion.
- Extent and pattern of internationalization.
- International diversification and performance.
- S-curve logic.
- Capability constraints and managerial bandwidth.
- Stakeholder relationships and externalities.
- Value creation versus overexpansion.

## Pre-class questions
- When does faster internationalization create advantage?
- When does pace exceed organizational capabilities?
- How does geographic diversification affect performance over time?
- What role do stakeholder relationships play in protecting or creating value?
- Which externalities can undermine an otherwise attractive expansion strategy?
