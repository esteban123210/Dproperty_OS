# Session 02 - Where to Invest

**Instructor:** Arad Ahi

## Learning objective
Evaluate how firms select foreign markets by assessing home-host country distance, location attractiveness, and ownership, location, and internalization advantages that shape internationalization success.

## Concepts to master
- Home-host country distance.
- CAGE-style distance logic: cultural, administrative, geographic, and economic differences.
- Location attractiveness.
- OLI / eclectic paradigm: ownership, location, internalization advantages.
- Localization versus standardization.
- Springboard internationalization.

## Pre-class questions
- What makes a foreign market attractive beyond market size?
- Which forms of distance are most consequential for the focal firm?
- What ownership advantages can the firm transfer successfully?
- Which advantages are location-specific?
- Does the firm need to localize its value proposition or operating model?
