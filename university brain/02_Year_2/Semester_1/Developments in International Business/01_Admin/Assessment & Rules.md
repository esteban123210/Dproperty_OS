# Assessment & Rules

## Assessment structure
| Component | Weight | Individual / Team | What is evaluated |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Mini case assignments | 20% | Team | Prioritization of business concerns and application of readings to the week's case |
| Case discussion / debate | 20% | Team | Strategic argument, rebuttal quality, Q&A, presentation and discussion contribution |
| Final examination | 60% | Individual | Comprehension, framework application, and comparison of IB strategies and perspectives |

## Non-negotiable pass conditions
- Final exam grade must be **5.5 or higher**.
- Weighted overall course average must be **5.5 or higher**.
- Attendance is mandatory.

## Mini cases
- Every team analyzes the weekly case.
- Non-presenting teams submit a **maximum one-page analysis, excluding references**.
- Use the case questions posted on Canvas.
- Put all team members' names in the header on page 1.
- The deliverable should **prioritize concerns around the actual business decision**, not summarize the case.
- Analysis should explicitly connect the case to the assigned readings.

## Case debate
From Week 2 onward:
1. Prepared argument - 10 minutes.
2. Debate round 1 - rebuttal.
3. Debate round 2 - rebuttal.
4. Q&A.
5. Non-presenting students act as the Board / Steering Committee and determine the preferred action.
6. Non-presenting students give feedback on both content and presentation form.

## Final exam
- **Open book**.
- Short-answer and/or essay questions.
- Exam source base: **slides and readings**.
- Material from in-class/debated cases is not directly tested.
- Main capabilities tested:
  - explain course concepts accurately;
  - apply frameworks to a new business challenge;
  - compare and contrast alternative IB strategies/perspectives;
  - justify a recommendation.

## Resit rules
- Only the final exam can be retaken in the resit.
- Grades from other course components remain valid.
- If the course is not passed during the current study year, the entire course must be repeated the following year, including attendance and all assignments.

## Strategic implication for study
Because 60% of the grade is an open-book exam based on slides and readings, notes should be optimized for **fast retrieval and framework application**, not for long narrative summaries. Every reading note should therefore capture:
1. Core claim.
2. Framework / mechanism.
3. Key variables.
4. Boundary conditions.
5. Managerial implication.
6. How it differs from another framework.
7. One reusable example.
