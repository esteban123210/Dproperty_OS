## Assigned question

Briefly evaluate Shopee's internationalisation performance. What evidence should management use to determine whether its international expansion is creating sustainable value, and what should Shopee prioritise over the next 3–5 years?

## Assessment preserved from the conversation

Estimated 4.25–4.5/5 (8.5–9/10), subject to the lecturer's actual rubric. This is an advisory estimate, not a grade. The draft addresses the evaluation, management evidence and priorities within one page. Strengths include a clear qualified judgment, analysis of losses relative to revenue, country-level evidence, selective expansion priorities and separation of Sea group revenue from Shopee country performance.

## Corrections needed before treating the argument as exam-ready

1. Brazil is consistent with a learning-and-transferability argument; it does not confirm a causal rule that speed caused success.
2. Lu and Beamish examine diversification extent and firm performance. New-market learning costs and footprint coordination burdens can be discussed without assigning Shopee to phases 1 and 3 from country count alone.
3. France as a fit failure and India as a legitimacy failure are interpretations, not conclusively established exclusive causes. The original case also reports competitive, logistics and uncertainty-related considerations.
4. A declining loss/revenue ratio shows improving loss intensity. It does not prove scale caused the improvement or establish country profitability.
5. The 2021 gaming profit was equivalent to approximately 90% of e-commerce losses. This numerical comparison does not demonstrate an accounting transfer funding exactly that fraction.
6. Sustainable value need not require all indicators to improve simultaneously. Require acceptable integrated economics, capital returns, capability and legitimacy, recognizing trade-offs and thresholds.
7. Clarify priorities over time: years 1–2 can consolidate country economics and capabilities; years 3–5 can scale proven clusters conditionally. Assess each Latin American neighbour separately rather than treating proximity as sufficient transferability.
8. Local data storage is a market-specific governance choice; it should follow the relevant requirements and risks, not become a blanket prescription.
9. The original case reports ten million daily Indian orders. Attribute this to the case, rather than presenting it as independently validated evidence.

The core structure and financial insight can be retained. Reduce categorical causal wording, strengthen evidence-to-mechanism links and specify conditions that would change the recommendation. This feedback is incorporated into DIB Quest's pace, S-curve, sustainable-value and Shopee transfer questions.

## Anonymized summary of the draft

The draft judges Shopee a regional competitive success while questioning whether its rapid distant expansion created sustainable financial value. It contrasts Southeast Asia and Brazil with the withdrawals from France and India. It applies expansion-speed, foreignness, stakeholder and diversification frameworks, and compares growing absolute losses with improving revenue-relative loss intensity.

The proposed management tests concern country economics, customer/seller retention, transferability of capabilities and stakeholder readiness. The draft recommends strengthening the core, developing a Brazil-centred cluster subject to evidence, staging distant entries and setting stakeholder-related entry gates. The review above qualifies its causal claims, financial interpretation and sequencing.
