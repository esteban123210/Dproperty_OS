Our position: localization is paramount for an AI-based medical device

Why we agree
• Acorai's value proposition relies entirely on an AI engine that 
analyzes physiological and sensor data (the SAVE system).
• An AI engine is only as reliable as the data, regulatory fit and 
clinical relationships around it.
• Local regulatory norms, institutional frameworks, 
demographic realities and societal expectations, decide 
whether the product works.
Position: Agree. Acorai must build a product and 
market-entry strategy tightly adapted to local 
market conditions, using Sweden's low -cost clinical 
environment as the foundation for expanding 
outward.
Our core arguments:
1
Bounded rationality and algorithmic governance
AI carries machine biases; a Swedish-trained model needs local 
calibration and human checks.
2
Institutions, liability of foreignness and data realities
FDA, MDR and GDPR compliance; local data definitions and 
infrastructure cannot be assumed.
3
Internationalization theory: the Uppsala framework
Zero commercial history abroad; experiential knowledge and Key 
Opinion Leader (KOL) networks are built step by step.
4
Financial refutation of global economies of scale
Burn would more than double against under €50,000 in cash; 
premature scaling triggers bankruptcy.
These core arguments lead to the conclusion that ACORAI should adapt locally and expand from a Swedish base

## Page 8

Conclusion: agree. Adapt locally and build from Sweden outward
Source: Cotte & Chandrasekhar (2025), Acorai AB, Ivey W42614; Anwar (2026); Moses et al. (2025); Johanson & Vahlne (1977); Zaheer (1995) Team B · October 6, 2026 8
A C O R A I
What the evidence shows
Theory
A one-size-fits-all approach risks biased outputs, 
heightened liability of foreignness and the 
absence of trusted local networks.
Finance
Premature global scaling would more than double 
monthly burn and quickly exhaust a company 
holding under €50,000 in cash.
Product
The SAVE system is only as reliable as the data, 
regulatory fit and clinical relationships that 
surround it.
What Acorai should do
1 Use Sweden's safe, low-cost clinical environment (~€100 per 
patient) to complete the 400-patient study and secure MDR 
approval.
2 Generate the foundational data and peer-reviewed evidence 
that KOLs in each next market will demand.
3 Calibrate the AI model and hardware locally, with human-in-
the-loop checks, before relying on outputs in a new market.
4 Adapt to each market's regulation, reimbursement tracks 
and clinical practices; expand outward in sequence as 
knowledge and capital grow.
Focus on a product and market-entry strategy tightly adapted to local conditions. Survive first, then expand.
