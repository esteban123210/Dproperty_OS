## 6. Board Q&A bank
Prepare concise answers to:
- What is the biggest downside of your recommendation?
- What evidence would make you reverse your decision?
- Why is the alternative not good enough?
- What is your recommendation assuming about the host market / government / partner / capabilities?
- How much control, investment, and risk does this require?
- What should management monitor after implementation?
- What happens under a negative scenario?

## 7. Red-team checklist
- [ ] We can state the opponent's argument fairly and strongly.
- [ ] We know our weakest assumption.
- [ ] Every major claim has case evidence or a course reading behind it.
- [ ] We have a response to the strongest alternative, not just the easiest one.
- [ ] We distinguish uncertainty from risk.
- [ ] Our recommendation includes implementation and triggers, not just a direction.
- [ ] We can explain the strategy without slides if necessary.
