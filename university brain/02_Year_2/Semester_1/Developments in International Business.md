## Course focus
Developments in International Business examines how multinational enterprises manage the added complexity of operating across countries. The course moves from the foundations of why MNEs exist to market selection, entry-mode choice, state relations, geopolitics, artificial intelligence, and the pace and performance of international expansion.

The recurring task throughout the course is to turn contextual differences across countries into **firm-level strategic decisions**.
