# Founder Interview Guide

The uploaded guide requests a 30-minute founder conversation and includes questions about mission, customers, proposition, pricing, clinical pathways, regulation, scaling, and future ambition. No founder answers or interview transcript were included in the material set.

## Interview priorities

If the conversation is time-limited, prioritize evidence that resolves the project's biggest uncertainties.

1. **Actual customer and payer:** Who books today? What are the age, gender, location, and payer patterns? How do these compare with the team's 25-45 expat and 35-55 affluent-segment hypotheses?
2. **Demand and capacity:** How many visits occur per week, what share of available slots are filled, and where is capacity constrained?
3. **Acquisition:** Which channels generate bookings? What share is referrals, gift cards, paid media, press, search, and the TrainMore collaboration? Is cost per booking known?
4. **Price and offer:** How was €299 chosen? Has Blue tested prices, bundles, employer payment, supplementary reimbursement, or membership? What did customers say or do?
5. **After the visit:** How do customers use the app after day one? Are reminders, follow-up, re-tests, or action-plan check-ins already offered? What repeat rate is known?
6. **Clinical pathway and trust:** What happens when a result needs follow-up? Which GPs or specialists accept referrals or results? How are clinical boundaries and claims governed?
7. **Growth and partnerships:** What limits growth now—demand, clinicians, facilities, regulation, diagnostics, or partners? What would make an employer, insurer, hospital, or research partnership viable?
8. **AI:** Where is AI used today, if anywhere? What would the founders want it to do? What data could it use, and what clinical, privacy, or human-review limits must apply?

## Suggested opening

“We are developing an independent academic marketing strategy for Blue as part of our Executive MBA course. We want to understand what the company has observed in practice, distinguish that from our outside hypotheses, and identify a few useful recommendations. We will treat information as confidential as agreed in our proposal.”

## Optional follow-ups

- What share of bookings comes through word of mouth or gift cards?
- How does the gym partnership work, and what would make it worth repeating?
- What does the app do between annual checks?
- Which customer questions or objections most often prevent a booking?
- Which recommendation is safe to automate, and which must remain with a clinician?

Avoid promising a specific AI solution before hearing how the current service and data actually work.
