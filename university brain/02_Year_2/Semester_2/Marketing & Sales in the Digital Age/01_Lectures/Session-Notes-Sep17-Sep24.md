# Sep 17 and Sep 24 Working Notes

## What the working deck currently says

The 17-slide deck frames Blue as an Amsterdam preventive-health check for people who feel healthy. The early proposition is a roughly one-hour visit for about €299, including tests, a doctor conversation, and an app-based result/action plan. It presents the journey as booking, on-site assessment, doctor conversation, app results within 24 hours, and referral advice if needed.

The working canvas covers:

1. **Purpose and mission:** “Know yourself. Live better.” Use screening and data to help identify risks earlier and support healthier lives.
2. **Customer:** initially “health-conscious expats” in Amsterdam, aged 25-45; international professionals, entrepreneurs, and highly educated.
3. **Job:** understand health before symptoms and gain early warning/reassurance.
4. **Innovation:** ten tests in about an hour, on-site doctor interpretation, same-day discussion, and app follow-up.
5. **Value:** functional insight and referral advice; emotional reassurance; social/giftability.
6. **Outreach:** social and search channels, expat platforms, company/community partnerships, and educational content.
7. **Transfer:** online booking, single-location visit, doctor conversation, app results, referral advice.
8. **Environment:** PESTEL around the clinic and its customers, competitors, partners, suppliers, and employees.
9. **Advocacy:** questions about trust, clinical credibility, referral pathways, accessibility, research partnerships, and repeat visits.
10. **Lifetime value:** possible annual checks, subscriptions, follow-up partners, and referral pathways are discussed as future directions, not demonstrated current performance.
