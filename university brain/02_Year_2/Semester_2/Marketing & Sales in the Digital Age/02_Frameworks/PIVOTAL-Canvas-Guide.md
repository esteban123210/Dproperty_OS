# PIVOTAL Infinity Marketing Canvas Guide

The uploaded team worksheet calls for one organization and one customer segment, evidence in every field, a named disconnect, and a repair that makes the AI dimension specific. It describes ten elements:

| No. | Element | Working question |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Purpose and mission | Why does the organization exist, and what does that purpose rule out? |
| 2 | Customer segment | Which exact segment is this canvas about, and who is excluded? |
| 3 | Problems and jobs | What is the segment trying to accomplish, and what does the problem cost them? |
| 4 | Innovations | How does the purpose become an answer to that job? |
| 5 | Customer value | What functional, emotional, and social benefit is created? |
| 6 | Outreach and engagement | How is the value communicated, and would a sceptic believe it? |
| 7 | Transfer and delivery | Does the value reach the stakeholder and get adopted? |
| 8 | Micro and macro environment | What regulatory, technological, competitive, social, and cultural forces shape the model? Read both directions. |
| 9 | Advocacy | Who supports the organization without being paid, and who challenges it? |
| 10 | Lifetime value | Is the organization managing an ongoing relationship or a series of transactions? |

## Useful working discipline

- Keep observed facts, company claims, third-party reporting, analysis, and hypotheses separate.
- Use one segment per canvas. If two segments are strategically different, make separate canvases or state why they can be combined.
- A disconnect should connect two or more elements and be supported by evidence.
- A recommendation should repair that specific disconnect. Name the AI capability, user benefit, metric, data required, human owner, and safeguards.
- Do not treat an AI feature as a strategy on its own.
