## Main argument

The reading distinguishes three layers:

- **Analytical AI** forecasts and recommends.
- **Generative AI** explains analysis or drafts communication.
- **Agentic AI** can monitor events and carry out a permitted next step in a workflow.

Agentic pricing could connect data, quote, discount, approval, renewal, and monitoring tasks. It does not remove the need for reliable data, sound pricing models, redesigned processes, clear governance, and human responsibility. The article recommends starting with a narrow pilot and increasing delegation only as rules, auditability, and reversibility improve.

The condensed reading reports survey expectations and an average profit sensitivity estimate. Treat these as the source's reported evidence, not as forecasts for Blue. In particular, a modeled profit effect under an unchanged-volume assumption is not a guarantee of price-test results.
