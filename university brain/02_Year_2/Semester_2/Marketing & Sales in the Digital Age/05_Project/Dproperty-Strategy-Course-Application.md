# Dproperty Digital Strategy - Course Application

The English Dproperty digital strategy can help with **method and execution structure**. It is not a case source for Blue and should not replace the PIVOTAL analysis.

## What transfers usefully

- Turn one positioning promise into clear audience paths and channel roles.
- Connect awareness, consideration, intent, and conversion rather than treating platforms as interchangeable.
- Define channel-specific measures, owners, and a staged implementation plan.
- Convert one source asset into different formats for different audiences without changing the underlying evidence.
- Make the work operational through responsibility assignments and a 90-day sequence.

## What does not transfer as a Blue fact

Dproperty's off-market access, real-estate investor segments, channel recommendations, transaction history, pricing, and KPIs are specific to a real-estate business. Do not reuse them as claims about Blue's customers, market, brand, or results.

## Application to the course project

For Blue, start from the PIVOTAL disconnect and choose channels only after the target segment is settled. The Dproperty report is a useful example of how to move from positioning to a funnel, metrics, ownership, and roadmap. Its main practical value is the strategy-to-execution structure, not the particular tactics.
