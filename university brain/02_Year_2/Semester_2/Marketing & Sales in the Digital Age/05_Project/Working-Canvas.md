## Candidate disconnect

**The offer promises ongoing prevention and personal insight, but the visible model ends after a one-off check.** The project files do not show that customers follow action plans, engage with the app between visits, or return annually.

## AI recommendation to test

Evaluate an opt-in, clinician-approved follow-up service that helps customers understand their own agreed action plan and remember next steps between visits. AI could personalize plain-language reminders or answer non-diagnostic service questions, with an easy handoff to a clinician and no autonomous diagnosis. Test whether it improves action-plan engagement and annual return, while monitoring opt-outs, complaints, and clinician workload.

This is a hypothesis to test with the founder and course readings, not a final recommendation. The PIVOTAL repair should state the customer benefit, AI task, data used, metric, human oversight, and stop condition.
