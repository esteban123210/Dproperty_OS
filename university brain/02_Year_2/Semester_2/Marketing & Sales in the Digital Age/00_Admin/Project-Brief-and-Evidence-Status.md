# Project Brief and Evidence Status

## What the project is

The September 2026 proposal describes a five-person University of Amsterdam Executive MBA team developing an independent marketing and sales strategy for a real organization. It is meant to assess the value proposition, target customers, market, positioning, adoption challenges, and sustainable growth, including how AI and digital tools could improve customer insight, engagement, and long-term value.

The selected organization in the proposal and diagnostic is **Blue Health Intelligence B.V., the Amsterdam preventive-health clinic whose consumer brand is Blue**. The proposal requests one 30-minute founder conversation with Leanne Verdonck. It says the final strategy presentation will be presented on **15 October 2026**, and that the final deck will be shared with the company as an independent academic perspective, not a paid consulting deliverable.

The PIVOTAL Infinity Marketing Canvas is named as the principal framework. The course worksheet asks teams to complete ten canvas elements for one organization and one segment, support each field with evidence, identify a disconnect, and specify a repair that makes the AI dimension concrete.

## Conflicts and missing authoritative requirements

1. **Company identity:** `The syllabus grades three things correct use of the PIVOTAL.docx` describes BHI as a U.S. company owned by Blue Cross Blue Shield plans and mentions HIPAA. That conflicts with the other project materials, which identify Blue Health Intelligence B.V. as an Amsterdam clinic branded Blue. Do not use the U.S. ownership or HIPAA statements for this project unless the instructor confirms a different case.
2. **Segment definition:** Three versions appear in the files: health-conscious Amsterdam expats aged 25-45 in the Sep 17/24 deck; professionals aged roughly 28-45 in the first-pass canvas; and affluent urban adults aged 35-55, plus entrepreneurs and expats, in the Phase 1 diagnostic. The beachhead segment is not final.
3. **Course name:** The University Brain lists “Marketing & Sales in the Digital Age”; the project proposal uses “Marketing & Sales in the Age of AI.” Confirm the official course title from the syllabus.
4. **Assessment rubric:** No official syllabus or assignment rubric was uploaded. A separate guidance note claims the Canvas, seven Kotler-Keller tasks, evidence-based claims, and a 10% presentation grade. Treat those details as unverified. The same note contains the company-identity mismatch above.
5. **Primary evidence:** The founder interview is not in the supplied material. Key commercial evidence such as customer mix, acquisition channels, retention, booking capacity, price sensitivity, and B2B pipeline remains unknown or only hypothesized.

## Evidence discipline

- Label company statements and public data in the diagnostic as **reported evidence** until the original source is checked.
- Label target segments, willingness to pay, retention, and proposed business-model changes as **hypotheses** unless supported by company data or an identified study.
- The Phase 1 PDF includes current-market, competitor, healthcare, and regulatory claims. Recheck high-impact claims against cited primary sources before including them in the final deck.
- The four AI reading files are condensed readings. Use them as study notes, then cite the original articles in the project bibliography where possible.
