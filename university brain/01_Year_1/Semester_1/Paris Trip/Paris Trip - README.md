## 📌 Core Question

---

## The Two Sides of Entrepreneurship

### Formal Ecosystem 🏛️
(What you see in meetings)

- Universities (École des Mines)
- Incubators (Hello Tomorrow)
- Startup hubs (Station F)
- Accelerators (WILCO)
- Creative spaces (Le19M, POUSH)
- Structured sessions & debriefs

**Provides:** Knowledge, talent, capital access, infrastructure, mentorship, credibility

### Informal Ecosystem 🤝
(What you feel between meetings)

- Breakfasts & lunches
- Opening drinks along the Seine
- Free time exploration
- Closing dinner
- Conversations & team debriefs
- Bar & restaurant moments

**Provides:** Trust, relationships, serendipity, networking, emotional energy, reflection

---

## ⚡ Core Insight

**Entrepreneurship is strongest when both sides connect.**

Formal support without trust → rigid and disconnected  
Social energy without structure → chaotic and ineffective  
**Innovation thrives when discipline and human connection coexist**
