## PHASE 1: TEAM ALIGNMENT (Due by July 4)

Your team must have these conversations BEFORE arriving in Paris.

### 1.1 Character Role Assignment

**TASK:** Assign each of the 5 team members to ONE character role.

**Roles available:**
1. **Narrator** 🎙️ — Voice guide, opens/closes, bridges between scenes
2. **Main Character** 🔍 — Learner perspective, on-camera presence, notebook
3. **Serious Academic Entrepreneur** 🎓 — Analyst, explains concepts, frameworks
4. **Messy Networking Entrepreneur** 🤝 — Connector, social moments, informal ecosystem
5. **Seasoned Entrepreneur** 🎯 — Observer, synthesizer, final wisdom

**Assignment method:**
- [ ] Have each person read the character descriptions in [[Team Roles & Video Characters.md]]
- [ ] Ask: "Which character feels natural to you? Where do you actually think?"
- [ ] Assign based on HONEST FIT, not who "should" play that role narratively
- [ ] **Confirm:** All 5 people say yes to their role

**Record assignments here:**

```
[ ] Messy Networker: ________________

```

## PHASE 3: TECHNICAL PREPARATION (Due by July 5)

### 3.1 Camera Settings Locked

**Before you leave, test and record these settings:**

**Video Format:**
- [ ] Resolution: 1080p (Full HD) minimum, 4K if available
- [ ] Frame rate: 24 fps (cinematic) or 30 fps
- [ ] Format: MP4 or H.264 (compatible with editing software)
- [ ] **Test:** Record 30 seconds, check file plays back smoothly ✓

**Audio Settings:**
- [ ] Audio input: External microphone (not internal mic)
- [ ] Audio levels: Test and set to -6dB (headroom for peaks)
- [ ] Mono or stereo: Stereo (for better quality)
- [ ] **Test:** Record with audio settings, listen back ✓

**Color & Exposure:**
- [ ] White balance: Auto or daylight (consistent)
- [ ] Exposure: Test in bright and dim light
- [ ] Color profile: Standard or cinema (test in post-production)
- [ ] **Goal:** Warm, summery aesthetic (adjust in editing)

**Focus & Stability:**
- [ ] Auto-focus: Enabled
- [ ] Image stabilization: ON (if available)
- [ ] Tripod: All settings locked and tested ✓

## PHASE 4: KNOWLEDGE PREPARATION (Due by July 6)

### 4.1 Concept Refresh

**All team members should understand these concepts:**
- [ ] **Deep Tech:** Long-term, science-based innovation requiring patient capital
- [ ] **Startup Hub:** A place where founders, investors, mentors, and infrastructure meet
- [ ] **Accelerator:** Structured support program for tested ideas (3–6 months)
- [ ] **Cultural Innovation:** Craft, design, and creative value creation
- [ ] **Social Capital:** Value created through relationships, trust, and networks
- [ ] **Knowledge Transfer:** How universities and research turn into entrepreneurship

**Study method:**
- [ ] Read quick definitions in [[Learning Hooks & Key Concepts.md]]
- [ ] Discuss what you already know about each concept
- [ ] Anticipate how each site will illustrate these concepts

---

### 4.2 Site Preparation

**For each site you're visiting, read the guide:**
- [ ] [[Hello Tomorrow Guide.md]] (or similar)
- [ ] Discuss: What will we see? What should we ask? What will this teach us?

**Recording opportunities at each site:**
- [ ] Who will ask a question?
- [ ] What should Serious char focus on?
- [ ] Where will Main char show wonder/confusion?
- [ ] What's worth filming (architecture, people, moments)?

---

### 4.3 Script Preparation (Optional but Recommended)

**Some dialogue can be natural, but plan key moments:**

**Do prepare:**
- [ ] Opening narration (Narrator's first line)
- [ ] Serious character's concept explanations (bullet points, not word-for-word)
- [ ] Final café scene dialogue (see [[Video Creative Direction.md]] Section 6 for exact words)
- [ ] Closing narration

**Do NOT prepare:**
- [ ] Casual conversation at meals
- [ ] Reactions to new information
- [ ] Relationship-building moments
- [ ] Messy character's spontaneous energy

**Script format:**
- [ ] Write key lines/bullet points in shared document
- [ ] Circulate to relevant characters
- [ ] Practice 1–2 times before trip (not too much—keep authenticity)
- [ ] Final script locked before departure
