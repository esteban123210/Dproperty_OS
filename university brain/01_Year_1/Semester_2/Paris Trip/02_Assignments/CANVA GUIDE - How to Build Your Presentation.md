# HOW TO BUILD YOUR PRESENTATION IN CANVA
## Step-by-Step Guide (30 minutes)

---

## PART 1: SETUP (5 minutes)

### Step 1: Go to Canva
1. Open **www.canva.com**
2. Sign in (or create free account)
3. Click **"Create a design"**
4. Search for **"Presentation"** or **"16:9"**
5. Choose blank presentation

### Step 2: Set Up Brand Colors
1. In the left sidebar, click **"Brand"**
2. Click **"Add brand colors"** 
3. Add these hex codes:
   - **#F9F7F0** (Cream white - background)
   - **#F4D03F** (Butter yellow - accent)
   - **#B4D4FF** (Powder blue - accent)
   - **#D4A5A5** (Dusty pink - warmth)
   - **#F5B041** (Soft orange - energy)
   - **#6B8E23** (Olive green - nature)

### Step 3: Set Default Fonts
1. Still in **Brand**
2. Set **Headlines font:** Georgia or serif style (elegant)
3. Set **Body font:** Open Sans or Montserrat (clean)

## PART 2: SLIDE 1 - TITLE SLIDE (3 minutes)

### Content:
```
WHERE PARIS LEARNS TO SLOW DOWN

Simlas's Slow-Living Sanctuary
A Lean Business Model Canvas

[Your names]
[University/Date]
```

### How to Build:
1. **Background:** Click background → Fill → Choose **#F9F7F0** (cream)
2. **Add centered shapes:**
   - Rectangle at top: **#D4A5A5** (dusty pink), 300px wide, thin
   - Rectangle at bottom: **#B4D4FF** (powder blue), 300px wide, thin
3. **Main title:** 
   - Type "WHERE PARIS LEARNS TO SLOW DOWN"
   - Font: Georgia, 60pt, **bold**
   - Color: Dark gray/black
   - Centered, positioned top-third of slide
4. **Subtitle:**
   - Type "Simlas's Slow-Living Sanctuary"
   - Font: Handwriting style (search Canva fonts for "Dancing" or "Brush"), 44pt
   - Color: **#D4A5A5** (dusty pink)
   - Centered, middle of slide
5. **Footer:**
   - "A Lean Business Model Canvas" + names + date
   - Font: Open Sans, 20pt, light gray
   - Centered, bottom third
6. **Add image:** Search "Paris café" or "bohemian café" → Insert faded/transparent behind text
7. **Tip:** Make text white or very dark for contrast

**Template to copy from Canva:**  
Search "Minimal Title Slide" and customize colors + fonts

## FINAL TIPS FOR CANVA

✅ **Do:**
- Save your work frequently (Canva auto-saves, but check)
- Download a PDF to backup
- Use consistent fonts (2–3 max across all slides)
- Keep color palette consistent (your 6 brand colors)
- Use plenty of whitespace
- Make text readable from distance (test on full screen)
- Add transitions: Fade only (not distracting)

❌ **Don't:**
- Mix too many fonts
- Use all caps for body text
- Add unnecessary animations
- Use overly bright colors (stick to your palette)
- Clutter slides (1 idea per slide)
- Use low-quality images
