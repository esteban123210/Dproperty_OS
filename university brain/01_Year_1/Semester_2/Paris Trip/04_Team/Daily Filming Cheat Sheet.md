## 📋 DAILY TEAM BRIEFING (5 min, each morning)

Before each day starts, assign roles and review:

**Questions to ask:**
1. Who's on camera #1 today? #2?
2. Who's recording audio?
3. **What's the priority character today?** (Everyone knows)
4. **What's the priority location?** (Gets most equipment)
5. **What's one dialogue/scene we need to capture?**
6. **Backup plan if weather/timing changes?**

**Example (Tuesday):**
- "Today is Serious char showcase at HelloTomorrow. We need clean audio of them explaining Deep Tech. Main char should be visibly processing. Messy char brings energy at lunch. Roll video."

## 📸 WRIST REMINDER

**What makes good footage:**
- ✓ Intentional framing (not random)
- ✓ Stable shots (tripod when possible)
- ✓ Clean audio (most important)
- ✓ Characters speaking clearly
- ✓ Natural moments (authenticity > perfection)
- ✓ Details (notebook, coffee, design, light)
- ✓ Multiple angles same moment (backup shots)
- ✓ Let scenes breathe (hold on emotional moments)
