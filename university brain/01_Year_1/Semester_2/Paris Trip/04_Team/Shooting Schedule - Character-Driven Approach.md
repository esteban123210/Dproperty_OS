# 🎬 Shooting Schedule: Character-Driven Approach

**Designed for:** 5-person team, 5-day Paris trip, character-driven narrative  
**Duration:** 5–7 minute video  
**Core Framework:** 5 characters with defined roles and footage responsibilities  
**Status:** Ready for team assignment and pre-trip prep

---

## PART 1: CHARACTER ROLE ASSIGNMENTS

Before the trip begins, assign each of the 5 team members to ONE primary character role.

| Character Role | Definition | Who This Might Be | Primary Responsibilities |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Narrator** 🎙️ | Voice & structure | Someone articulate, calm, observant | Voice-overs, opening/closing, bridges between scenes |
| **Main Character** 🔍 | Learner & perspective | Someone creative, on-camera comfortable, curious | On-camera presence throughout, notebook scenes, evolution |
| **Serious Academic** 🎓 | Analyst & framework | Someone analytical, systematic | Explaining concepts, analyzing spaces, direct-to-camera |
| **Messy Networker** 🤝 | Connector & relations | Someone social, energetic, people-focused | Social moments, bar/dinner scenes, informal footage |
| **Seasoned Entrepreneur** 🎯 | Synthesizer & wisdom | Someone observant, experienced, balanced | Background observation during visits, final café scene |

**CRITICAL:** Assign roles based on genuine fit, not narrative ideal. If no one fits a role naturally, adapt the framework.

| Element | Who Films? | Who Appears? | Details |

**Contingency:** This is NON-NEGOTIABLE. If you miss this moment Saturday morning, you MUST reschedule it before departure (could be Friday evening at café, could be Saturday afternoon, could be virtually). The video cannot end without this scene.

### Filming Team Structure

**Assign roles BEFORE the trip:**

| Role | Responsibility | Ideal Person |
|------|---|---|
| **Primary Camera Operator** | Main footage, formal visits, primary angles | Someone steady, organized, attentive |
| **Secondary Camera Operator** | Backup angles, detail shots, character close-ups | Someone creative, detail-oriented |
| **Sound Recorder** | Audio capture, microphone management, voice-overs | Someone careful, patient, audio-savvy |
| **Continuity/Props Manager** | Notebook, styling, ensuring consistency | Someone detail-oriented |
| **Edit Lead (plan post-trip)** | Organizing footage, assembling rough cut | Someone logical, tech-comfortable |

**Note:** Some people can have dual roles, but DO NOT let a character be their own primary camera operator (they can't act and film simultaneously). Rotate responsibilities.

## PART 4: CONTINGENCY PLAN

**If you miss a critical character moment:**

| Missed Moment | Why It Matters | Backup Plan |
|---|---|---|
| **Serious character hasn't explained concepts clearly** | 30% of rubric is Learning Integration | Record voice-over explanation Saturday evening (before departure). Script it, record it clean, include in final edit |
| **Main character doesn't appear much** | They're the narrative spine | Dedicate Saturday morning to extra café/reflection scenes before final café scene |
| **Messy character has no good footage** | They represent informal ecosystem | Extended filming Friday free time and dinner; if still missing, film a bar/café scene Saturday afternoon |
| **Seasoned entrepreneur only in final scene** | Risk: feels unused | They can appear quietly in background at 2–3 earlier visits, brief observation comments (voice-over), then deliver final scene |
| **Final café scene falls through** | This is your climax | PRIORITY: reschedule immediately. Friday evening café, Saturday afternoon, Sunday by video call—whatever it takes |
| **No good dialogue footage between characters** | Narrative relies on conversation | Record dialogue voice-overs and edit carefully in post; can work but less authentic |
