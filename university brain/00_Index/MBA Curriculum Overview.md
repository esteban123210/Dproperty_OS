# 🎓 MBA Curriculum Overview

## Program Structure

**Duration:** 2 years  
**Total Courses:** 18 + 1 Capstone  
**Format:** Semester-based (2 semesters per year)  

---

## Year 1: Foundation & Strategy

Focus areas:
- Strategic thinking & corporate strategy
- Performance management & change
- Financial fundamentals
- Digital transformation
- Leadership development

**Courses:** 9  
**Semesters:** 2  

See: [[Year 1 Curriculum]]

---

## Year 2: Advanced & Specialization

Focus areas:
- Ethics, sustainability & responsibility
- International business & negotiations
- Valuation & financial analytics
- Digital marketing & sales
- Capstone project (in-company thesis)

**Courses:** 9 + Capstone  
**Semesters:** 2 + Capstone period  

See: [[Year 2 Curriculum]]

---

## Curriculum at a Glance

### Year 1
| Semester | Courses |
|----------|---------|
| **Semester 1** | Strategic Performance Management, Competitive & Corporate Strategy, Change Management, Electives |
| **Semester 2** | Corporate Strategy, Corporate Finance, Purpose-Driven Strategy, Digital Business, Amsterdam Leadership Programme |

### Year 2
| Semester | Courses |
|----------|---------|
| **Semester 1** | Ethics, Responsibility and Sustainability, Business Negotiations, Developments in International Business, Electives |
| **Semester 2** | Valuation, The Executive Process, Marketing & Sales in the Digital Age, Strategic Analytics, Actualysis |
| **Capstone** | MBA In-Company Thesis Project |

---

## Key Themes Across Program

- **Strategy:** Strategic Performance Management → Corporate Strategy → Strategic Analytics
- **Finance:** Corporate Finance → Valuation → Strategic Analytics
- **Leadership:** Amsterdam Leadership Programme → The Executive Process → Ethics
- **Digital:** Digital Business → Marketing & Sales in the Digital Age
- **International:** Competitive & Corporate Strategy → Developments in International Business
