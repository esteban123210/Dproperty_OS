# B_RealEstate Investor Two-Pager Pack

**Version:** 1.0  
**As of:** 23 September 2026  
**Purpose:** Simple, comparable explanations of the seven commercial offers for investors, partners, and management.

## How to read this pack

Each two-pager answers the same questions: what the offer is, who pays, why they buy, how it makes money, what the current model says, who else solves the problem, what could make the business defensible, what can go wrong, and what must be proven next.

Financial figures are current management hypotheses from the canonical finance records. They are not audited results, customer commitments, or promised returns. Restricted brand funds are not operating revenue. Free software periods create no software revenue. Marketplace or success fees are not recognized until the legal trigger, attribution, partner shares, reversals, and collection rules are contracted.

## Portfolio logic

The shared asset is the governed transaction spine. BlankCRM creates and qualifies demand; Building Blocks makes users competent; Dproperty and white-label partners provide controlled customer cohorts; developers provide structured supply; VAULTED controls access and attribution; BluePrint governs execution from qualified opportunity to close, commission, and report.

This is not yet a proven network effect. It becomes one only when an additional participant improves measurable value for other participants without creating proportional service cost.

## Recommended investor hierarchy

1. **Underwrite BluePrint first.** It must sell and retain without requiring a franchise.
2. **Treat Dproperty and white-label as distribution and cash-flow channels.** Do not value entry fees as recurring software revenue.
3. **Treat BlankCRM and Building Blocks as activation/retention products.** Their strategic contribution may exceed their standalone revenue.
4. **Validate the developer program with one paid design partner.** Call it a franchise only after the model is standardized, transferable, and legally reviewed.
5. **Value VAULTED as option value until attributable, contribution-positive closes repeat.**
