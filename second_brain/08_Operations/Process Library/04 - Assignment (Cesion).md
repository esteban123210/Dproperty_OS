# Process 4 — Assignment (Cesión)

**Objective.** Manage the assignment of an investor's contracted property to a final buyer, maximizing the original investor's return.

**Roles used:** SA, SA·Originating Advisor, SD, OC, PH, BI. (See [[00 - Process Library Index|Role Map]].)

> ⚠️ **Critical rule (dual validation).** When the SA selling to the final buyer is **different** from the Originating Advisor who sourced the investor, the deal requires dual validation. In any conflict between advisors, **SD decides.** This safeguards the original investor relationship and prevents internal client-ownership disputes. `[APPROVED — structural rule in source]`

## Flow

| Step | Action | Role | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Initial meeting | SA | Profile final buyer: budget, zone, buildings, financing |
| 2 | Agenda & visits | SA | Select and show assignable properties |
| 3 | Internal validation | SD | If SA ≠ Originating Advisor: validate with Originating Advisor |
| 4 | Prepare offer | SA | Structure offer maximizing investor's return |
| 5 | Dual approval | SD | Approve strategy; on advisor conflict, SD decides |
| 6 | Present to assignor | SA | Send offer to the investor/assignor |
| 7 | Negotiate | SA + SD | SD ensures investor return is maximized |
| 8 | Reservation | OC | Deal sheet + agreement + documents + payment |
| 9 | Assignment contract | OC | Draft assignment-specific contract |
| 10 | Final approval | SD | Validate assignment contract before signing |
| 11 | Sign + collect | OC | Coordinate tripartite signing; collect commissions |
| 12 | Closing & handover | SA + PH | Legal process + physical handover + inventory |
| 13 | Return report | BI | Compare real vs projected ROI for the investor |
