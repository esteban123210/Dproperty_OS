## 4. Final Content (self-contained) — slide by slide

**Recurring one-liners:** North star — "We are building the hub where real estate's future is made." · Accelerator — "An accelerator for the next generation of real estate leaders — wrapped in a franchise for safety." · Thesis — "We franchise the system, not the brand — and franchising funds the ecosystem."

**PART 1 — THE ECOSYSTEM VISION**
- **S1 · Title + Hook:** "Dproperty OS — Building the hub where real estate's future is made." Sub: "10 years to build it. 5 years to fund it. Franchising is how we start." Footer: 10+ yrs · $200M+ transacted · 700+ operations *(pending validation)*.
- **S2 · The 10-Year Vision:** one place where real estate talent, capital, and ideas converge — architects, designers, analysts, developers, agencies, investors, next-gen agents, walking distance; live activity (hackathons, training, deal origination). "The soul of 19M. The model of Station F. For real estate."
- **S3 · The Problem the Ecosystem Solves:** talent fragmented and gated by network; designers can't reach developers; developers rebuild teams; talent trapped by geography; capital & projects can't find each other → slow innovation, costly collaboration, brain-drain. Concrete example: the 28-year-old Medellín architect (say out loud).
- **S4 · Why Franchising Is Phase 1:** we can't build the hub without proof + non-burnable capital; franchising gathers outside capital (fees) + hard validation; proves system/brand/cash; every franchisee = a founding member. **Diagram:** fees + royalties + Select → HQ EBITDA (Yr 3+) → reinvested into hub/talent/equity → network deepens → franchises more valuable → more franchises.

**PART 2 — THE FRANCHISE SYSTEM**
- **S5 · The Problem (why someone buys):** newcomers (no system/network/credibility); experienced agents (capped, solo, doing admin); all agents (no curated inventory, no mentorship, vision capped by resources). "The industry hands talented people a desk — not a launchpad."
- **S6 · The Solution (an accelerator, not a dead franchise):** come for network/software/CRM/know-how/training/curated deals; the difference is we operate like an accelerator — we potentiate & tailor each operator. "Franchise wrapper = safety; mission = potentiate & escalate visions."
- **S7 · Proof of Concept (micro-ecosystem):** start focused on boutique agents; each franchise is a node; franchises + HQ + curated deal flow + community = a distributed micro-ecosystem; validates curation/network effects/shared deal flow; stands alone as recurring-revenue business. *(Intellectual core.)*
- **S8 · What We Refuse to Lose:** boutique positioning; not the next RE/MAX; "Somos más amigos de la matemática que de la arquitectura"; premium people & standards protected as we scale.
- **S9 · The Thesis:** HQ keeps Dproperty Select, developer relationships, projection standards, direction; franchisees get the full system (OS, manuals, Academy, CRM, templates, methodology, community); we supervise one director per market, not every deal; Dproperty = the infrastructure layer behind boutique real estate.
- **S10 · Why Now:** ops documented (15 areas) — extractable now; European expansion in motion; AI + low-code make productizing cheap; LATAM boutique market under-systematized; competitive urgency (RE/MAX, E&V, Colliers moving).
- **S11 · The Product (system + community):** software (HQ dashboard, franchise workspace, deal pipeline, doc generator, projection generator, commission tracker, reporting); manuals; Training Academy; white-labeled CRM; Dproperty Select; community layer. "Data first, documents second."
- **S12 · Three Revenue Lines, One Rule:** branded franchise (investment-funnel markets) · white-label OS (local/transactional) · Developer Sales OS (priced on gross sales value). Rule: "the brand goes where investors gather; the systems go everywhere."
- **S13 · Dproperty Select — HQ-controlled upside:** curated investor-grade inventory; franchisee earns fixed % of sale price (2.5% branded / 1.5% white-label, external-partner-broker terms); upside above 5% stays with HQ; no unapproved projections/direct developer negotiation/edited materials; white-label gets access too, at the external-partner rate, not the branded rate.
- **S14 · Unit Economics (3 sources per franchise):**
  - **① Local sales waterfall (per $300k unit):** 5% = $15,000 → less 50/50 external = **$7,500 into company** (royalty base) → seller 35% / director 10% / franchise net 55% ($4,125). **HQ take ≈ $562/unit** (7.5% on $7,500).
  - **② Dproperty Select (per $300k):** franchisee fixed 2.5% = $7,500; **HQ retains ≈ $7,500** (white-label: partner 1.5% = $4,500, HQ ≈ $10,500). Margin engine.
  - **③ Franchise fees:** launch $30k→$40k; OS $1,000/mo; 6% + 1.5% on collected GCI; $500/mo floor (creditable, from month 7); CRM resale margin. Honesty: local royalty is thin by design; scalable money = recurring fees + Select + developer.
- **S15 · Conservative 5-Year Projection:** targets 5 branded / 20 white-label / 15 developer; revenue Y1 ~$87k → Y3 ~$660k → **Y5 ~$1.59M**; EBITDA negative Y1–2 → +$15k Y3 → **+$630k Y5**. Explicitly conservative. *(Model v0.7 — OPEX/staffing pending owner validation.)*
- **S16 · What It Costs — $650k / 24 months:** Tranche 1 $350k (MVP, legal, manuals, brand, 2 pilots); Tranche 2 $300k at milestones (MVP live, 2 white-label pilots, 1 developer agreement, package ready). "You fund milestones, not promises."
- **S17 · The JV — what each brings:** Luz Adriana (investor: capital + Dproperty brand + agency know-how + network + core system); Simón (investor: capital + TheVelopers brand + developer insight + network + projects: Dovle Selva, Dovle Cincuentenario, potentially Nayamara, Cavarrosa, the Victory's); owners participate as investors, no operating workload; Esteban + Miguel operators (full-time build); capital = $650k.
- **S18 · Team & Equity:** Simón 35% (investor/governance) · Luz Adriana 35% (investor/brand+op) · Miguel 15% (operator, vesting) · Esteban 15% (operator, vesting). Owners 70% / operators 30%; vest 4 yrs, 1-yr cliff. Miguel $0 for now; Esteban USD salary (restate former EUR), steps up at Tranche 2. Operator earn-in + investor protection under exploration. "The asymmetry is the argument."
- **S19 · Why Franchising Matters (bridge):** franchising validates the model, builds the brand, generates the cash; by Year 5 → 5+20+15, ~$630k EBITDA to redeploy, LATAM leaders ready to gather, brand = "the standard." "That is the launchpad for the hub — starting in Panama."
- **S20 · The Decision (3 yes/no):** (1) build as NewCo/JV with defined roles & equity? (2) fund Tranche 1 ($350k)? (3) commit to the 5-year vision? Close: "You keep doing what only you can do. The system does the rest, everywhere else. And in 5 years, we have the capital and the network to build the place where real estate's future actually happens — starting in Panama."
