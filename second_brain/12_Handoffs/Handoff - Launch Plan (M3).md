## 4. Final Content (self-contained)

### Goal
First franchisee: signed agreement → **$10k+ commission revenue by Week 12**; first deal ~Week 8; ≥3 in pipeline by Week 12. HQ: weekly calls + coaching.

### 30-60-90 (high level)
- **Days 1–30 (Setup):** legal signed; workspace + CRM/OS live; brand assets; email/domain; training started; Dproperty Select training; first 50 contacts; broker + developer lists; announcement drafted; scripts practiced; weekly HQ call scheduled.
- **Days 31–60 (Activation):** first client meetings; broker outreach; project presentations; first projections (approved assumptions); first lead campaign; weekly pipeline review; Select opportunities via HQ; local market mapping; referral campaign; CRM hygiene.
- **Days 61–90 (Commercial discipline):** pipeline audit; first reservation targets; broker partnerships active; strategy refined; marketing reviewed; compliance review; HQ quarterly review; first case study; next 90-day plan approved.
- **90-day success:** CRM active; 100 contacts approached/segmented; weekly rhythm; ≥1 strong opportunity; brand & compliance understood; can explain local vs. Dproperty Select economics.

### 12-week week-by-week (condensed from Launch Playbook v0.2)
- **Pre-launch (Week 1 setup):** legal/entity/licence/insurance `[local]`; CRM+OS branded & tested; brand materials; Academy first 3 modules; compliance cert assigned; recurring OS fee `[local]`; commission-tracking template (three-flow firewall).
- **W1 Kickoff:** kickoff call; CRM training; 100-contact list; send first 20 outreach. *Success: CRM live, 20+ sent, 5+ replies.*
- **W2 Pipeline + training:** Dproperty Select training; Sales Playbook review; expand to 100+; schedule 5+ calls; Academy Brand+OS. *Success: 100+ messages, 15–20 signals, 5+ calls.*
- **W3 First calls:** 8–10 discovery calls; **compliance certification**; prepare first presentation (dry-run w/ HQ); CRM hygiene. *Success: 3–5 warm, certified.*
- **W4 First presentation:** deliver; 5–8 more calls; if ready, guide reservation (**HQ reviews terms first**). *Success: first deal in flight, 15+ calls.*
- **W5 First close:** manage deal to close → **first commission**; keep prospecting; document deal. *Success: first deal closed.*
- **W6–8 Scaling:** 2–3 more deals; weekly rhythm; optional first hire; Academy certs. *Success by W8: 3+ closed, $10k+ revenue.*
- **W9–12 Refinement:** target 4–6 closed / 8–10 pipeline; team scaling; marketing launch; broker network; finish certs; Year-2 plan. *Success by W12: 4–6 closed, plan locked.*

### HQ support
Weekly 1-hr call (pipeline · deal coaching · prospecting feedback · blockers); ad-hoc (deal-closing, CRM, legal/compliance ≤48h); quarterly business review (2h).

### Economics (USD, base case with co-broke)
Per $300k unit: 5% = $15,000 → less 50/50 = **$7,500 GCI** → **HQ royalty ~$562**; franchise retains **~$6,938** (before internal split). No-co-broke: retains ~$13,875. Solo operating costs ~$1,900–2,000/mo. Break-even ~Month 3; profitable Month 4+. *(Validate vs Financial Model v0.7.)*

### Risk triggers
No pipeline by W4 → review calls, adjust messaging, HQ warm leads, 2×/wk coaching (decision point W6). First deal >6 weeks → HQ joins prospect calls, offer a Select deal (decision point W8). Wants to modify/skip the system → system is contractual; test-as-designed 4 weeks; iterate only with HQ approval (culture-fit flag).
