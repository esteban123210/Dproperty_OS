## 4. Final Content (self-contained) — slide by slide
**One-liners:** Mission — "We connect those with the means to build with those with the vision to lead — so the future of real estate gets built better." · "The soul of 19M. The model of Station F. For real estate." · Bridge — "Franchising proves the model and pays for the dream." · Close — "This is bigger than any of us. That's the point."

**PART 1 — THE VISION**
- **S1 · Title / The Dream:** "Dproperty Ecosystem — The hub where real estate's future is made." Sub: "One place. The whole value chain. Walking distance."
- **S2 · The World We Want to Build (Mission):** talent shouldn't be gated by resources/relationships; a great idea finds its architect, capital, builder, market — fast. Mission line.
- **S3 · The Problem at Scale:** value chain fragmented, siloed, gated by network; talent/capital/ideas/execution in different rooms/cities; cost = slow innovation, brain-drain, worse buildings.
- **S4 · Proof It Works — 19M + Station F:** 19M (Chanel métiers d'art → craft renaissance + talent magnet); Station F (1,000+ startups/investors → deal gravity). Insight: concentrate the best → accelerate the whole field. "No one has done this for real estate. We will."

**PART 2 — THE ECOSYSTEM**
- **S5 · What It Is:** a curated physical hub + digital network for the whole value chain; not co-working — a talent/capital/idea marketplace with the soul of a craft house + engine of an accelerator.
- **S6 · Who Gathers Here:** emerging talent (architects, designers, analysts, PropTech); builders (developers); commercializers (boutique agencies + Dproperty network); capital (investors); connective tissue = Dproperty HQ curating.
- **S7 · What Happens Under One Roof:** desks & studios; hackathons; live Academy; networking/showcases; live deal origination.
- **S8 · The Member Journey (a project is born):** the 4-week architect story (analyst → developer showcase → franchise commercializes → investor funds). Emotional peak.
- **S9 · The Flywheel:** more talent → more ideas → more capital → more projects → more developers/agencies → more opportunity → more talent. Network effect = deepest moat. **Loop diagram.**

**PART 3 — THE BUSINESS**
- **S10 · How It Makes Money:** space & membership; events; matchmaking fees; equity stakes; franchise expansion (showroom); data. *(Footer: Phase-2 P&L in development.)*
- **S11 · How Franchising Pays for the Dream (bridge):** fees + royalties + Select → HQ EBITDA (Yr 3+) → seeds hub + talent + equity fund → network → more franchises → more EBITDA. "We don't bet the company on the hub." Deck 1 = engine; Deck 2 = where its output goes.
- **S12 · The Phased Build (table):** Phase 1 Franchise (Yr 0–5, owners' $650k) · Phase 2 Digital community (Yr 2–5, franchise revenue) · Phase 3 First physical hub — **Panama City** (Yr 5–8, EBITDA + external capital + potential government) · Phase 4 Network of hubs (Yr 8–15, hub cashflow + equity gains).
- **S13 · What We Capture Along the Way:** equity upside; data moat; brand gravity; optionality ("byproducts might be worth more than the core").

**PART 4 — FUTURE + ASK**
- **S14 · The Moat at Ecosystem Scale:** network effects; curated trust; data compounding; first-mover brand; honest note (incumbents have capital, not our community/curation/LATAM trust).
- **S15 · Where This Goes:** a network of Dproperty hubs across LATAM & Iberia; the default gathering place; a category-defining institution; bigger than any agency/franchise/founder.
- **S16 · The Invitation / Ask (audience-specific):** owners (approve Phase 1 knowing where it leads); major investors (co-fund first hub in Panama City — capex/terms TBD); government (national economic-development + talent-retention partnership); anchor talent (be a founding member). Offer: early position in a category before it exists.
- **S17 · Close — Bigger Than Us:** started as a boutique agency believing real estate should be built with trust + vision; franchising scales the belief; the ecosystem changes the field. "We want to build the place where the future of real estate happens — and we want it to outlast every one of us."
