## 4. Final Content (self-contained)
**Cover:** *Welcome to Dproperty* · Franchise Onboarding · Confidential.

1. **Welcome to Dproperty** — into a boutique real estate system built on trust, curation, discipline, and investor-aware guidance.
2. **What Dproperty is** — a boutique real estate agency and operating system for curated real estate sales: brand, methodology, project access, software, training, documents, HQ support.
3. **What makes Dproperty different** — not mass-market, not a generic CRM, not an aggressive sales machine. Curated, premium, systematized guidance for clients and investors.
4. **What you are joining** — a franchise system that gives you structure, not just a logo: a way of operating, selling, reporting, training, and presenting opportunities.
5. **What Dproperty gives you** — brand · Dproperty OS · CRM setup · Sales Playbook · Operations Manual · Training Academy · templates · project presentation structure · projection logic · Dproperty Select access (under rules) · HQ support · reporting rhythm.
6. **What we expect from you** — protect the brand; use approved materials; keep CRM/OS updated; follow client-registration rules; respect Dproperty Select rules; report pipeline & deals; participate in training; represent Dproperty with professionalism and discretion.
7. **How the franchise model works (pricing callout):** launch fee **$30,000 founding / $40,000 after 5 successful franchises** · OS fee **$1,000/month** · royalty **6% + Network & Brand Fund 1.5% on collected local GCI** · minimum royalty floor **$500/month, creditable, from month 7** · local commission per your market `[local]` · Dproperty Select payout **2.5% of sale price** to the franchisee (HQ retains the balance; upside above 5% stays with HQ) · reporting obligations.
8. **How Dproperty OS works** — your operating platform for clients, brokers, projects, deals, documents, projections, training, reporting.
9. **How Dproperty Select works** — HQ-controlled curated inventory; present under rules; earn **2.5% of sale price**; no direct developer negotiation, no unapproved projections, no edited materials.
10. **First 30 days** — setup, training, brand activation, CRM/OS onboarding, first network list, first contacts, first broker list.
11. **First 60 days** — meetings, outreach, project presentations, lead campaigns, broker relationships, first opportunities.
12. **First 90 days** — pipeline discipline, reporting, first reservations, HQ review, compliance review, next quarterly plan.
13. **Support team** — HQ contacts, support channels, review rhythm, escalation rules `[MOCK]`.
14. **Launch checklist** — from legal-signed to first meetings booked (see M3 Launch Plan).
15. **Final message from HQ** — you are not buying a logo; you are joining a disciplined boutique operating system.
