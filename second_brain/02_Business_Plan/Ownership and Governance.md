# Ownership and Governance

## Proposed NewCo Ownership

- Dproperty Owner 1: 40%.
- Dproperty Owner 2: 40%.
- Esteban: 15%.
- Option pool/key hires: 5%.

## Alternative

- Esteban: 10% vesting equity.
- Additional 5% milestone-based equity.

## Governance Questions

- Who has final decision rights?
- What requires unanimous approval?
- What is Esteban's operational authority?
- What happens if owners stop funding?
- What happens if Esteban leaves?
- What happens on sale/change of control?

## Suggested Protections

- 4-year vesting with 1-year cliff.
- Acceleration if terminated without cause.
- Acceleration on sale/change of control.
- Clear buyback formula.
- Tag-along/drag-along rights.
- Written dividend policy.
- IP ownership clarity.
