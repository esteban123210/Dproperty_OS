# Ownership & Investor Protection — Proposal One-Pager

**Purpose:** a structure that (1) **protects the investors' capital first** and (2) lets the **operators grow their equity as they create value.** For discussion with the owners and finalization with the lawyer. *All figures illustrative and non-binding until legal sign-off.*

## Guiding principle
> Investors are protected on the **downside** (capital returned before any equity split). Operators are rewarded on the **upside** (earn additional equity only as real milestones are hit). Nobody's stake moves until value is provably created.

## 1. Founding cap table (unchanged)
| Party | Role | Equity |
|---|---|---:|
| Simón | Investor — capital + TheVelopers brand + projects + network | 35% |
| Luz Adriana | Investor — capital + Dproperty brand + operation + network | 35% |
| Miguel | Operator — communications & creative (vesting) | 15% |

**Owners 70% / operators 30%.** Common shares. Operators vest 4 years, 1-year cliff.

## 2. Investor protection — how the $650k goes in
- Contribute the **$650k venture budget as a convertible instrument / shareholder loan**, released in the agreed tranches (T1 $350k, T2 $300k at milestones).
- Attach a **1× non-participating liquidation preference**: on a sale or wind-down, investors receive the **greater of** (a) their contributed capital back, or (b) their equity-percentage share of proceeds — **not both.**
- Result: if the venture underperforms, investors rank ahead of equity and recover capital first. If it succeeds, they ride the equity upside. **This protects them without needing more than 70% ownership.**
- *(Alternative to discuss: straight preferred equity with the same 1× preference. Same economics, different mechanics — lawyer to advise which is cleaner in the chosen jurisdiction.)*

## 3. Operator growth — milestone earn-in (performance ratchet)
Create a reserved **earn-in pool (illustrative: up to +10% combined, e.g. +5% Esteban / +5% Miguel)** that vests to the operators **only** as milestones are achieved, diluting owners pro-rata **after** their capital is secured by the preference above.

| Gate | Illustrative trigger | Illustrative earn-in |
|---|---|---:|
| A | MVP live **and** first franchise signed | +2% each (+4% total) |
| B | Cumulative EBITDA ≥ [threshold, e.g. $250k] | +1.5% each (+3% total) |
| C | Year-5 targets met (5 franchises / 20 WL / 15 dev, or $X revenue) | +1.5% each (+3% total) |

- Fully earned, operators move from 30% → ~40% combined; owners from 70% → ~60%.
- Unearned portions expire. Numbers, gates, and cap are **owner-set** — placeholders only.
- *(Mechanically this can be founder warrants, growth shares, or a milestone option pool — lawyer to pick the cleanest instrument.)*

## 4. Leaver & protection provisions
- **Good leaver:** keeps vested + earned equity.
- **Bad leaver / for cause:** company buy-back of unvested (and optionally earned-but-unpaid) equity at nominal value.
- **Founder cliff:** nothing vests before month 12.
- **Anti-dilution / pro-rata rights** for investors on future rounds (if any).

## 5. Governance (Phase 1)
- **Board:** two owners + (optionally) one operating co-founder.
- **Reserved matters (owner consent):** budget, debt, sale of company, new equity issuance, major partnerships, IP licensing, changes to founder equity.
- Preference and reserved matters give investors control during the build; the earn-in gives operators a reason to over-deliver.

## 6. Why this is the right balance
- **For the owners:** capped downside (capital back first), majority control, no vesting risk, and operators incentivized to grow the pie — not just hold a slice.
- **For the operators:** a real, mechanical path to grow from 15% toward more as they build the value — the upside that justifies full-time, below-market commitment (Miguel $0 salary; Esteban stepped salary).
