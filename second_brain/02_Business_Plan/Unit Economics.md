# Unit Economics

## Base Sale

- Unit price: $300,000.
- Commission: 5%.
- Gross commission: $15,000.

## Local Franchise Inventory

Franchise receives the local gross commission and pays:

- 6% royalty on GCI.
- 1.5% network/brand fee.
- Monthly OS/platform fee.

HQ revenue per $15,000 local commission:

- Royalty: $900.
- Network fee: $225.
- Total before OS monthly fee allocation: $1,125.

## Private Collection Sale

Gross commission: $15,000.

- HQ direct split: $7,500.
- Franchise split: $7,500.
- Royalty on franchise half at reduced 3%: $225.
- Network fee on franchise half at reduced 1%: $75.
- HQ total: $7,800.
- Franchise before local costs: $7,200.

## Interpretation

Local sales create stronger franchise economics. Private Collection creates stronger HQ economics and should be used to add premium upside, not as the only business of a franchise.
