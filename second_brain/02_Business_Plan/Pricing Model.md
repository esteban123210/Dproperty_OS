# Pricing Model

## Branded Franchise

- Launch fee: $15,000.
- OS/platform fee: $1,000/month.
- Royalty on local gross commission income: 6%.
- Brand/network marketing fee: 1.5%.
- Private Collection gross commission split: 50/50 HQ/franchise.
- Reduced royalty/network fee on franchise half of Private Collection.

## White-label

Starter:
- $10,000 setup.
- $1,500/month.

Growth:
- $20,000 setup.
- $2,500/month.

## Developer Sales OS

- 0.5% of gross sales value on developer-team sales.
- 2.5%–3% on Dproperty/network-sourced sales.
- $3,000–$5,000/month managed desk minimum.
- Exclusive sales partner model: negotiated project-level commission.

## GoHighLevel

GoHighLevel should be treated as pass-through cost or implementation value-add, not as the core revenue driver.
