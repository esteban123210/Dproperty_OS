# Pricing Model

> **Revision 2026-07-04:** Launch fee raised from $15k to a staged $30k/$40k;
> royalty confirmed on **collected** GCI; "brand/network fee" renamed **Network & Brand
> Fund**; Dproperty Select (formerly Private Collection) moved from a 50/50 gross split
> to a **fixed % of sale price**. Financial projections must be recalculated — see
> Validation Register in the Business Plan.

## Branded Franchise

- **Launch fee (founding): $30,000.**
- **Launch fee after 5 successful franchises: $40,000.** ("Successful" to be defined — see Open Questions.)
- OS/platform fee: $1,000/month.
- Royalty 6% + **Network & Brand Fund** 1.5% = **7.5%, charged on gross-into-company** (the commission remaining after the external-advisor co-broke split), collected basis.
- **Commission waterfall:** 5% charged → 50/50 external advisors → **2.5% into company** (royalty base) → 35% seller / 10% sales director / 55% franchise net. Royalty is on the 2.5% into-company, not on franchise net (un-gameable). HQ nets ~$562 per $300k local unit.
- Minimum royalty floor: $750/month [VALIDATE — placeholder] from month 7, creditable against the percentage royalty.
- GoHighLevel: resold sub-account with margin (not at-cost).
- **Dproperty Select payout to franchisee: 2.5% of sale price** (replaces 50/50 split).

### What the launch fee covers
Initial brand-use right; franchise onboarding; Dproperty OS setup; CRM/GoHighLevel setup; sales pipeline configuration; operating manuals; training/academy access; welcome kit; initial brand assets; sales + presentation templates; initial reporting structure; Dproperty methodology training; Dproperty Select training; first 90-day plan; HQ launch support. The fee signals entry into a boutique operating system, not a cheap licence.

### What the royalty covers
Continuous brand-use right; ongoing OS access; methodology/process/playbooks; HQ operational support; pipeline review; quality control; Dproperty knowledge ecosystem; manual/process updates; reporting structure; brand & operating compliance supervision; use of accumulated Dproperty know-how. The royalty pays for the *living system*, not just the name.

### What the Network & Brand Fund covers
Brand material development; shared network campaigns; regional content; commercial template updates; Dproperty Select presentation material; network visibility; franchise launch support; visual-standard maintenance; institutional material production; building Dproperty's reputation as a boutique network. Named "Network & Brand Fund" (not "marketing fee") because it builds network, brand, reputation and shared materials — not only advertising.

## White-label

- Starter: $10,000 setup + $1,500/month.
- Growth: $20,000 setup + $2,500/month.
- Enterprise: $50,000+ setup + $5,000+/month.
- Monthly OS fee positioned **higher than branded**, because white-label builds no Dproperty brand value.
- Plus GoHighLevel sub-account resale margin per client.
- **No automatic Dproperty Select access.** If a white-label partner participates in Dproperty Select: **2.0% of sale price.**

## Developer Sales OS

- 0.5% of gross sales value on developer-team sales.
- 2.5%–3% on Dproperty/network-sourced sales.
- $3,000–$5,000/month managed desk minimum.
- Exclusive sales partner model: negotiated project-level commission.

## Dproperty Select (formerly Private Collection)

- **Projection assumption: always model at 5% total commission.** If HQ negotiates 6%–8% on a select project, that upside is retained by HQ, not shared — so the model is never inflated by optimistic commission assumptions.
- Payout to **branded franchise: 2.5% of sale price.**
- Payout to **white-label partner: 2.0% of sale price.**
- HQ retains control of developer relationships, materials, pricing, projections and negotiation.

## GoHighLevel

Resold as a margin line via white-label sub-accounts (confirmed over HubSpot, 2026-07-02), no longer treated as pure pass-through cost.

## Benchmark basis
- **Keller Williams:** est. initial investment ~$183k–$337k, ~$150k minimum cash to open a Market Center; FDD references ~$35k initial fee, 6% royalty on gross commission income. [VALIDATE — figures to confirm]
