# Esteban Compensation Package

## Baseline Need

Esteban needs at least €5,000/month to commit seriously while living in Germany.

## Suggested Role

- Co-founder & Venture Lead.
- Product & Strategy Lead.
- Possible Managing Director of Dproperty OS.

## Compensation Components

1. Salary: €5,000/month starting point.
2. Equity: 10%–15% in NewCo.
3. Bonus: performance-based.
4. Salary step-ups: tied to revenue/EBITDA.
5. Protections: vesting, acceleration, buyback rights.

## Suggested Salary Step-Ups

- €5,000/month starting.
- €6,000/month after $250k annualized revenue/run-rate.
- €7,500/month after $750k run-rate.
- €8,500/month after two EBITDA-positive quarters.

## Bonus Options

- 5% of first-year revenue from clients personally originated/closed.
- 2x monthly recurring revenue for white-label/franchise clients closed.
- 5%–10% of EBITDA above annual threshold.

## Dividend Policy

Once profitable and with six months cash reserve, 20%–30% of annual free cash flow can be available for distributions unless unanimously reinvested.
