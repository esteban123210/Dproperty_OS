# HANDOFF — Welcome Kit

> **Self-contained package** to produce the **franchisee Welcome Kit** — a premium, founder-led onboarding experience (physical box + digital items). This handoff is a **design + print-vendor brief**, not a single PDF: it lists every item, its spec, and the tone.

## 0. Production Brief
- **Deliverable:** the physical + digital welcome experience delivered when a franchisee signs.
- **Output:** (a) print/production spec for a design + print vendor; (b) designed digital assets.
- **Fidelity goal:** boutique, personal, premium, founder-led — the first tangible proof of the brand.
- **Tone:** boutique, personal, premium, founder-led.

## 1. Design & Brand Direction
- Palette `#F6F3ED`/`#161616`/`#1F4E79`/`#B89B5E`; editorial serif + sans; champagne foil/accents on print; heavy premium paper stock; restrained, elegant.

## 2. Assets Required
| Asset | Purpose | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Logo (print-ready, foil variant) | All printed items | Brand (to produce) | need |
| Printed manifesto + welcome letter copy | Box | HQ/founder | draft |
| Founder welcome video | Digital | Record | need |
| QR to onboarding portal/data room | Card | Generate | build |
| Fonts | All items | Brand kit | need |

## 3. Tool Instructions (the prompt)
> "Produce (a) a **print production spec sheet** and (b) **designed digital assets** for the items in *Section 4*. For each physical item give dimensions, material/stock, color (with the brand hex + champagne foil where noted), and finishing. For digital items, design to the brand system. Deliver print-ready files (CMYK, bleed, crop marks) and web-ready digital files. Keep everything boutique and founder-led. Flag any item needing copy or the founder's input."

## 4. Final Content (self-contained)

### Physical items
- Welcome letter (founder-signed) · printed manifesto · printed 90-day launch plan (see M3) · branded notebook · premium pen · business-card holder · small architectural object/desk item · franchise partner certificate/plaque · bottle of wine/champagne (if appropriate) · QR card to onboarding portal · Dproperty Select teaser card.

### Digital items
- Welcome email · welcome video · Dproperty OS login · CRM login · brand assets link · Training Academy link · calendar invites (kickoff + weekly) · support directory · launch checklist.

### Copy anchors
- Manifesto tone: "You are not buying a logo; you are joining a disciplined boutique operating system." · Welcome-letter warmth; founder-led; premium and personal.

### Production notes
- Box: rigid premium box, champagne foil logo. · Certificate: heavy stock, foil seal, franchisee name + territory. · QR card: links to onboarding portal / data room. · Keep everything consistent with the Brand Manual.

## 5. Build & QA Checklist
- [ ] Every physical item spec'd (size, stock, color/foil, finishing) — print-ready CMYK w/ bleed.
- [ ] Every digital item designed to brand.
- [ ] Copy anchors included; founder-signature placeholders.
- [ ] QR resolves to correct portal.
- [ ] Consistent with Brand Manual.
