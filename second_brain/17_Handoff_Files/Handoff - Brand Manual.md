# HANDOFF — Brand Manual

> **Self-contained package** to generate the **Dproperty Brand Manual** (PDF brand book) and seed a brand kit. The verbal system (essence, voice) and now the **visual system (colors, type)** are defined; logo files and photography library still need to be produced.

## 0. Production Brief
- **Deliverable:** the brand book that governs how Dproperty looks, sounds, and feels across franchise, white-label, developer, pitch, docs, and client comms.
- **Output:** **PDF brand book**, ~25–40 pages, plus exportable color/type tokens.
- **Target tool:** design tool (InDesign/Canva) or AI design/doc tool.
- **Fidelity goal:** the reference for every other deliverable's look — must itself be exemplary.

## 1. Design & Brand Direction (this IS the content)
- **Palette:** background off-white `#F6F3ED` · text charcoal `#161616` · primary accent deep blue `#1F4E79` · secondary champagne `#B89B5E`.
- **Typography:** editorial **serif** for headlines; clean **sans-serif** for body. (Select and document exact families in the kit.)
- **Feel:** boutique, calm, editorial, generous whitespace; few, high-quality images.

> "Generate a **premium PDF brand manual** from *Section 4*. ## 4. Final Content (self-contained)
**Cover:** *Dproperty — Brand Manual.*

**Brand essence:** boutique, curated, discreet, intelligent, investor-aware.
**Brand is NOT:** mass-market, pushy, generic, loud, overly corporate, a random broker network, a commodity listing agency.
**Brand IS:** personal, premium, warm, trust-based, clear, analytical, elegant, international, investor-aware.
**Tone of voice:** confident not arrogant · warm not casual · analytical not cold · premium not pretentious · direct not aggressive.
**Signature phrase (keep in Spanish):** *"Somos más amigos de la matemática que de la arquitectura."*

**Brand facts (for boilerplate/about):** founded 2017, Panama; the specialist firm in real estate investment (without being a fund); $200M+ transacted · 700+ operations `*(pending validation)*`; 99%+ clients never lost capital; 80%+ reinvest; portfolio landmarks Bioma, Mova, Cavarrosa, Nayamara, Playa Escondida, La Maison by Fendi; recognition SIMA / ELDI / Gran Salón Inmobiliario / Lonja de Bogotá board.

**Color system:** off-white `#F6F3ED` (backgrounds) · charcoal `#161616` (text) · deep blue `#1F4E79` (primary accent) · champagne `#B89B5E` (secondary/premium accent). Provide tints/shades + accessibility contrast notes.
**Typography:** editorial serif headlines; clean sans body; scale + usage rules.
**Visual identity sections to build:** logo usage · spacing · minimum size · monochrome · typography · color palette · photography style · icon style · presentation style · document style · social media style · office experience · welcome kit.
**Client-experience principles:** make the client feel guided · make complexity feel organized · never overpromise · use data carefully · protect trust · present projects with quality, not volume.
**Multi-market/brand rules (add):** how the brand adapts across ES/EN and markets; white-label contexts use the *system* without Dproperty brand (see [[Multi-Line Manual Strategy]]).
