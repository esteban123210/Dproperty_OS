# Franchisee Acquisition Playbook

**Goal:** Close 1-2 franchises by Sept 15, 2026.  
**Timeline:** 10.5 weeks (Jul 6 – Sept 15).  
**Primary metric:** Franchises signed.

---

## Part 1: Candidate Screening & Qualification Framework

### Who We're Looking For (All Three Personas)

A qualified franchisee candidate has:

✅ **Capital:** $30-50k available to invest (willingness to commit)  
✅ **Motivation:** Clear reason for wanting to start/scale real estate (ambition, income, autonomy)  
✅ **Credibility:** Either sales background, business experience, or real estate knowledge (lowers onboarding risk)  
✅ **Coachability:** Willing to follow Dproperty system, ask for help, iterate  
✅ **Timeline alignment:** Can sign by Sept 15, launch by Oct 1 at latest  

### Lead Scoring (Qualification Matrix)

Use this simple scoring model to prioritize follow-up:

| Criteria | Score |
|----------|-------|
| **Persona fit** (Agent seeking independence = 3, Career-changer = 2, Youth = 1) | 1-3 |
| **Capital readiness** (Has $50k+ = 3, Has $30-40k = 2, Needs financing = 1) | 1-3 |
| **Market advantage** (Local network in target market = 3, Can build network = 2, New to market = 1) | 1-3 |
| **Timeline** (Ready to sign within 6 weeks = 3, 8-12 weeks = 2, TBD = 1) | 1-3 |
| **Real estate background** (Licensed/selling = 3, Business ops = 2, Learning fresh = 1) | 1-3 |
| **Responsiveness** (Replied same day = 3, Within 48 hrs = 2, Slow = 1) | 1-3 |

**Scoring interpretation:**
- **15+:** Hot lead (prioritize; schedule discovery call immediately)
- **12-14:** Warm lead (schedule call within 1 week)
- **9-11:** Cool lead (follow-up sequence; revisit if bandwidth available)
- **<9:** Cold lead (low priority; nurture list for Phase 2)

## Part 2: Discovery Call Framework

### Pre-Call Prep (You)

**Before every discovery call:**
- [ ] Review lead profile (how did they come in? What's their background?)
- [ ] Check LinkedIn (understand credibility + network signals)
- [ ] Have franchise brochure, FAQ, economics one-pager ready to share
- [ ] Brief note on objection patterns from this persona (know your counter-arguments)
- [ ] Calendar blocked for 30-45 minutes (no interruptions)
- [ ] Zoom link ready + testing camera/audio
- [ ] Note-taking template prepared (structure for CRM entry post-call)

### Discovery Call Agenda (30-45 min)

**1. Opening (2 min)**
- "Thanks for making time. Quick goal for this call: I want to understand your situation and see if Dproperty is worth exploring further. No pressure — at the end, we'll both know if it makes sense or not. Sound good?"

**2. Background & Motivation (8 min)**

*For Youth:*
- "Tell me about your background — education, work experience, what brought you to real estate?"
- "Why are you thinking about starting a business vs. staying employed?"
- "What would your ideal first year look like? Revenue, lifestyle, growth?"

*For Career-Changer:*
- "What's your background? What field are you in now?"
- "What's prompting the shift to real estate?"
- "What would success look like — income target, timeline, team size?"

*For Agent Seeking Independence:*
- "How many years have you been selling real estate?"
- "How many deals did you close last year?"
- "Why are you thinking about going independent? What's pushing that?"

**3. Barriers & Pain Points (5 min)**
- "If you started your own [business / agency] today, what would be your biggest challenge? (systems, capital, knowledge, network, credibility)"
- "What would make that challenge go away?"
- "What's your biggest worry about making this move?"

**4. Dproperty Pitch (5 min)**
- "Here's what Dproperty solves: We've spent 10+ years building a real estate system, and we're packaging it as a franchise. It includes software (CRM), manuals (sales, compliance, ops), training, brand, and ongoing HQ support."
- "Investment is $30k launch + $1k/month. You keep 55% of commissions. Time to first deal: weeks, not months."
- "You get access to Dproperty Select — curated premium deals HQ brings to you."
- "And you're not alone — you're part of a network of other franchisees, plus HQ support."
- *Pause for reaction.*

**5. Qualification Questions (5 min)**
- "How does this land for you?"
- [Listen for objections; address them]
- "Do you have capital to invest?"
- "Would you be ready to launch this [timeline]?"
- "Do you want to explore this further?"

**6. Next Steps (5 min)**
- "Here's what I'm thinking: I'll send you [franchise brochure + FAQ + economics doc]. Take 20 minutes to read. Then, let's do a second call where I walk you through the full package and answer detailed questions. Sound good?"
- *If enthusiastic:* "And I'd love to connect you with [existing franchisee / HQ team member] so you can ask real questions from someone living it. Would that be helpful?"
- "What's your availability next week for a follow-up?"

## Part 3: Objection Handling Scripts

### "$30k is a lot of money"

**Response (build the case):**
"I hear you. Let's do the math. If you started alone:
- Consultant: €10-20k
- CRM + legal setup: €5-10k
- Compliance/training: €5-10k
- Still no deal access, no brand, no methodology.
- Plus: 12-18 months to first deal.

With Dproperty, you earn that $30k back in 7-8 deals. And your first deal often comes in Weeks 3-4 because you're not learning from scratch. You're leveraging a proven system."

**Follow-up:** "And the $1k/month fee is covered by your first 2-3 deals. After that, it's just the cost of the system — like a subscription software, but you own your business."

---

### "I don't have enough experience"

**Response (reframe):**
"Actually, that's often an advantage. You haven't developed bad habits. You're coachable. And here's what matters: you're willing to learn and you follow systems. That's more valuable than someone with 20 years of bad habits."

**Follow-up:** "Plus, our training and support are built for exactly this. Week 1, you get onboarded on the system, CRM, compliance, sales playbook. Within weeks, you're productive. We'll be on calls with you every week."

---

### "I want to do this completely independently"

**Response (the trade-off):**
"I get the appeal. Pure independence is attractive. Here's the trade-off you'd face:
- You build systems from scratch (12-18 months)
- You figure out compliance alone (risky)
- You build your own brand (takes years)
- You source deals from your network only (limited)
- You scale slowly (every process is you)

Dproperty franchise: you own your local business, keep your clients, run it your way — but we handle infrastructure. That speed and support is worth sharing revenue with HQ. You get to Year 2 profitability; an independent operator gets there in Year 3+."

---

### "What if the market turns / real estate crashes?"

**Response (resilience):**
"Real estate is cyclical — you're right. Here's how Dproperty de-risks that:
1. We charge on *collected* commission, not projected. We're not chasing bubbles.
2. Dproperty Select (HQ-managed deals) are premium, curated projects that often hold value even in slow markets.
3. If you build diversified clients + projects, one slowdown doesn't tank your business.
4. Plus, in a downturn, boutique agencies like ours do *better* (we're selective, not desperate). Mass-market franchises suffer more."

---

### "I don't want to be tied to HQ — I want full autonomy"

**Response (clarify the model):**
"You will have autonomy. You own your local business. Your clients are yours. Your pricing is yours. Your team structure is yours. What we do is:
- Provide the operating system (playbooks, manuals, compliance, reporting)
- Set brand standards (you represent Dproperty brand well)
- Require royalty on commission (you keep 55%)
- Provide weekly support + coaching

It's not like a McDonald's franchise where every detail is controlled. It's more like joining a professional network where you get structure + support + brand. You're still entrepreneurial."

---

### "Can I do this part-time while keeping my current job?"

**Response (honest take):**
"You can, but it's not ideal. This is a business, not a side gig. Your clients need you focused. Your team needs you focused. Our support works best when you're all-in.

That said, if you need to transition (giving notice, finding office, etc.), we can work with a 4-6 week ramp-up. But once you launch, we recommend 100% commitment for the first 90 days."

---

### "I already have clients / am already selling. Why do I need Dproperty?"

**Response (for Agents Seeking Independence):**
"Great question. You have the hardest part: clients + sales skills. Here's what you'd gain with Dproperty:
- Systems so you can scale beyond yourself (hire a team without chaos)
- CRM + reporting so you know what's happening (pipeline visibility)
- Compliance covered (no liability for you)
- Branding + materials you don't have to create
- HQ support + coaching on business strategy
- Access to Dproperty Select (deals beyond your current network)
- Recurring revenue sharing (not just your commission)

Basically, you get to scale from solo operator to a real business. And you keep 55% of commissions instead of 70% working alone, but you make *more* total money because you're scaling."

---

### "I want to think about it / I need to talk to my spouse / I need to research more"

**Response (move to next step, not goodbye):**
"100% — this is a big decision. Here's what I recommend:
1. Read the brochure + FAQ I'm sending
2. Maybe jump on a quick call with [existing franchisee name] — they went through exactly this decision
3. Talk to your spouse/advisor
4. Let's schedule a follow-up call for [day/time] when you've had time to digest. We can answer deeper questions then. Sound good?"

*This keeps the lead warm without pressure.*

## Part 5: Materials Checklist — What to Share

### During Discovery Call
- [ ] Franchise brochure (PDF, visually appealing, 3-5 pages)
- [ ] FAQ document (15-20 Q&A)
- [ ] Economics one-pager (commission splits, time to profitability visual)

### During Second Call (deeper dive)
- [ ] Full franchise agreement (lawyer-reviewed, in plain language with annotations)

- [ ] Training outline + academy roadmap
- [ ] Support structure + weekly call schedule

- [ ] Financial model for their market (what does Year 1 look like for them?)
- [ ] Territory map (where can they operate? Exclusive vs. non-exclusive?)
- [ ] Brand guidelines summary (what are the rules?)

### Before Signature
- [ ] Final legal agreements (all 6 contracts)
- [ ] NDA (if not already signed)
- [ ] Compliance checklist (what needs to be done before launch?)
- [ ] Onboarding timeline (Week-by-week for first 90 days)

### Weekly Rhythm

**Monday:**
- [ ] Review pipeline: Who's hot? Who's stalling? Who needs follow-up?
- [ ] Prioritize outreach: Focus on hot leads this week
- [ ] Update metrics: Track leads, calls, LOIs, close rate

**Tuesday:**
- [ ] Launch outreach: Send emails, post on LinkedIn, call hot leads
- [ ] Schedule calls: Aim for 3-5 discovery calls this week

- [ ] Discovery calls: Take calls, take notes, score leads

**Thursday:**
- [ ] Deep-dive calls: Walk hot leads through package, answer questions

**Friday:**
- [ ] CRM updates: Log all calls, update status, next steps

## Part 9: Special Playbook for "Agents Seeking Independence"

Since this persona has the highest close likelihood and fastest decision timeline, give them white-glove treatment:

### Fast-Track Process for Agents

**Week 1 (First contact → Discovery):**
- Personal, warm outreach (not generic email)
- 15-min discovery call same week if possible
- Emphasize: "You have the hard part (clients + skills). We have the infrastructure."

**Week 2 (Deep dive):**
- 30-min call: Walk through entire franchise agreement, Q&A
- Share: brochure, FAQ, agreement, economics, reference franchisee contact
- Emphasize: "Your existing clients move with you. Your book is yours."

**Week 3 (Negotiation):**
- Answer final questions, handle objections
- Offer LOI; negotiate terms if needed (e.g., reduced first-month fee, extended platform fee)
- Timeline: "We can close this by [date]."

**Week 4+ (Closing):**
- Legal review (should be fast; standard agreement)
- Signature + kickoff

**Total timeline:** 4-6 weeks from first contact to signed.

## Part 10: Risk Mitigation

### Red Flags (Don't Sign)

🚩 Candidate is only interested in accessing Dproperty Select (deal flow) without doing local sales.  
🚩 Candidate has stated they want to copy the system and go independent later.  
🚩 Candidate has very limited capital + unwilling to get financing.  
🚩 Candidate has a history of quitting ventures quickly.  
🚩 Candidate is dismissive of training, onboarding, or HQ support.  
🚩 Candidate has non-compete clause with existing broker + isn't confident about legal standing.

**Action:** If multiple red flags, politely pass. Not every lead should be signed.
