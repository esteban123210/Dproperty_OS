# First Franchisee Launch Playbook (Weeks 1–12)

> **Standards note (v0.2).** Rebuilt to project standards: **roles never names**, **USD**, **relative weeks** (not calendar dates), and **corrected commission math** tied to the [[../../08_Operations/Process Library/07 - Commissions]] firewall. HQ-side roles are HQ-shared services; the franchisee is the **PRIN**.

**Goal:** the first franchisee goes from signed agreement to **$10k+ in commission revenue** by end of Week 12.
**Timeline:** 12 weeks (relative; set actual dates per launch instance `[local]`).
**HQ support:** weekly calls, coaching, operational support.
**Success metric:** first deal closed by ~Week 8; ≥ 3 deals in pipeline by Week 12.

| Code | Role |

## Part 2: Week-by-Week Launch Plan

### Week 1 — Kickoff & system onboarding
**PRIN deliverables:** attend kickoff call (legal/tax/compliance overview, CRM walkthrough, brand review, support cadence, Q&A); complete CRM setup training (first 3 test contacts, first pipeline, first automations); set up the CRM front end (branding, first landing page, calendar, mobile test); personal-network launch (build 100-contact list; segment; draft launch message; send first 20).
**HQ support:** kickoff (2h) + tech (1h) + async feedback on first outreach (0.5h).
**Success:** CRM operational; 20+ outreach sent; 5+ responses.

### Week 2 — Pipeline building & training acceleration
**PRIN:** Dproperty Select training (deal flow, commission structure, HQ approval, rules); Sales Playbook review (scripts, weekly rhythm, objections, role-play); expand outreach to 100+; follow up Week-1 responses (schedule 5+ calls); begin broker outreach; Academy (Brand & Culture, OS Basics).
**HQ:** Select training (2h) + sales coaching (1.5h) + weekly call (1h).
**Success:** 100+ messages; 15–20 interest signals; 5+ discovery calls scheduled; compliance + sales training in progress.

### Week 3 — First calls & deal identification
**PRIN:** 8–10 discovery calls (use the discovery script; qualify; log in CRM; identify 3–5 warm); complete **compliance certification** + acknowledgment; prepare first presentation (project + local customization; dry-run with HQ); CRM hygiene (clean data, stage pipeline, automate follow-ups, build forecast).
**HQ:** compliance review + first-presentation dry-run + weekly call.
**Success:** 8–10 discovery calls; 3–5 warm prospects; compliance certified; first presentation scheduled.

### Week 4 — First presentation & potential first deal
**PRIN:** deliver first presentation; follow up; continue 5–8 more discovery calls; if a prospect is ready, guide reservation (**HQ reviews terms before signature**); prepare paperwork; Academy (Client Advisory, Projection intro).
**HQ:** presentation feedback + first-deal review/compliance check + weekly call.
**Success:** first presentation delivered; 15+ cumulative discovery calls; first deal in flight; 5+ prospects in stages.

### Week 5 — First deal close & momentum
**PRIN:** manage first deal to close (follow-ups, legal/escrow, documentation) → **first commission** ✅; keep prospecting (5–8 calls); document the first deal for a case study.
**HQ:** deal-closing support + optimization coaching + weekly call.
**Success:** **first deal closed**; first commission; 20+ cumulative calls; 5–8 prospects; credibility ("I just closed my first deal").

### Weeks 6–8 — Scaling pipeline & patterns
**PRIN:** 2–3 more deals closed/advanced; weekly prospecting (8–10 calls, 1–2 presentations, follow-ups); establish repeatable rhythm + standardized templates; optionally hire first support person and onboard on Dproperty systems; Academy (Client Advisory, Projection, Private Collection certs).
**HQ:** weekly calls + deal support + team-scaling guidance.
**Success by Week 8:** 3+ deals closed; 5+ in pipeline; **$10k+ commission revenue**; CRM smooth; repeatable process; (optional) first hire trained.

### Weeks 9–12 — Refinement & future planning
**PRIN:** aggressive prospecting (target 4–6 deals closed, 8–10 in pipeline by Week 12); team scaling if started; marketing launch (profile/content, business listing, local page, first campaign); broker-network building; complete remaining Academy certs; **Year-2 strategic plan** (review results, set targets, budget) with HQ.
**HQ:** weekly calls + marketing guidance + Year-2 planning (2h) + broker intros.
**Success by Week 12:** 4–6+ deals closed; 8–10+ in pipeline; mature CRM; documented process; (optional) first hire producing; marketing foundation; Year-2 plan locked.

## Part 3: HQ Support Structure

### Weekly accountability call (1 hr)
1. **Pipeline review** (15m) — how many prospects; closed/active/pending; stalls?
2. **Deal coaching** (20m) — walk 1–2 deals; obstacles; playbook adherence; next steps.
3. **Prospecting feedback** (15m) — discovery-call quality; objection patterns; messaging tweaks.
4. **Blockers & support** (10m) — tech, ops, compliance, coaching needs.
**Attendees:** HQ Launch Lead + PRIN (+ HQ Tech if needed).

### Ad-hoc support
- Deal-closing support (on-demand) · CRM troubleshooting (same-day) · legal/compliance questions (HQ Legal, within 48h) · sales coaching · marketing/brand support.

### Quarterly business review (2 hrs)
Performance vs. targets · metrics/trends · strategic adjustments · scaling plan · relationship check-in.

## Part 5: First-Franchisee Economics (USD, corrected)

**Assumptions:** avg unit price **$300,000**; **5%** gross commission; base case assumes **50/50 co-broke** with an external advisor (an editable model input). Uses the local commission waterfall + royalty base from [[../../00_Start_Here/Decision Log]] (2026-07-05) and the firewall in [[../../08_Operations/Process Library/07 - Commissions]].

**Per-deal money flow (base case, with co-broke):**
| Line | Amount | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Sale price | $300,000 | |
| Gross commission (5%) | $15,000 | |
| Less 50/50 co-broke | −$7,500 | external advisor share `[local: editable]` |
| **Gross-into-company (GCI)** | **$7,500** | royalty base |
| Less HQ royalty (7.5% of GCI) | −$562 | 6% + 1.5% on **collected** GCI |
| **Franchise retains** | **~$6,938** | before the office's own internal advisor/director split |

*No-co-broke upside:* if the franchisee sources and closes directly (no external split), GCI = $15,000 and HQ royalty = $1,125, so the franchise retains ~$13,875. The office's internal advisor/director payout is a separate flow (office policy).

**Illustrative cumulative (base case, ~$6,938 retained/deal):**
| Metric | ~Week 4 | ~Week 8 | ~Week 12 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deals closed (cumulative) | 1 | 3 | 5–6 |
| Franchise retained (approx.) | ~$6,900 | ~$20,800 | ~$34,700–41,600 |
| HQ royalty (approx.) | ~$562 | ~$1,686 | ~$2,810–3,375 |

**Operating costs (solo, monthly, USD):**
| Cost | Monthly |
|---|---|
| OS/platform fee | ~$1,000 |
| Office/workspace | $500–1,000 |
| Marketing | $200–500 |
| Admin/support (once hired) | $1,500+ (not needed solo) |
| Professional services | $200–500 |
| **Total (solo)** | **~$1,900–2,000** |

**Profitability:** Month 1–2 negative (setup); ~Month 3 break-even on first close; Month 4+ profitable; clearly profitable by Month 6 (2–3 deals/month in pipeline).

**Year-1 conservative:** 12–15 deals; franchise-retained roughly **$83k–104k** (base case, before internal splits); HQ royalty ~$7k–8.5k; operating costs ~$20–24k. **Year-2 (with team):** 40–60 deals; PRIN shifts to management. *(All figures modelled at 5% commission per Decision Log; validate against the Financial Model v0.7.)*

## Part 6: Risk Mitigation & Contingencies

### Red flag: no pipeline by ~Week 4
**Signs:** < 10 discovery calls; 0 pipeline; messaging not resonating.
**Intervention:** review recorded calls; specific feedback; adjust messaging/target; HQ introduces 3–5 warm leads; increase coaching to 2×/week.
**Decision point (~Week 6):** if still no pipeline, assess fit (coachable? wrong market? wrong person?).

### Red flag: first deal taking > 6 weeks
**Signs:** warm prospects not moving ("still thinking", financing).
**Intervention:** HQ joins prospect calls; deal-closing coaching; expedite financing/legal; offer a Dproperty Select deal (easier close, HQ-managed); reset timeline expectations `[local: some markets move slower]`.
**Decision point (~Week 8):** if still no close, assess market fit / sales skills.

### Red flag: PRIN wants to modify the system / skip steps
**Signs:** "I'll use my own CRM"; "the playbook doesn't work here, I'll do it my way"; "I don't want Dproperty Select."
**Response:** the system is contractual (that's the value they pay for); understand the real concern; test-as-designed for 4 weeks and measure; iterate **with HQ approval**, never unilaterally.
**Decision point (~Week 4–6):** refusal to follow the system is a culture/values-fit issue — an early warning.
