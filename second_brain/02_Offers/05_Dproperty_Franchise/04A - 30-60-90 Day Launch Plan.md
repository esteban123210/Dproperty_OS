# 30/60/90-Day Franchise Launch Plan

## Purpose

Give a new franchise owner a clear operating path.

## First 30 Days — Setup

- Legal agreements signed.
- Franchise workspace created.
- CRM/GoHighLevel setup completed.
- Brand assets delivered.
- Email/domain/signature created.
- Training started.
- Private Collection training completed.
- First 50 personal contacts identified.
- First broker list created.
- First developer list created.
- Social announcement drafted.
- First sales scripts practiced.
- Weekly HQ launch call scheduled.

## Days 31–60 — Activation

- First client meetings booked.
- First broker outreach completed.
- First project presentations delivered.
- First projections generated using approved assumptions.
- First lead campaign launched.
- Weekly pipeline review running.
- Private Collection opportunities registered through HQ.
- Local market mapping completed.
- Referral campaign activated.
- CRM hygiene reviewed.

## Days 61–90 — Commercial Discipline

- Pipeline audit completed.
- First reservations target set.
- Broker partnerships active.
- Local market strategy refined.
- Marketing performance reviewed.
- Compliance review completed.
- HQ quarterly review call completed.
- First case study drafted if a transaction or meaningful opportunity exists.
- Next 90-day plan approved.

## 90-Day Success Criteria

- CRM active and updated.
- First 100 contacts approached or segmented.
- Weekly pipeline rhythm active.
- At least one strong opportunity in progress.
- Brand and compliance standards understood.
- Franchisee can explain local vs Private Collection economics.
