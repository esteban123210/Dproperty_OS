# Dproperty Franchise — Executive Summary

## Offer

The Dproperty franchise is a premium, locally operated real-estate brokerage built on B_RealEstate's operating system. The franchisee receives Dproperty brand rights, BluePrint workflows, a configured CRM option, mandatory Building Blocks onboarding, Dproperty Select/eligible VAULTED access, templates, operating standards, HQ reporting, and launch support.

It is not a passive investment, guaranteed territory, lead guarantee, or promise of returns. The operator remains responsible for licensing, local employment/agent arrangements, sales execution, client service, local expenses, and legal compliance unless the agreement expressly allocates otherwise.

## Customer and job to be done

Best fit: an owner-operator or existing small brokerage that wants a credible investment-property brand, structured operations, technology, training, curated supply, and measurable governance. Poor fit: passive capital, undercapitalized candidates, operators unwilling to follow process, or markets without licensable brokerage economics.

## Included core

- Organization setup, Dproperty brand pack, workflows, templates, reporting, and test transaction.
- BluePrint Core for 12 months for defined users/office limits.
- Core onboarding: brand standards, CRM/BluePrint workflow, qualification, product diligence, documentation/compliance, closing and commission procedures.
- HQ standards, operating reviews, approved supply access, and defined support tier.

## Excluded/charged separately

Premium courses/certifications, custom migration, additional offices, excess AI/messaging usage, local brokerage licensing, office build-out, employees/agents, advertising outside the restricted-fund plan, travel, events, legal/tax work, and custom development.

## Gates before scaled sales

Two profitable reference offices; documented owner economics; launch checklist delivered within target hours; verified support capacity; trademark/territory protection; local franchise, brokerage, employment, tax, privacy and consumer advice; no material brand-standard exceptions.

## Key risks

Unproven brand premium, operator quality, support overload, local regulatory variance, overreliance on entry fees, data-neutrality concerns, and territory disputes. Mitigation is staged founding pricing, qualification scorecard, five-year conditional territory rights, measurable SLAs, restricted funds, and cohort-level contribution reporting.
