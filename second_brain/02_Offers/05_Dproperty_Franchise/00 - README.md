# Dproperty Franchise

**Type:** **Channel** — branded, vertically integrated deployment of the full stack. **Not a separate software product, and not the definition of the company.**
**Job:** Deploy the full branded operating model.
**Price:** Entry/launch fee + **6% royalty + 1% restricted brand fund** `[A]` · plus platform fees. Conflicts remain with the isolated 20-unit model's flat 6% + 1.5%.
**Status:** Richest operating material in the vault. **Economics and contract structure unresolved.**

Dproperty is the flagship investment brand, operating testbed and a vertically integrated distribution channel for the B_ stack.

It is **not** the definition of B_RealEstate's TAM and does not make B_ "a franchise company." It is a GTM and proof advantage most software startups do not have.

A franchise may receive the full branded operating model: Dproperty identity, configured stack (BluePrint + BlankCRM + Academy + VAULTED), standards and training, Dproperty Select access, implementation and network participation.

## What it owns

Branded operating model and identity · franchise onboarding and launch sequence · territory and brand rights · franchisee acquisition and sales process · local operating standards adherence · restricted brand-fund contributions

## What it does NOT own

Software product definition — that is BluePrint's and BlankCRM's · Dproperty Select curation approval (HQ-controlled; franchisees may propose, never self-approve) · the company's TAM definition · shared tenant data across independent customers
