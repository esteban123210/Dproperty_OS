# Franchise Unit Economics

## Base Assumption

- 50 units/year.
- $300k average unit price.
- 5% commission.
- $15k gross commission per unit.

## HQ Revenue From Local Sale

- Royalty 6%: $900.
- Network fee 1.5%: $225.
- Total before monthly OS allocation: $1,125.

## HQ Revenue From Private Collection Sale

- Gross commission: $15,000.
- HQ direct 50%: $7,500.
- Reduced royalty on franchise half: $225.
- Reduced network fee on franchise half: $75.
- HQ total: $7,800.
