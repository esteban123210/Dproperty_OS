# Welcome Kit Checklist

## Purpose

Create a premium onboarding experience.

## Physical Items

- Welcome letter.
- Printed manifesto.
- 90-day launch plan.
- Branded notebook.
- Premium pen.
- Business card holder.
- Small architectural object or desk item.
- Certificate/plaque.
- Bottle of wine/champagne, if appropriate.
- QR code to onboarding portal.
- Private Collection teaser card.

## Digital Items

- Welcome email.
- Welcome video.
- Dproperty OS login.
- CRM login.
- Brand assets link.
- Training academy link.
- Calendar invites.
- Support directory.
- Launch checklist.

## Tone

Boutique, personal, premium, founder-led.
