# Dproperty Franchise — Offer and Pricing

> **Resolved 2026-09-24.** Three competing royalty structures existed across the vault. This note fixes one and records why. Contract design and local legal advice are still required before any franchise signs. `[R]`

## The conflict that existed

| Source | Structure |
|---|---|
| Integrated offer / finance policy | Dproperty **6% + 1%** restricted brand fund · own-brand **4%, no fund** |
| Isolated 20-franchise workbook | **Flat 6%** across the whole portfolio **+ 1.5%** network fund |

## Resolution — the differentiated structure wins

**Canonical: Dproperty 6% + 1% restricted brand fund. B_ Partner 4%, no fund.**

Reasons:

1. **It is the only structure with commercial logic.** The 3-point gap between 6% and 4% is *the price of the Dproperty brand*. A partner keeping their own brand gets no value from a shared brand fund and no benefit from Dproperty's name, so they pay less. Remove the differential and one of the two channels becomes redundant.
2. **The flat 6% + 1.5% is a modelling simplification**, not an offer design. It exists so an isolated workbook could project 20 units without modelling two fee schedules. It remains valid **as a model scenario** and invalid **as a commercial offer**.
3. **A 1.5% fund on own-brand partners is unjustifiable.** They market their own brand; charging them into a Dproperty brand fund is a fee with no delivered benefit.

**The isolated workbook is not wrong — it is answering a different question.** It stays as-is, labelled as a portfolio projection, not as the fee schedule.

## Canonical price

| Line | Price | Status |
|---|---:|---|
| **Entry fee** | **$50,000** list · **$40,000** for the first two founding openings | `[A]` pilot offer |
| **Royalty** | **6% of collected GCI** | `[A]` pilot offer |
| **Minimum royalty** | **$750/month** from office month 13 | `[A]` |
| **Restricted brand fund** | **1% of collected GCI** — segregated, excluded from EBITDA | `[D]` |
| BluePrint | Included year 1; allocated at standalone list thereafter | `[D]` allocation rule |
| BlankCRM | Optional, allocated at standalone list ($750 + $249/mo) | `[D]` |
| Building Blocks core onboarding | Included, allocated at standalone value | `[D]` |
| Local setup / rebrand | ~$12,000 (franchisee's own cost) | `[A]` |

> **Definition matters:** royalty is on **collected GCI** — commission actually collected and retained after external co-broker shares and refunds, excluding indirect tax, before internal agent payouts. See [[../../01_Canon/12 - Glossary and Metric Dictionary]].

> **Restricted fund rule:** the 1% is **not revenue and not operating profit.** It is segregated for contractually defined brand/network use and excluded from EBITDA.

**Modelled fee-plus-setup cash payback: ~37 months.**

### The number a franchise prospect will actually ask

> *"What do I have to earn to justify the franchise cost versus staying independent?"*

**Approximately $92,632 additional annual collected GCI — about 9.3 additional closings per year** `[A]`.

That is the honest headline for a franchise proposal. It is a real bar, and stating it plainly is more persuasive than any projection.

## B_RealEstate economics — HQ's view

Per mature office at $360,000 GCI:

| Line | Annual |
|---|---:|
| Royalty at 6% | **$21,600** |
| BluePrint after free year (allocated) | ~$4,788 |
| BlankCRM if attached (allocated) | ~$2,988 |
| **Gross recurring per mature office** | **~$29,376** |
| Restricted brand fund (1%) | $3,600 — **not revenue** |

Against this sit direct support, platform and field costs, which are **not yet measured** `[R]`.

**Entry-fee margin cannot be claimed** until launch labour, travel, sales commission, legal and support costs are measured. At $40,000 founding pricing, the entry fee may be at or below true launch cost — as with B_ Partner, that is an acceptable disclosed investment in references, recorded as such.
