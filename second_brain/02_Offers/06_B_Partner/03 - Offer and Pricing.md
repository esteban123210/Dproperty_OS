# B_ Partner — Offer and Pricing

> **Rebuilt 2026-09-24.** The historical $10k/$20k setup and $1.5k/$2.5k monthly plans are **retired**. This note rebuilds the price from cost upward, as the canon requires: *software bundle + onboarding effort + ongoing service cost + network rights + target gross margin.*

## Canonical price

| Line | Price | Status |
|---|---:|---|
| **Entry fee** | **$30,000** list · **$25,000** for the first two founding partners | `[A]` pilot offer |
| **Royalty** | **4% of collected GCI** | `[A]` pilot offer |
| **Minimum royalty** | **$400/month** from office month 13 | `[A]` |
| **Restricted brand fund** | **None** — this is the deliberate difference from Dproperty | `[D]` |
| Software (BluePrint + BlankCRM) | Included year 1; allocated at standalone list thereafter | `[D]` allocation rule |
| Building Blocks core onboarding | Included, allocated at standalone value | `[D]` |
| Complex migration / extra offices | Quoted | — |

Founding discounts must be **disclosed, time/quantity limited and tied to reference participation** — never presented as list-price traction.

## Build-up — what the entry fee must cover

The entry fee is not margin. It is the recovery of a real, labour-heavy launch.

| Component | Estimate `[A]` |
|---|---:|
| Implementation and data migration | $4,000–$8,000 |
| Operating-model setup and process/manual pack | $3,000–$6,000 |
| BluePrint configuration and onboarding | $2,000–$4,000 |
| BlankCRM configuration | $750–$1,500 |
| Building Blocks core onboarding (allocated) | $1,500–$3,000 |
| Integration support | $1,000–$3,000 |
| Sales cost of acquiring the partner | $2,000–$5,000 |
| Legal and contracting | $1,000–$2,500 |
| **Total launch cost** | **$15,250–$33,000** |

**At $30,000 list the entry fee roughly covers launch cost — with little or no margin at the top of the range.** At the $25,000 founding price it is likely **below cost**. That is acceptable for the first two partners *as a deliberate, disclosed investment in references*, but it must be recorded as such and never modelled as entry-fee profit.

> **Canon rule:** entry-fee margin cannot be claimed until all launch labour, travel, sales commission, legal and support costs are measured.

## Why the royalty is 4% and not 6%

Dproperty charges **6% + 1% restricted brand fund**. B_ Partner charges **4% with no fund**. The 3-point difference is the price of the brand:

| | Dproperty Franchise | B_ Partner |
|---|---|---|
| Consumer brand | **Dproperty** — provided | **Partner's own** |
| Brand fund | 1% of GCI, restricted | **None** — they market their own brand |
| Royalty | 6% | **4%** |
| Dproperty Select access | Full | Eligible |
| Entry fee | $50,000 | $30,000 |

A partner keeping their own brand receives no brand-building value from a shared fund and no benefit from Dproperty's name, so they pay less. **This differential is the entire logic of having two channels** — if it collapses, one channel is redundant.

## Recurring economics for B_RealEstate

At a mature office producing **$360,000 collected annual GCI** `[A]`:

| Line | Annual |
|---|---:|
| Royalty at 4% | **$14,400** |
| Minimum royalty floor | $4,800/yr (only if GCI < $360k) |
| BluePrint after year 1 (allocated) | ~$4,788 |
| BlankCRM if attached (allocated) | ~$2,988 |
| **Gross recurring per mature partner** | **~$22,176** |

Against this sit **direct support, platform and account-management costs**, which are not yet measured. `[R]`

**Compare Dproperty at the same GCI: ~$21,600 royalty + ~$3,600 software ≈ $25,200.** B_ Partner yields roughly 12% less recurring revenue per office and — critically — **carries no brand fund**, so it also demands less HQ marketing obligation. Whether that trade is favourable depends on support cost, which is unmeasured.

## The capacity risk — the real constraint

B_ Partner is **service-heavy**. Each partner consumes implementation and operating-guidance capacity that would otherwise serve direct SaaS customers.

**Guardrail:** cap founding partners at a number the team can actually implement (suggest **two**, matching the founding-discount count) before committing to more. A signed partner who cannot be implemented on time damages the reference value the founding discount was bought for.

## Why this is not "BluePrint at a higher price"

BluePrint standalone is $399–$799/month with a $1,500 setup. B_ Partner justifies a $30,000 entry and a royalty only because it includes **real services**: implementation and migration, operating-model setup, process and manual packs, BlankCRM configuration, Building Blocks onboarding, integration support, ongoing operating guidance, network participation, and eligible Dproperty Select/VAULTED access.

**If a prospect wants only the software, sell them BluePrint.** Forcing a royalty model onto a SaaS buyer is how this channel cannibalizes direct sales.

> **Canon rule:** if own-brand partners prefer SaaS, **change the model** rather than forcing a royalty.

## Data and brand rule

The customer owns their brand and their tenant data. B_RealEstate cannot treat B_ Partner data as a shared competitive dataset. White-label partners run the **same codebase and data model** — branding and entitlements are configuration, never a source fork.

## Metrics to validate

Acceptance of the 4% royalty · actual launch cost vs the $30,000 entry fee · implementation hours per partner · ongoing service hours per partner per month · whether partners prefer a SaaS alternative · collected GCI per mature office · support capacity consumed per partner.
