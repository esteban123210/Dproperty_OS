# B_ Partner

**Type:** **Channel** — managed own-brand package. **Not a separate software product.**
**Job:** Deliver the managed stack and operating model under the customer's own brand.
**Price:** Entry fee + **4% royalty**, no restricted brand fund `[A]`. Historical $10k/$20k setup and $1.5k/$2.5k monthly plans are **retired**.
**Status:** Definition clear. **Pricing must be rebuilt from cost.**

An agency keeps its own consumer brand but buys a deeper managed implementation of B_ infrastructure and operating standards.

**Canonical name: B_ Partner.** "White-Label" is a legacy alias.

**Why this is not "BluePrint at a higher price":** BluePrint is standalone SaaS. B_ Partner can justify materially higher price only because it includes real services — implementation/migration, operating-model setup, process/manual packs, BlankCRM configuration, Academy onboarding, integration support, ongoing operating guidance, network participation and eligible Dproperty Select/VAULTED access.

White-label partners share the same codebase and data model. **Branding and entitlements are configuration, not a fork.**

## What it owns

Own consumer brand · own tenant data · managed implementation and operating model · entitlement configuration · network participation rights

## What it does NOT own

The software product definition · a source-code fork or dedicated codebase (explicitly not-now) · Dproperty brand rights · Dproperty Select curation approval

**Data rule:** the customer owns their brand and tenant data. B_RealEstate must not treat B_ Partner data as a shared competitive dataset.

- **Pricing not rebuilt.** Must be: software bundle + onboarding effort + ongoing service cost + network rights + target gross margin. The files still carry retired historical figures. `[R]`
- **`02 - ICP and Jobs To Be Done` missing.** How does a B_ Partner prospect differ from a direct-SaaS prospect? This distinction decides whether the channel cannibalizes direct sales.
- **4% royalty acceptance unvalidated.** `[A]` — needs founding-partner offers.
- **Trust question open:** will non-Dproperty firms trust a platform owned by a company that also runs a competing franchise brand? Requires a legal/technical separation story. `[R]`
- **Service delivery capacity unmodelled** — this channel is service-heavy and can silently consume implementation capacity needed for direct SaaS.
