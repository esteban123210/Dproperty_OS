# VAULTED — Standalone Product Record

## Executive summary

VAULTED is a gated real-estate opportunity and distribution network for qualified investors accessing developer-approved opportunities through trusted agencies. It combines controlled discovery, profile/qualification, NDA and deal-room access, agency/developer coordination, attribution, and conversion into a BluePrint transaction.

Initial VAULTED does not operate open mass-market listings, custody, escrow, FX, pooled investment vehicles, automated investment advice, or independent AML adjudication. Payment is tracking-only until legal structure and licensed providers are established.

## Access state

Public teaser → registered/qualified → NDA signed → invited/restricted → participant in a specific transaction. Each transition records policy version, evidence, actor, time, expiry and revocation. Access to one opportunity does not imply access to another.

## Supply and demand

- Supply: approved developer/project/unit records, availability windows, prices, documents, incentives, responsible owner and freshness SLA.
- Demand: investor identity/profile, consent, investable capital/cash-flow capacity, horizon, risk tolerance, markets, property type, objective and constraints.
- Matching must be explainable and must not imply regulated advice without appropriate product, licensing and disclosures.

## Minimum transaction loop

Approved opportunity → permitted discovery → qualification/NDA → interest → agency/developer response → validated inventory → BluePrint workspace → reservation/contract → close → attribution/commission confirmation → outcome data.

## Business model canvas

| Block | Design |
|---|---|
| Customers | Developers, agencies, qualified investors/buyers |
| Value | Gated trusted access, current information, controlled distribution, attributable execution |
| Revenue | Project/setup/SaaS first; transaction economics only after repeated closes and legal review |
| Key resources | Supply rights, identity/access graph, transaction spine, attribution, partner trust |
| Costs | Partner onboarding, diligence, data freshness, support, legal/compliance, product |

## Standalone financial hypothesis

The isolated model assumes average property value of **$300k** and a **0.35% effective take rate** in the base case, with adoption rising to 0.45 attributable transactions per active client in year 5 and 20% COGS. Base year-5 VAULTED revenue is **$133,216**; positive case is **$1.016M**. These are scenario mechanics, not verified pricing or legal entitlement.

## North star and health metrics

Completed attributable GMV with positive contribution. Supporting metrics: active approved supply, freshness, qualified demand, NDA-to-interest, response SLA, interest-to-workspace, close rate, cycle time, leakage, cancellation, partner share, disputes, net revenue and repeat use.

## Cold-start strategy

One corridor, one property/customer type, three to five developers, five to ten agencies, and manually supported attribution. Do not launch a broad public marketplace. Use concierge operations to learn, then automate repeated controls.

## Kill criteria

Pause if fewer than five credible supply partners participate; fewer than 25 qualified demand participants engage; no attributable close after a defined pilot window; inventory freshness remains unreliable; off-platform leakage destroys attribution; legal model is unclear; or contribution remains negative after direct labor. A pause protects BluePrint rather than invalidating it.
