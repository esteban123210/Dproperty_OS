# VAULTED

**Type:** **Product** — network/marketplace. The potential network-effect and transaction-monetization engine.
**Job:** Access and match network supply and demand.
**Price:** Success fee / take rate on attributable transactions. Effective base **0.35%** `[A]` — unvalidated.
**Status:** **Hypothesis only. Gated behind BluePrint stability.** No liquidity, attribution model or legal structure proven.

VAULTED is B_'s private marketplace/network for eligible real-estate supply, qualified demand, introductions and attributable transactions.

**BluePrint is the SaaS/IP engine. VAULTED is the potential network-effect engine.** These are different bets with different risk profiles and must be modelled separately.

**Modelling rule — non-negotiable:** never estimate VAULTED revenue as "all BluePrint customers × arbitrary GMV." Model the funnel: eligible organizations → activation → active listings/matches → attributable completed transactions → GMV → effective take rate.

## What it owns

Listings and opportunities · access rules · participant identity · marketplace engagement · matches and introductions · **attribution** · completed marketplace transaction records · GMV and marketplace fees

## What it does NOT own

CRM conversations · BluePrint management processes and transaction execution · accounting ledger · Dproperty Select curation decisions · **custody, escrow, FX or pooled investment vehicles** (explicitly not-now)

## Gaps — genuine, not placeholders

This is the **thinnest offer folder in the vault**, and that is honest — it reflects real maturity, not missing documentation work.

- **`02 - ICP and Jobs To Be Done` missing.** Who is the first supplier and the first buyer? Undefined.
- **`03 - Offer and Pricing.md` missing.** Take rate, commission base and fee mechanics undefined per jurisdiction. `[R]`
- **`05 - Economics.md` missing.** The funnel model above has never been built. `[R]`
- **`06 - Legal.md` missing — highest-risk gap.** Brokerage licensing, commission-sharing legality, marketplace liability and AML all vary by jurisdiction and none have counsel review. `[R]`
- **No attribution design.** How attribution survives an off-platform closing is the single question that decides whether this business exists.
- **No leakage/circumvention controls designed.**
