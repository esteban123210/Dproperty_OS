# Developer Partnerships

## Strategic role

Developer Partnerships are a **revenue + supply + distribution + learning channel**, not Product #5.

## What B_ may provide

CRM/sales-process setup, training, broker activation, reporting, managed sales support, network distribution and selected operating services.

## Why developers matter to the ecosystem

1. Direct service/performance revenue.
2. Seed supply for VAULTED.
3. Potential Dproperty Select opportunities.
4. Product learning from complex sales organizations.
5. Distribution into developer sales teams.

## BluePrint boundary

BluePrint does not own developer project/unit inventory or reservation execution. It may be used by the developer organization for management-control use cases if it fits the ICP.

## Economics

Historical baselines (~0.5% team-managed sales; ~2.5–3% network-sourced; $3k–$5k managed desk) remain hypotheses/legacy commercial structures. Clarify fee bases before external publication.
