# Developer Pricing Model

## Sales Enablement

- Setup/training fee: negotiable.
- 0.5% on developer-team sales.
- 2.5% on Dproperty-sourced sales.

## Managed Sales Desk

- $3,000–$5,000/month.
- 0.5%–0.75% on developer-team sales.
- 2.5%–3% on Dproperty-sourced sales.

## Exclusive Project Sales Partner

- Larger negotiated commission pool.
- Dproperty takes deeper role in sales execution.

## Principle

Never price only as 10% of salesperson commission payout.
