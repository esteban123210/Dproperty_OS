# Developer Partnerships — Offer and Pricing

> **Clarified 2026-09-24.** The historical percentages were ambiguous about **what base they applied to** — the total commission pool or B_RealEstate's retained fee. That ambiguity made every figure unusable externally. This note fixes the bases with worked examples.

## The ambiguity being fixed

"~0.5% on team-managed sales" and "~2.5–3% on network-sourced sales" were quoted without stating the base. Those are **completely different businesses** depending on interpretation:

- 0.5% **of transaction value** on a $300,000 unit = **$1,500**
- 0.5% **of a 3% commission pool** on the same unit = **$45**

A 33× difference. No figure may be published until the base is explicit on every line.

**Rule: every percentage states its denominator. Always.**

## Worked examples on a $300,000 unit

| Scenario | Fee | B_RealEstate receives |
|---|---|---:|
| Team-managed (developer's own brokers, B_ runs process/reporting) | 0.5% of transaction value | **$1,500** |
| Network-sourced (B_ brings the buyer) | 2.5% of transaction value | **$7,500** |
| Network-sourced at the upper band | 3.0% of transaction value | **$9,000** |
| Managed desk retainer | flat | **$3,000–$5,000/month** |

**Why the 5–6× gap between team-managed and network-sourced is correct:** in the team-managed case B_ provides *process and infrastructure* while the developer's brokers own the relationship and the selling. In the network-sourced case B_ provides *the buyer* — which is the scarce input and carries the acquisition cost.

## Relationship to the total commission pool

A developer typically allocates a total sales commission pool (commonly 3–5% of transaction value `[A]`, market-dependent). **B_RealEstate's fee is drawn from within that pool, not added on top.**

On a $300,000 unit with a 4% pool = $12,000 available:

| Case | B_ fee | Remaining for other participants |
|---|---:|---:|
| Team-managed 0.5% ($1,500) | $1,500 | $10,500 to the developer's brokers |
| Network-sourced 2.5% ($7,500) | $7,500 | $4,500 |
| Network-sourced 3.0% ($9,000) | $9,000 | $3,000 |

**This is where network-sourced pricing gets tested in reality.** At 3% of transaction value, B_ takes three-quarters of a 4% pool. That is defensible only when B_ genuinely sourced a buyer the developer could not otherwise reach. **If a developer's pool is 3%, a 3% network fee consumes all of it and the deal is impossible.** Every quote must be checked against the actual pool for that project before it is offered.

## Retainer economics

At **$3,000–$5,000/month** for a managed desk:

| Cost `[A]` | Monthly |
|---|---:|
| Desk staffing allocation | $1,500–$3,000 |
| Reporting, process setup, account management | $500–$1,000 |
| **Contribution** | **$500–$2,500** |

**This is headcount-heavy revenue with thin contribution.** It buys relationship, supply access for VAULTED and product learning from complex sales organizations — **not** margin. Price it as a strategic access cost, and do not scale it as though it were software.

## Why developers matter beyond the fee

1. Direct service and performance revenue.
2. **Seed supply for VAULTED** — the cold-start advantage.
3. Potential Dproperty Select opportunities.
4. Product learning from complex sales organizations.
5. Distribution into developer sales teams.

Reasons 2–5 are why this channel exists despite thin retainer contribution. Be honest internally that the retainer is close to a cost-recovery mechanism.

## Boundary — must not drift

B_RealEstate does **not** own the developer's project or unit **inventory**, and BluePrint does **not** become the inventory system of record. Reservation execution stays with the developer's own system.

Scope discipline: this is a **sales and inventory-distribution partnership**, not broad development consulting.

## Metrics to validate

Actual pool percentage by project and market · realized fee by base · desk staffing cost per project · attribution on network-sourced deals · whether developers will pay separately for Building Blocks cohorts or expect them included in the retainer · VAULTED supply actually contributed per developer relationship.
