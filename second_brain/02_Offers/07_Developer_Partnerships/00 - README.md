# Developer Partnerships

**Type:** **Channel** — revenue + supply + distribution + product learning. **Not Product #5.**
**Job:** Serve developer sales organizations and seed network supply.
**Price:** Retainer + performance/commission. Historical baselines: ~0.5% team-managed sales · ~2.5–3% network-sourced · $3k–$5k managed desk. **All legacy hypotheses.** `[A]`
**Status:** Real relationships and revenue history. **Fee bases ambiguous — not safe to publish.**

Developer Partnerships are a revenue, supply, distribution and learning channel.

**Why developers matter:** direct service/performance revenue · seed supply for VAULTED · potential Dproperty Select opportunities · product learning from complex sales organizations · distribution into developer sales teams.

**Canonical name: Developer Partnerships.** "Developer Sales OS" is a legacy alias.

Scope discipline: this is a sales/inventory partnership, **not** broad development consulting.

## What it owns

Developer commercial relationship · managed sales desk and broker activation · sales-process setup and training for developer teams · reporting to the developer · network distribution of developer inventory

## What it does NOT own

Developer project/unit **inventory** as a system of record · reservation execution · the developer's own CRM · pricing authority over developer stock

BluePrint may be used by the developer organization for management-control use cases where it fits the ICP, but it does not become the inventory system.
