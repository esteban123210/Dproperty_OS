# Pricing and Packaging

> These are **commercial hypotheses, not validated prices**. Test with design partners before external publication.

## Pricing philosophy

Price per organization/operating complexity, not per sales-agent seat. Sales agents normally remain in the CRM; BluePrint value is management leverage and avoided management overhead.

## Working plans

| Plan | Target | Working price | Core idea |
|---|---|---:|---|
| **Core** | small agency beginning to professionalize; up to ~10 users | **$399/month** | assistant, knowledge, transaction spine, processes, glitches, actions, basic finance/report, capped AI |
| **Growth** | growing agency with real management complexity; up to ~25 users | **$799/month** | Core + integrations, deeper financial control, commission engine depth, approvals, process health, advanced reports |
| **Scale** | larger/multi-team or multi-office SME | **quoted — deliberately undefined** | Growth + advanced permissions, controls, integrations, audit and executive reporting |

Priced **per organization/office**, never per agent seat.

> **Superseded 2026-09-23:** the previous $299 / $599 / $999 tiers are retired. The $399/$799 points are tied to the integrated financial model with CAC/payback/ARR outputs. See [[../../18_Ecosystem/18 - Canonical Reconciliation and Precedence]] §5.

## Onboarding

One-time implementation/onboarding: **$1,500** standard. Higher for complex migration, additional offices, dedicated environment or custom integrations — separately quoted.

Excess AI, storage and messaging use is separately quoted.

Setup revenue is not incidental: it funds the implementation cost that makes the subscription retainable.

## Value logic

The product should be easy to justify if:
- the commission snapshot is trusted over the spreadsheet;
- a deal file survives an audit or a dispute without reconstruction;
- one administrator + BluePrint replaces substantial founder admin time;
- the customer delays/avoids one additional management hire;
- BluePrint catches material financial/process errors;
- management reporting becomes reliable without manual spreadsheet reconciliation.

If customers perceive it as “prettier Odoo” or “a dashboard on top of GHL,” these price points will be difficult to sustain.

## Bundling

Dproperty Franchise and **B_ Partner** commercial packages may include BluePrint, but the software must remain independently sellable to agencies using their own brand and CRM. Bundles must not obscure the standalone product economics, and bundled software revenue must never be double counted in the company model.

## Modeled economics `[M]` — unvalidated, do not market

Standalone model base case, including BlankCRM attach: software ARR per customer **$4,645.80**; recurring gross-margin contribution **$3,600.40**; modeled CAC **$1,000**; payback **3.33 months**; simplified LTV/CAC **45x**. These are attractive *assumptions*, not actual unit economics, and must never be presented as traction.

## Metrics to validate

Willingness to pay by agency size; onboarding willingness; gross margin after AI/integration/support cost; expansion triggers; churn; CAC/payback.
