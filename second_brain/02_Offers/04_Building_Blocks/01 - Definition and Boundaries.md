# Building Blocks

## Product definition

**Building Blocks is B_RealEstate's standalone learning, onboarding and certification product.** It turns operating standards into role-based curricula and preserves assignment, completion, certification, expiry and competency evidence.

It is powered by **Open edX** as infrastructure. Open edX is the engine; Building Blocks is the product.

**Canonical name: Building Blocks.** Legacy aliases: *Academy*, *Building Blocks*, *Training Academy*.

## It is a standalone product — by decision

Building Blocks is **separately definable, separately priced and separately sellable**. It is not a feature of BluePrint and not merely an onboarding cost centre.

This is a deliberate portfolio decision (2026-09-24). See [[../../01_Canon/19 - Portfolio Composition Principle]].

Two consequences:

1. **It must stand on its own.** Its own ICP, its own price, its own economics, its own P&L line. A product that cannot be sold alone is not a product.
2. **It composes into the franchise and partner packages.** "You receive BluePrint, BlankCRM, Building Blocks and VAULTED" is a materially stronger offer than "you receive our platform." Each named product carries its own perceived value into the bundle.

## The job

**Make capability transferable.** An operating standard that lives only in a manual does not change behaviour. Building Blocks converts standards into assigned, assessed, evidenced competence.

## Owns

- learning content and role-based curricula;
- course structure, modules and learning paths;
- assignment and enrollment;
- assessments and scoring;
- **certification, expiry and renewal**;
- competency evidence and completion records;
- cohort and instructor management;
- learner-facing experience.

## Does NOT own

- management or process truth → **BluePrint**;
- the process system of record — **Building Blocks never becomes that**;
- transaction execution, compliance sign-off or approvals → **BluePrint**;
- lead or contact data → **BlankCRM**;
- marketplace listings → **VAULTED**;
- accredited formal qualifications — it makes **no** claim to issue them;
- licensed legal/compliance training where local regulation requires an accredited provider;
- a document repository for daily transaction files.

## Product architecture

| Layer | Content | Monetization |
|---|---|---|
| **Core onboarding** | Brand and operating standards, BluePrint/BlankCRM workflow, qualification, diligence, documents/compliance, closing/commission | Included for defined franchise and partner users |
| **Paid specialist courses** | Investing, project sales, compliance operations, leadership, advanced product | Per enrollment — **$199 blended** `[A]` |
| **Certification** | Assessment, evidence, expiry/renewal, role or inventory gating | Enrollment/program fee |
| **Enterprise / developer cohorts** | Branded cohort, project-specific training, manager dashboards | Scoped licence + setup |

## The closed loop with BluePrint

This is what makes Building Blocks more than a generic LMS:

```
approved SOP → execution → BluePrint Glitch / process-health signal
   → identified capability gap → Building Blocks module or cohort
      → assessment + certification → BluePrint gates activation or privilege
         → later operating outcome measured in BluePrint
```

BluePrint **displays** learning status and **may gate** activation, role assignment or inventory access on certification. Open edX owns the learning activity; BluePrint owns the gate and the management consequence.

No competitor selling a real-estate LMS has the operating-failure signal to drive enrollment. No competitor selling a management system has the remediation path. **The loop is the differentiator, not the courseware.**

## Users

New franchise and partner teams · agents and coordinators · managers · developer sales teams · external real-estate professionals buying focused courses.

## Investor framing — be precise

Building Blocks is a **real product with real revenue**. It is **not** where the proprietary moat sits.

State both honestly:
- **Commercially:** a standalone product with its own price, its own buyers and its own revenue line.
- **Strategically:** proprietary value concentrates in **BluePrint** (transaction/management IP) and **VAULTED** (network effect). Building Blocks is retention, quality, enablement and attach revenue.

Do not present it as a headline venture-scale business on its own. Do not hide it either — a franchise buyer values it highly, and it is part of why the package commands its price.

## Boundaries with Dproperty Franchise

The franchise **consumes** Building Blocks; it does not own it. Franchise-specific packaging lives in `02_Offers/05_Dproperty_Franchise/`. Shared operating standards — the source material curricula are built from — live in `08_Operations/Process Library/`.

When a process changes in `08_Operations/`, the corresponding Building Blocks module is stale. That dependency is real and currently unmanaged. See [[00 - README]] gaps.

## Metrics

Onboarding completion rate · time to launch readiness · assessment performance · certification currency (% non-expired in required roles) · **enrollments triggered by an operating failure** · measured performance change after intervention · paid enrollments per customer per year · standalone course revenue.
