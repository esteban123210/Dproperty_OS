# Dproperty Select Commission (formerly Private Collection Split)

> **Revision 2026-07-04:** The 50/50 gross split is replaced by a **fixed percentage of
> sale price** paid to the originator. This preserves HQ negotiation upside and gives the
> franchisee a predictable number.

## Payout Structure

The originator (whoever brings/sells the deal) earns a fixed % of the **sale price**, not a share of commission:

- **Dproperty branded franchise: 2.5% of sale price.**
- **White-label partner: 1.5% of sale price** (external-partner-broker terms, revised 2026-08-03 from 2.0%; the earlier blanket exclusion on white-label access is retired).

HQ retains the balance of the negotiated commission.

## Projection Assumption

Always model Dproperty Select at **5% total commission**. If HQ negotiates 6%–8% on a select project, the originator payout stays fixed and **HQ captures the upside** — projections are never inflated.

## Example (branded, $300,000 unit, modelled at 5%)

- Total commission (5%): $15,000.
- Franchisee payout (2.5% of price): **$7,500**.
- HQ retains: **$7,500**.

## Example (white-label, $300,000 unit, modelled at 5%)

- Total commission (5%): $15,000.
- Partner payout (1.5% of price): **$4,500**.
- HQ retains: **$10,500**.

## Notes

- At exactly 5%, the branded payout equals the old 50/50 ($7,500). Above 5%, the fixed-% structure is strictly better for HQ.
- **Open item:** whether the franchisee pays any royalty/Network fee on Dproperty Select earnings. Default assumption: **no** (2.5% / 1.5% is net to the originator).

## Reason

HQ contributes curated inventory, developer relationships, project control, negotiation, and quality assurance. The originator contributes client origination, local trust, and sales activation.


## Proposed Select distribution scheme — 2026-10-26 (management decision for modeling; contract approval pending)

**Replaces the earlier branded-originator 2.5%-of-sale-price approach for the proposed new Select network model; existing signed agreements, if any, must be honored or amended.** Historical material above remains as an audit trail, not an alternative to stack on the new payout.

| Eligible realized distributable commission | Allocation |
|---|---:|
| Dproperty Select headquarters, clearing, QA and network services | 30% |
| Registered franchise that originated and successfully onboarded the accepted project | 20% |
| Selling partner(s) on the registered buyer transaction | 50% |

- Where originating and selling franchise are the same: it earns **70%**; HQ **30%**.
- Where HQ originated the project and a partner sells: HQ takes **50%** (30% network + 20% sourcing); seller **50%**.
- The selling pool can be divided among lead generator, referring brokerage and closer if registered and agreed before closing; total remains 50%.
- The originator attribution is tied to accepted project/developer contract, a documented originator ID and an explicit contractual term, **not** merely sharing a lead or having indefinite rights.
- Define the commission pool as collected developer commissions less only contractually approved taxes, refunds, co-broker deductions and offsets; record all gross-to-net adjustments.
- **No automatic additional ordinary franchise royalty on Select distributions** in the planning model; ordinary non-Select GCI remains subject to its own documented royalty terms. Restricted brand fund is not HQ profit.
- Applicability to B_ Partner/white-label/independent agents is **not decided** by this scheme; preserve separate partner contracts pending approval. Do not apply the 20% source bounty to every external introduction.
- Approval gates: actual developer commission agreements; compliant brokerage/referral licensing by country; due diligence and project approval; buyer attribution; cancellation/clawback; cash collections; support cost; conflict handling.

**Example:** $300k sale × 5% commission = $15k. HQ $4,500; project originator $3,000; selling allocation $7,500; same originator selling earns $10,500.

**Projection assumptions:** 2–3 newly accepted Select projects sourced by each mature active Dproperty franchise each year, 40 unit closings/project at $300k and 5% commission. Project-level results: $12M annual GMV, $600k commission, HQ $180k, originator $120k, selling pool $300k.
