# Dproperty Select Deal Workflow (formerly Private Collection)

## Steps

1. Franchise identifies potential client.
2. Franchise qualifies client.
3. Franchise submits Dproperty Select request to HQ.
4. HQ approves or rejects access.
5. Client is registered.
6. Approved materials are shared.
7. Franchise presents or co-presents with HQ.
8. HQ manages sensitive developer/project questions.
9. Reservation/contract process follows HQ rules.
10. Fixed-% payout is recorded (2.5% of sale price branded / 1.5% white-label, external-partner-broker terms).

## Required Controls

- Client registration timestamp.
- Approved project materials.
- Approved assumptions.
- Payout confirmation (fixed % of sale price).
- HQ handoff record.
