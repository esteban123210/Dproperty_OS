# Developer Deal Workflow

## Steps

1. Developer discovery call.
2. Project data intake.
3. Sales process audit.
4. CRM/pipeline setup.
5. Sales team training.
6. Broker network activation.
7. Project launch plan.
8. Weekly pipeline review.
9. Monthly developer reporting.
10. Fee/commission reporting.

## Required Documents

- Developer Sales OS Agreement.
- Project Data Intake Form.
- Sales Team Training Plan.
- Broker Activation Plan.
- Reporting Dashboard.
- Commission/Fee Schedule.
