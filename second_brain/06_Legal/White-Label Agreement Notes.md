# White-Label Agreement Notes

## Key Clauses Needed

- Scope of software/workflow license.
- Brand use restrictions.
- No Dproperty brand unless approved.
- No automatic Private Collection access.
- Confidentiality.
- Non-copy/non-circumvention.
- Implementation obligations.
- Monthly fees.
- Support level.
- Data ownership.
- Termination.
- IP ownership.

## Strategic Point

White-label clients get systems, not the full Dproperty franchise identity.
