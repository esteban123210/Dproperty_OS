# Data and Privacy Notes

## Data Types

- Client personal data.
- Buyer preferences.
- Investor budgets.
- Broker data.
- Developer/project data.
- Deal documents.
- Commission records.
- Training records.

## Requirements To Verify

- GDPR implications if EU clients/users are involved.
- Panama/Colombia data privacy rules.
- Data processing agreements for franchisees/white-label clients.
- CRM/GoHighLevel data processing.
- Access controls.
- Data retention.
- Export/deletion rights.

## Principle

Do not centralize sensitive client data without clear permission, access rules, and legal review.
