# Legal Architecture

## Purpose

Map the legal documents needed to make Dproperty OS/franchise/white-label/developer models safe.

## Core Legal Areas

- Franchise agreement.
- Brand license.
- Dproperty OS software license.
- Territory agreement.
- NDA/confidentiality.
- Non-circumvention.
- Data processing/privacy.
- Commission and royalty agreement.
- Private Collection access agreement.
- Broker network rules.
- White-label agreement.
- Developer Sales OS agreement.
- IP ownership.
- Termination and renewal.
- Dispute resolution.

## Legal Principle

Obsidian may hold summaries and draft logic, but signed agreements should live in a secure legal folder / SharePoint / DocuSign / Adobe Sign.
