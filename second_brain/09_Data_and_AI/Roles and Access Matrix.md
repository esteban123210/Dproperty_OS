# Roles and Access

Canonical personas/jobs: [[../02_Offers/01_BluePrint/02 - ICP and Jobs To Be Done]]

Core roles:
- **Owner/CEO:** company health, finance, reports, approvals, decisions.
- **Administrator/Secretary:** primary operator; upload, record, retrieve, prepare, route and execute non-sensitive work.
- **Finance/Accountant:** verification, budgets, reconciliation and financial views.
- **Manager:** department processes, glitches and actions.
- **Sales Agent:** normally works in CRM; minimal/no full BluePrint access.

Sensitive financial, HR, policy and approval actions require explicit permissions and audit events.
