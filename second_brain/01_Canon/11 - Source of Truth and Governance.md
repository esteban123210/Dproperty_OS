# Source of Truth and Governance

## Precedence

When records conflict, use this order:

1. Executed contract, law, regulator instruction, bank statement, payroll record, or audited actual.
2. Board-approved decision record and budget.
3. **`18_Ecosystem/18 - Canonical Reconciliation and Precedence.md`** — controls the vault.
4. `18_Ecosystem/` canon and the `04_Product/BluePrint/` product folder.
5. This folder's canonical finance, data/AI, operations and diligence records.
6. Current named financial model for quantitative projections.
7. Current product/technical requirements for build scope.
8. Business plan and pitch deck.
9. Prior manuals, notes, brainstorms, and chat outputs.

Higher precedence does not make a source universally authoritative. A contract governs its parties; a financial model governs projections; a technical brief governs quotation scope. Never use a pitch slide to override a contract or model.

## Document state

`DRAFT → REVIEW → APPROVED → SUPERSEDED → ARCHIVED`

Every approved record needs: owner, approver, effective date, review date, source links, truth labels, and a change note. Superseded records remain available but begin with a visible supersession notice.

- Financial projections cite workbook, sheet, scenario, horizon, and version.

## Change control

Material changes require a decision record if they affect price, rights, data use, regulatory exposure, brand promise, product boundary, roadmap gate, funding, or more than 10% of model results. The decision record must show alternatives, evidence, owner, approval, downstream files, and rollback conditions.

## Review cadence

- Weekly: pipeline evidence, experiments, product blockers, incidents.
- Monthly: budget/actual, model assumptions, support capacity, roadmap gates, evidence register.
- Quarterly: strategy, pricing, product boundaries, risk register, data retention, PESTEL.
- Event-driven: law/regulation, security incident, pricing change, new country, new payment/custody role, new AI provider, or acquisition.

## Prohibited knowledge practices

- No silent overwrites of approved policy or financial assumptions.
- No AI-generated claim presented as fact without an evidence link.
- No customer or tenant data pasted into uncontrolled prompts.
- No cross-tenant aggregation unless contract, privacy basis, and metric policy permit it.
- No “latest” file based only on filename; check version/effective date.
- No market-size or valuation claim without formula and source.
