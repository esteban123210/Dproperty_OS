# DpropertyLiving Reactivated — 2026-09-24

## The decision

**DpropertyLiving is reactivated as the consumer-facing (B2C) channel for commercializing inventory to end users.** It receives its own GoHighLevel sub-account in Phase 1 of the development route map.

## Why it was parked, and why that no longer holds

| Parked because (2026-09-20) | Why that changed |
|---|---|
| "Adds brand/commercial complexity without solving a necessary problem" | It now solves a **specific, named problem**: Dproperty is positioned for *investors*, but inventory must also be sold to *end users who want to live in the property*. Those are different buyers with different language, different objections and different funnels. |
| "End-user/lifestyle agencies can use B_ products under their own brand" | That addresses *other agencies*. It does not address **our own** need for a consumer-facing front door for our own inventory. |
| No commercial case | The commercial case is now concrete: a second GHL sub-account, its own content calendar, its own pipelines, running the same inventory to a different audience. |

## The brand split — and the line that must not blur

| | **Dproperty** | **DpropertyLiving** |
|---|---|---|
| Audience | Investors, buyers seeking **return** | End users seeking **a home** |
| Language | Yield, appreciation, absorption, exit | Lifestyle, location, space, community |
| Product framing | An asset | A place to live |
| Typical channel | Referral, broker network, investor relationships | Social (Instagram, Facebook), portals, WhatsApp |
| Inventory | **Same inventory, different narrative** | **Same inventory, different narrative** |
| Curated program | Dproperty Select | Not applicable |

**Critical rule:** DpropertyLiving is a **marketing and sales channel brand**, not a new product, not a new franchise line, and not a new software tenant. It does not get its own royalty model, territory rights or economics.

## What was NOT reversed

The 2026-09-20 parking decision remains correct on these points, which stay in force:

- **No franchise or territory rights** for DpropertyLiving. It is not a fourth commercial door.
- **No separate economics or pricing.** Revenue flows through the same transaction and commission structures.
- **Not an investor-deck component** as a standalone business. It appears as a channel inside the Dproperty go-to-market story, nothing more.
- **No product claims.** It sells inventory; it does not sell software.

If DpropertyLiving is ever proposed as a franchise line or a separate business, that requires a **new** decision record. This note authorizes a channel, not a company.

## Ecosystem placement

```
                     same inventory
                           │
        ┌──────────────────┴──────────────────┐
        ▼                                     ▼
  Dproperty                            DpropertyLiving
  investor narrative                   end-user narrative
        │                                     │
        └──────────────┬──────────────────────┘
                       ▼
                 BlankCRM (GoHighLevel)
              two sub-accounts, one engine
                       │
              qualified opportunity
                       ▼
                   BluePrint
```

Both brands feed **BlankCRM** as separate sub-accounts, and both hand qualified opportunities to **BluePrint**. The handoff line is unchanged: BlankCRM owns demand until qualification, BluePrint owns everything after.

## Risks accepted

1. **Brand dilution.** Two Dproperty-prefixed brands can confuse the market. Mitigated by keeping DpropertyLiving strictly consumer-facing and never using it in investor, franchise or software contexts.
2. **Operational overhead.** Two sub-accounts, two content calendars, two pipelines — real work for a small team. Phase 1 of the route map must budget for it explicitly.
3. **Message leakage.** An investor seeing lifestyle content, or an end user seeing yield calculations, damages both. Requires disciplined channel separation.
