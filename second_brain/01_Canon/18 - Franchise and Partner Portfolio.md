# B_Franchising Portfolio

B_Franchising offers three controlled entry models into the same operating universe. They share identity, BluePrint, selected BlankCRM and Building Blocks capabilities, governance, analytics, and support infrastructure. They differ in brand, economics, entitlements, inventory, responsibility, and legal relationship.

| Dimension | Dproperty franchise | Own-brand / white-label partner | Developer sales program |
|---|---|---|---|
| Buyer | New or converting brokerage operator | Existing agency retaining its brand | Developer/project owner or sales team |
| Brand | Dproperty | Customer brand | Developer/project brand |
| Core value | Brand + system + playbook + network | Operating system without rebrand | Controlled inventory distribution and sales execution |
| Initial price hypothesis | $50k list; first two $40k | $30k list; first two $25k | Scoped setup; quote by projects/integrations |
| Recurring hypothesis | 6% collected GCI; $750 monthly floor from month 13 | 4% collected GCI; $400 monthly floor from month 13 | Platform/SaaS + optional success fee after counsel |
| Restricted fund | 1% collected GCI in integrated offer; isolated model used 1.5% | None | Campaign/project budget only if contractually segregated |
| Software | BluePrint first year; then priced plan; BlankCRM optional | Same shared stack | Project/inventory/distribution/transaction modules |
| Training | Mandatory core curriculum | Core implementation; optional certification | Product, inventory, channel and sales enablement |
| Governance | Highest brand/territory control | Workflow/data/SLA standards, not brand control | Inventory truth, access, attribution and response SLAs |
| Primary proof metric | Incremental owner cash profit/payback | Operating gain vs independent status | Sell-through, cycle time, attribution and developer ROI |

## Portfolio principles

- No country master rights, unlimited territory, guaranteed leads, guaranteed returns, or unlimited implementation in the entry fee.
- Recommended agreements are five years with territory/quality performance conditions, subject to local advice.
- Entry fees cannot fund permanent payroll without a recurring-revenue and cash bridge.
- GCI means commission actually collected and retained after external co-broker shares/refunds and excluding indirect tax, before internal agent payouts. Never charge royalties on property value.
- Brand/marketing funds are restricted and excluded from EBITDA.
- Brokerage referral income and private-collection commissions remain in the appropriate brokerage ledger; never double count them as B_ royalties or software revenue.
- Additional offices, complex migration, custom workflow, premium content, messaging/AI usage, events, and travel are separately priced.

## Investor reading of the 20-unit model

The isolated model reaches 20 active franchises in year 5. Base year-5 network GCI is **$6.5M** on **$130M property sales volume**; B_ income is **$680.4k**, EBITDA **$457.4k**, and maximum 10-year normalized EV at 8× is **$3.328M**. Only the positive case reaches the illustrative $5M EV milestone (year 7). This confirms franchising can be profitable and strategically useful but is not the standalone unicorn engine.

The integrated launch model supersedes isolated pricing where they conflict. Final contractual economics require legal/tax review and paid pilots.
