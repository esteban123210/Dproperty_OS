# Supervisor and Owner Q&A

## Why not just improve internal Dproperty operations?

Because internal operations are only the first step. The real opportunity is to turn the operating model into a scalable product and franchise system.

## Why would someone buy this?

Because they get brand, systems, training, software, templates, and access to a premium operating method without building it from scratch.

## Why would developers pay?

Because many developers are good at building projects but weak at sales process, CRM, broker management, and reporting.

## Why does Esteban need equity?

Because this is not a normal employee task. It requires building a new venture, product architecture, business model, and operating system from scratch.

## Why not raise external capital?

The existing owners already have the most strategic assets. External capital may be useful later, but early funding can be internal and milestone-based.

## What is the biggest risk?

The biggest risk is building documents and software without clear adoption. That is why pilots should start early.

## What must be protected?

Private Collection, brand quality, developer relationships, and legal control.
