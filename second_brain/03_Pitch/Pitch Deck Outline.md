# Investor Pitch Deck Outline — Product-Led Reset

> Supersedes the July franchise-first 20-slide deck for external/investor use.

## S1 — Thesis
**B_ — operating infrastructure for independent real-estate businesses.**

## S2 — Problem
Great sellers become overloaded managers. As agencies grow, truth fragments across CRM, WhatsApp, spreadsheets, accounting and people; founders lose selling time or hire expensive management too early.

## S3 — Why current tools leave a gap
CRM optimizes sales. ERP/BPM can control enterprises but is heavy/implementation-led. Small agencies bridge the gap manually.

## S4 — BluePrint
Chat-first AI management OS. One capable administrator can operate standardized finance oversight, processes, glitches, management actions, reporting and audit.

## S5 — Product in action
Show four concrete flows:
1. invoice → classification/budget/approval;
2. glitch → SOP/control/process-health;
3. CRM “won” → reported vs verified truth;
4. monthly management report / “why are we over budget?”

## S6 — Product stack
**BlankCRM sells · BluePrint runs · VAULTED connects · Academy standardizes.**

## S7 — Why the pieces reinforce
Demand from CRM → network matches in VAULTED → verified outcomes → BluePrint intelligence → better agency performance/retention → more participants.

## S8 — Beachhead ICP
Sales-led real-estate agency, initially roughly 5–50 people, too complex for founder/spreadsheets but too small/unwilling for enterprise ERP/C-suite overhead.

## S9 — Business model
BluePrint SaaS + BlankCRM attach + VAULTED take rate + managed/channel economics. Separate recurring software, transaction revenue and services.

## S10 — Distribution advantage
Dproperty operating testbed, franchise channel, B_ Partners, developer relationships and existing investor/broker network.

## S11 — Competition / why B_
Compare GHL, Odoo, SAP/Signavio, vertical transaction tools and marketplaces. Do not claim feature uniqueness; emphasize prebuilt real-estate management operating model, chat-first administration, verification/process assurance and network.

## S12 — Market
**DO NOT USE OLD 600k/100k/1,500 numbers.** Build bottom-up from ICP-specific agency counts, segment ACV and reachable countries. Show verified sources and assumption labels.

## S13 — Validation / roadmap
Prototype → Dproperty internal pilot → 5 design partners → paid pilots → product engineering → BlankCRM attach → narrow VAULTED network validation.

## S14 — Team / unfair starting position
Attribute Dproperty history/relationships honestly. Show why this team has access to real operators, developers and supply.

## S15 — Ask
Capital tied to proof milestones. Ask must be rebuilt after MVP/vendor/build-cost and GTM model, not inherited from historical $650k plan.

## Appendix
BluePrint architecture; pricing hypotheses; BlankCRM economics; VAULTED funnel/take rate; franchise economics; B_ Partner; developer economics; TAM sources; historical model vs new model; data governance; long-term optional hub vision.
