# Pitch Deck Outline v0.5

Audience: Luz Adriana + Simón. Internal venture pitch, not VC deck.
Thesis sentence (repeats on slides 4, 8, 14): 
"We franchise the boutique know-how, not the investment operation."

## Slide 1 — Title
Dproperty OS. The operating system behind boutique real estate.
One line: 10+ years, $200M+ transacted, 700+ operations. Now productized.
[Track record figures pending validation per handoff doc]

## Slide 2 — The Problem We Actually Have
Not an abstract market problem. OUR problem:
- Growth today requires Luz Adriana to be present everywhere.
- Every new market = more management load on the owners.
- The know-how lives in people, not in systems.
Purpose: Luz Adriana recognizes her own life on this slide. Emotional anchor.

## Slide 3 — What We Refuse to Lose
Boutique positioning. Curated access. Trust-based advisory.
"Somos más amigos de la matemática que de la arquitectura."
Purpose: pre-empt the fear that scaling = becoming a mass franchise like RE/MAX.

## Slide 4 — The Thesis
We franchise the know-how, not the operation.
- HQ keeps: investment portfolio, Private Collection, developer relations.
- Franchisees get: the complete system to run a boutique agency.
- We supervise one director per market, not every deal.
Purpose: the core reframe. This is the slide they remember.

## Slide 5 — Why Now
- Operations documented (15 functional areas mapped).
- European expansion already in motion (DACH, Benelux, Iberia).
- The know-how is extractable NOW because we just spent a year documenting it.
- AI/low-code makes system productization cheap for the first time.
- Every year we wait, competitors systematize first.

## Slide 6 — The Product: Dproperty OS
Agency-in-a-box: OS software + manuals + training (Academy) + CRM 
(white-labeled GHL sub-accounts, HQ retains network visibility) + 
document/projection generators + reporting.
Data first, documents second.
Purpose: show it's a system, not a folder of PDFs.

## Slide 7 — Three Revenue Lines, One Decision Rule
1. Branded franchise: markets that feed the investment funnel.
2. White-label OS: markets that are purely local/transactional.
3. Developer Sales OS: priced on gross sales value.
The rule: brand goes where investors are; systems go everywhere.

## Slide 8 — Private Collection Stays Home
HQ-controlled. 50/50 gross split. No unapproved projections, no direct 
developer negotiation. White-label gets no automatic access.
Purpose: answers "do we lose control of our crown jewels?" No.

## Slide 9 — Unit Economics (per branded franchise)
Base case: 50 units/yr × $300k × 5% GCI = $750k franchise GCI.
HQ take: $15k launch + $12k/yr OS fee + 6% royalty + 1.5% network fee.
KEY HONESTY POINT: royalty triggers on COLLECTED commission, not signed.
Minimum royalty floor from month 7 (placeholder $750/mo, creditable).
Playbook: franchisees run fast-collecting local business; deferred LATAM 
pre-construction routes through Private Collection where HQ manages timing.
Purpose: this slide survives Luz Adriana's cash instinct because it was 
built FROM it.

## Slide 10 — Conservative 5-Year Plan
5 branded / 20 white-label / 15 developer projects.
Y1 ~$128k → Y5 ~$2.27M revenue, Y5 EBITDA ~$601k.
[Numbers need re-run under collected-GCI trigger before v0.9]
Explicitly labeled conservative. No hockey stick.

## Slide 11 — What It Costs
$650k over 24 months, two tranches.
T1 $350k: MVP, legal, manuals, pilots.
T2 $300k: released ONLY at milestones (MVP live, 2 white-label pilots, 
1 developer agreement).
Purpose: owners fund milestones, not promises.

## Slide 13 — Team & Structure
Simón 35 / Luz Adriana 35 / Miguel 15 (communications & creative, vesting) 
/ Esteban 15 (strategy & management, vesting). 4-year vest, 1-year cliff 
for operating partners.

1. Do we build this as a NewCo/JV? 
2. Do we fund Tranche 1 ($350k)?
3. Do we formalize the four-person structure and roles?
Close: "You keep doing what only you can do. The system does the rest, 
everywhere else."
