# Founder Pitch to Dproperty Owners

## Purpose

This document is the internal pitch to the two Dproperty owners. It should explain why Dproperty OS is an opportunity, what needs to be built, what the owners need to invest, and what role/equity Esteban should receive.

## Positioning

This should not sound like asking for a favor. It should sound like a structured venture opportunity.

## 1. Why This Exists

Dproperty already has knowledge, relationships, projects, brand potential, and sales experience. The missing piece is a system that makes this scalable.

## 2. What We Are Building

A three-part venture:

1. Branded Dproperty franchise.
2. White-label OS for boutique agencies.
3. Developer Sales OS.

## 3. Why Now

- Boutique agencies need systems.
- Developers need better sales infrastructure.
- Dproperty already has project access and credibility.
- AI/software makes process standardization easier.
- A franchise can scale the brand without opening every office directly.

## 4. How It Helps Existing Dproperty

- Creates new recurring revenue.
- Professionalizes internal operations.
- Turns knowledge into an asset.
- Protects and monetizes Private Collection.
- Creates a network of future client/broker/developer sources.

## 5. What Needs to Be Built

- Franchise package.
- Legal agreements.
- Brand manual.
- Sales manual.
- Operations manual.
- Dproperty OS MVP.
- CRM setup.
- Training academy.
- Reporting system.
- Private Collection workflow.
- Developer Sales OS offer.

## 6. Required Internal Investment

Recommended internal venture budget:

- $650k over 24 months.
- Split into two tranches.
- Includes product, legal, training, content, customer success, sales/BD, and Esteban's salary.

## 7. Esteban's Role

Proposed role:

- Co-founder & Venture Lead.
- Product & Strategy Lead.
- Managing Director Dproperty OS.

Responsibilities:

- Build venture architecture.
- Manage product development.
- Create franchise system.
- Coordinate docs/manuals/templates.
- Prepare pitch/sales materials.
- Build prototype and workflows.
- Support pilot execution.
- Manage tracker/Obsidian/knowledge system.

## 8. Proposed Compensation

- Salary: €5,000/month starting.
- Equity: 10%–15% in NewCo.
- Vesting: 4 years with 1-year cliff.
- Possible bonus and salary step-ups.

## 9. Decision Needed

The owners need to decide:

1. Do they want this to become a new business line/NewCo?
2. Are they willing to fund the first 24 months?
3. Are they willing to give Esteban a formal venture role and equity?
4. Which first market/product should be prioritized?
