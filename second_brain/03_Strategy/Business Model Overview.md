# Business Model Overview

## Primary engine — BluePrint SaaS

Organization-level subscription for management/back-office OS. Working pricing hypothesis: $299 / $599 / $999, not yet validated.

## Attach engine — BlankCRM

Configured GoHighLevel product sold standalone or attached. Revenue = subscription/resale margin after third-party + communications + support cost.

## Network engine — VAULTED

Transaction-linked monetization on attributable marketplace transactions. Model from real activation/transaction funnel and effective take rate.

## Learning — Building Blocks

Primarily bundled retention/standards layer; possible premium/custom training.

## Distribution/service channels

### Direct SaaS
Independent agencies keep their own brand and potentially their own CRM.

### B_ Partner
Managed own-brand implementation, higher ARPA because services/operating model are included.

### Dproperty Franchise
Branded full-stack deployment with separate franchise economics.

### Developer Partnerships
Revenue + supply + distribution; may generate managed-service/performance revenue and seed VAULTED/Select.

## Modeling principle

A customer can touch several layers. Do not double count software revenue embedded inside channel packages. Report recurring SaaS, marketplace transaction revenue and services/channel economics separately.
