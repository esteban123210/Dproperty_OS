# Customer Ladder & Expansion Economics

## The Entry-to-Premium Journey

B_RealEstate is designed as a **ladder**, not a single product. Customers enter at different levels and move upward as they find value.

```
                     TIER 4: B_ FRANCHISE
                   Full operating model
                   Full brand integration
                  $45k launch + $2.5k/mo + 7.5%
                        ▲
                        │
                        │ (Highest-value customers)
                        │
                   TIER 3: B_ NETWORK MEMBER
                 Preferred distribution access
               VAULTED priority, benchmarking
                      (Optional add-on)
                        ▲
                        │
                        │ (Natural migration)
                        │
                    TIER 2: VAULTED
                    Marketplace participant
                 Access off-market inventory
                  0.75% transaction fee
                        ▲
                        │
                        │ (Automatic if using BluePrint)
                        │
                    TIER 1: BLUEPRINT
                   Operating system only
                      $99–1,500/mo
              (No commitment beyond SaaS)
```

## Tier 1: BluePrint (Foundation)

### Entry Point

**Target:** Any real-estate agency, anywhere.

**Offer:** "Better transaction management for your existing CRM and workflow."

**Pricing:**
- Solo: $99–149/month (3 users, 5 active transactions)
- Growth: $299–499/month (10 users, 25 active transactions)
- Pro: $750–1,500/month (unlimited users, 100+ transactions)
- Enterprise: Custom

### Customer Profile

| Segment | Size | Typical Agency | Monthly Fee | Use Case |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Solo** | 1–3 people | Colombian boutique, single broker | $99–149 | Transaction tracking, basic compliance |
| **Growth** | 5–20 people | Regional agency (10–30 units/yr) | $299–499 | Full transaction workflow, team performance |
| **Pro** | 20–100 people | National/multi-office broker | $750–1,500 | Complex compliance, benchmarking, reporting |
| **Enterprise** | 100+ people | Large brokerage, developer sales team | Custom | White-label, custom integrations, SLAs |

### What They Get

- Transaction workspace (intake → closing)
- Compliance gates and document generation
- Commission tracking and reporting
- Team performance dashboards
- Copilot assistance (included allowance)
- Integration with existing CRM
- Mobile access
- Audit trail and compliance reporting

### Why They Adopt

1. **Low friction.** No long contract, no setup fee, works with existing tools.
2. **Immediate value.** Solo agent sees benefit in Week 1.
3. **Growing with them.** As they scale, they upgrade tiers (not lock-in, but natural upsell).
4. **Industry-specific.** Built for real estate, not generic project management.

### Year 1 Economics

Assume 50 BluePrint organizations in Year 1:

| Segment | Orgs | Avg Price/mo | Annual Revenue |
|---|---|---|---|
| Solo | 25 | $120 | $36k |
| Growth | 20 | $350 | $84k |
| Pro | 5 | $1,000 | $60k |
| **Totals** | **50** | **$300** | **$180k** |

**Churn assumption:** 20%/year (low for high-value SaaS).

**Retention revenue:** 40 orgs × $300 = $144k.
**New customer acquisition:** 30 new orgs × $200 (mix of solos joining) = $72k.
**Year 2 BluePrint revenue:** $144k + $72k = $216k (plus upgrade upsell).

Actually, let me recalibrate to match the revenue model:

| Year | Organizations | Avg Price | ARR |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 50 | $300 | $180k |
| 2 | 150 | $350 | $630k |
| 3 | 350 | $380 | $1.596M |
| 4 | 850 | $420 | $4.284M |
| 5 | 1,500 | $450 | $8.1M |

This assumes:
- Year 1→2: 3x growth (early adoption curve)
- Year 2→3: 2.3x growth
- Year 3→4: 2.4x growth
- Year 4→5: 1.8x growth
- Churn: 15–20%/year (compensated by new + upsell)

## Tier 2: VAULTED (Expansion)

### Entry Point

**When:** After 2–4 months on BluePrint.

**Offer:** "Access off-market inventory from your network. Sell properties faster to better buyers."

**Pricing:** Free to join. Commission on transactions: 0.75% of deal value.

### What Triggers Adoption

Agencies naturally discover VAULTED:
1. They're using BluePrint daily (transactions are flowing through).
2. They hear about a project/property that's available through VAULTED.
3. They list it. They sell something. They make money.
4. They come back.

**There's no friction because they already pay for BluePrint.**

### Customer Profile

| Engagement Level | Behavior | Annual VAULTED Revenue |
|---|---|---|
| **Light** | 2–5 deals/year | $300–1,500 |
| **Active** | 10–20 deals/year | $1,500–6,000 |
| **Power** | 30+ deals/year | $6,000–15,000+ |

### Expansion Economics (Tier 1 → Tier 2)

**Scenario:** BluePrint Growth agency ($350/month = $4,200/year).

- Y1: $4,200 (BluePrint only).
- Y2: $4,200 (BluePrint) + $2,500 (10 VAULTED deals) = $6,700.
- Y3: $4,200 (BluePrint) + $6,000 (20 VAULTED deals) = $10,200.
- Y4: $4,200 (BluePrint) + $12,000 (40 VAULTED deals) = $16,200.

**LTV doubles** (or triples) without increasing base subscription cost.

**B_ captures this value without friction.** The agency is already paying for BluePrint; VAULTED is pure expansion.

## Tier 3: B_ Network Member (Optimization)

### Entry Point

**When:** After 6–12 months, agency is regularly using BluePrint + VAULTED.

**Offer:** "Join the B_ Network. Get exclusive access, benchmarking, referrals, and enhanced support."

**Pricing:** Optional add-on, $500–2,000/month (depending on size/commitment).

### What They Get

- Preferred access to Dproperty Select inventory
- Benchmarking reports (compare to network average)
- Referral network (agencies refer deals to each other)
- Collective marketing (B_ brand co-op funds)
- Priority support + account manager
- Network training (Academy access)
- Copilot Pro tier (higher allowance)
- International co-broking (international referrals)

### Why They Join

The agency is already making money through BluePrint + VAULTED. Adding $500–2,000/month for benchmarking + Select access + referral network is a natural upsell.

**The math:**
- Agency was making $10–20k/year from VAULTED deals.
- With Select access, they close 3–5 additional premium deals = $15–30k additional.
- Network referrals: 2–3 referral deals/year = $10–15k additional.
- **Total additional revenue: $25–45k.**
- **Cost of membership: $6–24k/year.**
- **ROI: 2–7x.**

## Tier 4: B_ Franchise (Premium)

### Entry Point

**When:** After 18–36 months, agency is highly successful and sees full franchise upside.

**Offer:** "Become B_ Franchise. Brand integration, full playbook, international network, preferred inventory, equity upside."

**Pricing:**
- $45,000 launch fee (one-time)
- $2,500/month platform fee (or included in franchise fee)
- 6% royalty on local GCI
- 1.5% network/brand fund
- 2.5% of Dproperty Select deals

### Who Becomes a Franchise

**Profile:** 
- Proven agency (30–60 units/year consistently)
- Owner-operator mindset
- Aligned with B_ brand and values
- Wants international positioning
- Wants scale capital/support

**NOT every BluePrint customer becomes a franchise.** That's not the goal. The goal is high-quality adoption.

### Franchise Economics

**Franchisee generates:**
- 50 units/year @ $350k = $87,500 GCI
- Plus 3 Select deals @ $350k

**B_ gets:**
- Platform fee: $30,000/year
- Royalty (6%): $5,250/year
- Network fund (1.5%): $1,312/year
- Select commission (2.5%): $26,250/year
- **Total: $62,812/year**

**Franchisee gets:**
- B_ brand (traffic, prestige, investor access)
- Operating model (blueprints, playbooks, processes)
- Technology (BluePrint, GoHighLevel, Building Blocks)
- Network (VAULTED, international referrals, benchmarking)
- Select inventory (priority access to premium deals)
- Support (compliance, HR, operations, sales coaching)

**This is business-in-a-box.**

### Expansion Economics (Network Member → Franchise)

**Scenario:** Agency was Network Member.

- Pre-franchise (Tier 3): $4,200 (BluePrint) + $1,000 (Network) + $15,000 (VAULTED + Select) = $20,200/year from B_.
- Post-franchise: $62,812/year + growth potential.

**Roughly 3x expansion.**

## The Full Journey Example

### Small Colombian Agency: Year 0–5

**Year 0 (Pre-B_):**
- Revenue: $200k GCI (20 units @ $350k @ 5% commission)
- Challenges: No infrastructure, manual processes, no network access

**Year 1 (Tier 1: BluePrint)**
- Adopts BluePrint Growth ($350/mo)
- Revenue: $200k GCI + $4.2k from B_ = $204.2k
- Benefit: Faster closings, better compliance, better reporting

**Year 2 (Tier 1 + Tier 2: VAULTED)**
- Still BluePrint Growth, discovers VAULTED
- Closes 8 VAULTED deals @ average $300k = $1,800 additional
- Revenue: $200k GCI + $4.2k (BP) + $1.8k (VAULTED) = $205.8k
- Benefit: Network access, more inventory

**Year 3 (Tier 2 + Tier 3: Network Member)**
- Joins B_ Network ($1,000/month)
- Closes 15 VAULTED deals = $4.5k
- Closes 2 Select deals (network member exclusive) = $7.5k
- Revenue: $200k GCI + $4.2k (BP) + $4.5k (VAULTED) + $7.5k (Select) + $1k (Network fee deducted) = $215.7k
- Benefit: Select access, benchmarking, referral network

**Year 4 (Tier 3 + Tier 4: Franchise)**
- Becomes B_ Franchise
- Agency grows to 35 units/year locally (due to network + brand)
- Plus 20 VAULTED deals, 4 Select deals
- Revenue structure changes:
  - Local GCI: $245k
  - VAULTED (20 × $300k × 0.75%): $4.5k
  - Select (4 × $350k × 2.5%): $35k
  - Total GCI to agency: $284.5k
  - B_ take: $62.8k/year
- Benefit: Brand, full support, international network, growth capital

**Year 5 (Franchise, Scaling)**
- Agency grows to 50 units/year (due to brand, support, network)
- Plus 30 VAULTED deals, 6 Select deals
- Revenue structure:
  - Local GCI: $350k
  - VAULTED (30 × $300k × 0.75%): $6.75k
  - Select (6 × $350k × 2.5%): $52.5k
  - Total GCI to agency: $409.25k
  - B_ take: $75k+/year

**Summary:**
- Year 0: $200k (no B_)
- Year 1: $204k (BluePrint, +2%)
- Year 2: $206k (+ VAULTED)
- Year 3: $216k (+ Network)
- Year 4: $285k (+ Franchise, +32%)
- Year 5: $409k (+ Growth, +43%)

**B_'s revenue from this customer:**
- Y1: $4.2k
- Y2: $6k
- Y3: $12.2k
- Y4: $62.8k
- Y5: $75k+

**LTV for this customer: $160k+ over 5 years.**

## Network Effects Acceleration

As more agencies adopt the ladder:

1. **More BluePrint → More data → Better Copilot.**
   - Copilot learns from thousands of transactions.
   - Better insights, better forecasting.
   - Existing customers see more value.

2. **More VAULTED participants → More liquidity.**
   - More inventory available.
   - More matches. More deals.
   - Developers see value. More supply.

3. **More Select deals → Higher-value ecosystem.**
   - Network members see premium inventory.
   - Motivation to stay in network.
   - Motivation to become franchise.

4. **More franchises → Stronger brand.**
   - B_ brand becomes more recognized.
   - Attracts more agencies who want to join.
   - Attracts better developers.

**This is the flywheel.**

## Retention & Expansion Revenue

### BluePrint Retention

**Year 1 Retention:** 80–85% (SaaS standard for high-value product)
**Year 2+ Retention:** 90–95% (as switching costs increase)

**Reason:** Once agencies embed BluePrint in their workflow, leaving is painful.

### Expansion Revenue (Tier Upgrade + Network Join + Franchise)

| Metric | Y1 | Y2 | Y3 | Y4 | Y5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| BluePrint orgs | 50 | 150 | 350 | 850 | 1,500 |
| % that upgrade tier | 30% | 35% | 40% | 40% | 40% |
| % that join VAULTED | 20% | 50% | 70% | 80% | 85% |
| % that join Network | 5% | 10% | 20% | 30% | 40% |
| % that franchise | 0% | 1% | 2% | 3% | 4% |

**Expansion revenue as % of total new revenue:**
- Y1: Low (mostly base subscriptions)
- Y3: ~40% (tier upgrades, VAULTED)
- Y5: ~60% (Network, Franchise, upsells)

## Why This Ladder Works

1. **Low barrier to entry.** BluePrint at $99/month is not a bet. Try it.

2. **Organic expansion.** No aggressive sales needed. Customers discover value naturally and upgrade.

3. **Aligned incentives.** Each tier creates real economic value for the customer, not just "more features."

4. **Defensibility.** As customers move up the ladder, switching costs increase (data lock-in, process integration, network value).

5. **Scalability.** Tier 1 (BluePrint) scales infinitely. Tiers 3–4 are high-touch but high-margin.

6. **Profitability at all stages.** Even if customers never upgrade beyond BluePrint, the unit economics work.

This is how you build a $100M+ business without needing to sell franchises to everyone.
