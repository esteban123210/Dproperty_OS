# KPIs, Gates, and Kill Criteria

## Company north star

Completed governed transactions with positive contribution and expanding recurring platform revenue. GMV without attribution or contribution is not the north star.

## KPI tree

- **Acquisition:** qualified pipeline, source, CAC, sales cycle, conversion.
- **Activation:** contract-to-live, onboarding hours, training completion, first transaction/time-to-value.
- **Engagement:** weekly active orgs/users, target-user rate, workflow completion, active transactions.
- **Retention:** logo/revenue retention, churn reason, expansion, renewal intent.
- **Economics:** ARR, gross margin, implementation/support contribution, CAC payback, burn multiple, runway.
- **Transactions:** completed count/GMV, cycle time, blocker aging, document/compliance error, commission accuracy.
- **Network:** fresh supply, qualified demand, response, close, leakage, attributable GMV, net take/contribution.
- **Franchise:** office GCI/profit/payback, launch cost, royalty collection, support load, quality score.
- **Risk:** security/privacy incidents, access violations, override rate, complaints, legal actions, vendor failures.

## Kill/pivot rules

### BluePrint/venture thesis

By five pilots: if fewer than three paid, weekly use <40%, pain not top-three for ≥40%, or implementation cannot trend below 40 hours, pivot ICP/value/scope. By month 18: if external retention/channel and recurring economics are not repeatable, do not raise a scale round on the same story; operate profitably, narrow, sell services/franchise, or wind down software expansion.

### Franchising

Freeze new sales if two reference offices do not show credible incremental profit/payback, support exceeds capacity, openings repeatedly miss standards, or local legal/brand protection is incomplete. Never compensate by selling territories faster.

### VAULTED

Pause the wedge if supply/demand minimums are not met, no attributable close occurs, legal model remains unclear, data is stale, leakage prevents economics, or direct labor makes contribution negative.

### Funding

If a gate fails, preserve at least six months of pivot runway. Do not use optimistic later funding, future entry fees, or uncontracted marketplace revenue to avoid the decision.

## Investment upgrade threshold

Move from conditional pilot to investable growth case only with paid external retention, non-founder acquisition, repeatable onboarding, security/privacy validation, clean IP/cap table, actual franchisee economics, completed attributable VAULTED transactions or explicit pause, and a reconciled model based on cohorts.
