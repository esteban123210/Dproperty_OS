# Roadmap and Validation Experiments

## Days 0–30: problem and commercial proof

- 20 qualified interviews across independent agencies, franchise candidates and developers.
- Five external design partners, at least three outside Dproperty.
- Current-state workflow observation and quantified failure/cost baseline.
- Pricing tests for BluePrint Core/Growth, own-brand offer and one developer program.
- Corporate/cap-table/IP/employment inventory.
- Two independent development proposals and counsel scopes.

**Gate:** pain top-three for ≥40% of target interviews; three written paid-conversion commitments; no fatal legal/neutrality blocker.

## Days 31–90: solution proof

- Clickable prototype and golden-workflow acceptance tests.
- Technical architecture, backlog, threat model, data map and vendor selection.
- Implement or concierge-test intake → workspace → evidence → documents → approvals → commission/report.
- Founding partner agreements and onboarding plan.

**Gate:** customers can complete the workflow; price/scope accepted; MVP fixed; IP owner clear; $950k budget and tranche approved.

## Months 4–6: paid production MVP

- Multi-tenant identity/roles/entitlements, Back Office Brain, transaction workspace, documents/tasks/audit, base reports.
- GoHighLevel connector, LMS completion sync and permission-grounded Copilot read/draft.
- Five paying external organizations; first partner/franchise launches.

**Gate:** no critical isolation/security failure; weekly use; implementation/support measured; one real transaction reaches completion.

## Months 7–12: retention and repeatability

- Ten paying external organizations; two validated office launches.
- Compliance/approval and commission engine hardened.
- Standard implementation pack; onboarding calendar <21 days trend.
- One paid developer pilot and Building Blocks cohort.

**Gate:** gross retention >90% for eligible cohort, target-user weekly use >60%, workflow completion >70%, support within capacity, franchisee economics on track.

## Months 13–18: independent channel and narrow network proof

- Base model target ~28.5 external BluePrint customers and ~$247.9k paid ARR at month 18.
- Non-founder channel produces ≥30% qualified pipeline.
- Narrow VAULTED corridor, attributable completed transactions or board pause.
- Three auditable case studies and independent security/privacy review.

**Gate:** repeatable SaaS acquisition/implementation and demonstrated partner/franchise ROI. Otherwise pivot product/ICP, simplify portfolio or preserve profitable operator model.
