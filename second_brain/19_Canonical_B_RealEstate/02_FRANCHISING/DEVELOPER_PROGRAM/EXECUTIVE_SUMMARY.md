# Developer Sales Program — Executive Summary

## Offer

The Developer Sales Program gives property developers a controlled way to publish project/unit truth, authorize agencies, release inventory windows, qualify demand, manage reservations and approvals, coordinate transactions, and see attribution and channel performance. It connects a developer workspace and VAULTED distribution to BluePrint execution.

This is not a generic marketing agency, construction-management product, or promise to sell inventory. The initial wedge is sales operations and channel governance for selected investment-oriented projects.

## Buyer and users

- Buyer: commercial director, project director, or developer owner.
- Users: inventory administrator, internal sales team, approved agencies, transaction coordinator, finance/commission reviewer, and reporting stakeholders.
- End participant: qualified investor/buyer whose access and data are minimized by role and stage.

## Core workflow

1. Developer due diligence and project approval.
2. Structured project, unit, price, availability, document, incentive, and contact records.
3. Versioned inventory feed with freshness owner and SLA.
4. Agency eligibility and distribution window.
5. Qualified interest with source attribution and consent.
6. Inventory validation/allocation or reservation.
7. BluePrint transaction workspace, documents, approvals, milestones, and closing.
8. Commission/fee validation and developer performance report.

## Commercial model to test

Do not publish one universal price before discovery. Test a combination of:

- one-time project setup/data normalization;
- monthly platform fee by active project/unit volume;
- agency/distribution workspace fees;
- separately contracted implementation/integration;
- success fee only where brokerage, referral, tax, consumer, securities/investment, and AML advice permits and attribution is auditable.

No GMV-based forecast should be recognized before a signed contract states trigger, payer, reversals, cancellations, taxes, partner shares, and collection timing.

## Developer business-model canvas

| Block | Design |
|---|---|
| Customer | Developers with fragmented agency channels and changing inventory |
| Pain | Stale availability, uncontrolled documents, duplicate leads, slow approvals, unclear attribution |
| Value | One governed source of project/unit truth and measurable sell-through |
| Channel | Founder/developer relationships, Dproperty, agencies, VAULTED |
| Revenue | Setup + SaaS; validated success economics later |
| Key metrics | Inventory freshness, approved agencies, qualified interests, response SLA, reservations, closes, cycle time, attributable GMV, developer NPS |

## Go/no-go gates

One paid developer design partner; signed data/responsibility matrix; reliable inventory update process; defined off-platform attribution; completed pilot transactions; legal opinion on commercial model; contribution-positive delivery. Pause if data stays stale, developer approvals remain manual/unowned, or leakage makes attribution unreliable.
