# Shared Franchise and Partner Operating Model

## Candidate funnel

Lead → qualification → financial/operating diligence → disclosure/information pack → discovery interviews → territory/market review → unit-economics model → legal review → approval → agreement/payment → onboarding → test transaction → launch → 30/60/90-day stabilization → quarterly business review → renewal/expansion/exit.

## Qualification scorecard

Score leadership, licensing/eligibility, market knowledge, capital, existing pipeline/GCI, sales discipline, reputation, technology adoption, training commitment, data quality, compliance history, and reference checks. Automatic exclusions include misrepresentation, insufficient liquidity, refusal to share economics, sanctions/adverse integrity findings, and expectation of guaranteed returns.

## Onboarding acceptance criteria

- Legal entity, licensing and beneficial owners verified.
- Agreement, data-processing terms, payment, territory/market schedule, and brand rights complete.
- Organization, roles, MFA, entitlements, branding, CRM connector, templates, commission rules, and reports configured.
- Required courses/certifications completed.
- Historical/active data migration reconciled.
- One test transaction passes intake, compliance, documents, approval, close/commission simulation, and reporting.
- Support contacts, SLAs, escalation, security/privacy, and incident route accepted.
- Production activation explicitly approved.

## Support tiers and capacity

Support demand is measured by implementation hours, tickets per office, severity, response/resolution time, product defects, training gaps, and exceptions. Openings stop when forecast support hours exceed staffed capacity or critical backlog breaches SLA. Founder time is included, not treated as free.

## Quality assurance

Monthly file audit, workflow completion, data freshness, document version, approval/override reason, commission calculation, training status, customer complaints, brand compliance, and security events. Remediation uses owner, due date, evidence, retest, and consequence. Repeated failure can restrict inventory, suspend brand use, or trigger termination according to contract.

## Portfolio KPIs

Qualified-to-approved conversion; contract-to-open days; launch hours; first transaction time; active users; weekly workflow completion; collected GCI; office contribution; royalty collection; support cost; retention; territory performance; complaint rate; compliance exceptions; benchmark improvement; founder dependency.
