# Investment Memo

## Recommendation

**Conditional pilot investment; not yet a full-conviction institutional venture investment.** I would consider investing into a staged $950k capitalization only after corporate, cap-table, IP, founder employment, bank/financial, and jurisdictional diligence is satisfactory and the first tranche is tied to the 90-day evidence gate.

## Thesis

Real-estate teams use fragmented front-office, document, spreadsheet, message and developer systems. B_ proposes a system of record after qualification, then layers training, configurable partner/franchise distribution and gated inventory. The strongest wedge is BluePrint's transaction spine. Dproperty supplies a controlled proof environment; white-label and external SaaS test neutrality; developers and VAULTED can add network value later.

## Why now/why this team

The founder appears to have domain access, existing operating artifacts and a credible product architecture. Commodity CRM/LMS/cloud/AI components reduce build time, making workflow and distribution—not infrastructure—the focus. However, team composition, execution history, references, time commitment, compensation/employment and product-building capability still need diligence.

## Economics

Integrated base model: $74.7k to $3.67M recognized revenue from Y1–Y5; EBITDA turns positive in Y4 and reaches $1.47M in Y5; paid software ARR reaches $3.28M. The raise model needs $929.7k including reserves and assumes $950k. The isolated standalone base supports independence from franchising; the 20-unit franchise model supports contribution but not unicorn scale.

## Upside

If B_ earns neutral trust and repeated workflow authority, it can compound recurring software, learning/implementation efficiency, partner distribution, attribution and marketplace economics. A unicorn path requires thousands of organizations, strong retention/expansion, large verified GMV and revenue scaling faster than service headcount.

## Principal risks

Five-startup scope; no PMF/retention evidence; founder/key-person concentration; pricing/model inconsistency; franchise support burden; brand/neutrality conflict; vendor dependency; cross-country legal/tax complexity; VAULTED leakage/regulation; weak evidence for CAC, GCI, payback and take rate; IP/corporate unknowns.

## Terms/conditions precedent concept

Do not invent valuation in the plan. Seek milestone tranches, information rights, board/observer governance, budget approval, related-party controls, IP assignment, founder vesting/time commitment, option-pool/cap-table clarity, reserved matters, and pro-rata rights as appropriate—subject to counsel and negotiation.

## 90-day conditions

Five external design partners; three written paid conversions; two development proposals; scoped MVP and security/data plan; named IP owner; founder employment/EOR quote; counsel workplan; evidence-quality pipeline; approved tranche budget. Failure returns undrawn capital or stops subsequent tranches according to documents.

## What would make this a “yes”

Ten paying external organizations with weekly use and retention trend; two economically healthy reference offices; implementation <21 days and falling hours; non-founder channel; one paid developer pilot; clean security/privacy review; actual CAC/gross margin; and completed attributable VAULTED transactions or disciplined pause.
