## Revenue rules

- Subscription revenue recognized over service period; cash and recognized revenue separated.
- Setup/implementation recognized when the performance obligation is delivered; allocation reviewed under applicable accounting policy.
- Entry fees require documented deliverables and deferral/release schedule.
- Restricted brand/network funds remain segregated and excluded from EBITDA.
- Free software entitlements create no software revenue.
- Transaction/success revenue requires completed trigger, attribution, partner shares, cancellation/refund policy and collectability.
- Pass-through messaging/AI/vendor usage is reported gross or net only after principal/agent analysis.
- Brokerage commissions and platform fees remain separate ledgers/contracts.

## Metrics and definitions

ARR excludes setup, entry, education enrollment, pass-through, restricted funds and uncontracted usage. CAC includes sales payroll, commissions, marketing, tools, allocated founder selling time and failed acquisition spend. Gross margin includes cloud, AI/API, vendor license, messaging, implementation labor, support and partner shares as appropriate. Payback uses gross-margin contribution, not revenue. LTV is not reported externally until cohort retention exists.

## Price validation

Founding discounts are disclosed, time/quantity limited and linked to reference participation—not disguised list-price traction. Test willingness to pay, time to value, quantified ROI, loss reasons, support effort and renewal. Raise prices only after repeatable delivery and two profitable reference offices. If own-brand partners prefer SaaS, change the model instead of forcing a royalty.
