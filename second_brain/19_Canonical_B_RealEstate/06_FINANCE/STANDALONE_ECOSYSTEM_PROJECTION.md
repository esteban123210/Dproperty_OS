# Standalone Ecosystem Projection Summary

**Source:** `Modelo_B_Standalone_Proyecciones_ES_v2_costos_realistas.xlsx`  
**Scope:** BluePrint + BlankCRM + Building Blocks + VAULTED. Excludes franchise fees/royalties and free franchise software.

## Core inputs

BluePrint $300/month + $1,500 onboarding; BlankCRM $249/month + $750 setup; Building Blocks $199 enrollment. Base churn 8% annual, BlankCRM attach 35%, 1.5 paid learning enrollments/customer/year, $300k average VAULTED property, 0.35% effective take rate, $1,000 CAC, and $650k reference initial investment.

## Milestones

- Pessimistic: no operating/cash/investment/$10M EV milestone within 10 years; reference $650k is inadequate by $1.869M.
- Base: operating break-even Y4; cumulative cash Y5; investment recovery Y6; illustrative $10M ARR/EBITDA EV Y6.
- Positive: operating break-even Y3; cumulative cash and investment recovery Y4; $10M EV Y5.

## Unit economics assumptions

Base expected software ARR/customer **$4,645.80**; approximate recurring gross margin **$3,600.40**; CAC **$1,000**; payback **3.33 months**; simplified LTV **$45,005** and LTV/CAC **45×**. LTV equals recurring gross margin divided by churn and excludes expansion/VAULTED. These figures are mechanically attractive and therefore a high-priority validation risk.

## Investor conclusion

The base case can support a valuable independent company, while the downside shows why cost gates and acquisition proof matter. The positive case is venture-like but depends on a strong unproven channel and VAULTED adoption. Do not blend this workbook with the integrated model as if revenues were independent.
