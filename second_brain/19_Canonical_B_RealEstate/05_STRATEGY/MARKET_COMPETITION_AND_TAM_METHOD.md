# Market, Competition, and TAM Method

## Market definition

Initial market: independent real-estate agencies and developer sales teams in selected Panama/Colombia corridors that manage investment/new-development or cross-border transactions and have enough volume/complexity to pay for transaction operations.

Do not claim all global real-estate transactions as TAM. Use bottom-up, auditable counts.

## TAM/SAM/SOM formulas

- **Software TAM:** eligible organizations × realistic annual BluePrint ACV + eligible attached BlankCRM/learning revenue.
- **Franchise/partner TAM:** qualified operator openings × net entry contribution + mature royalty/software contribution.
- **Developer TAM:** addressable active projects × validated annual platform/project economics.
- **VAULTED TAM:** legally eligible, attributable completed GMV × validated net take rate after partner shares, cancellations and direct delivery cost.

For each country record organization/project count, source/date, qualification filters, price, adoption assumption, currency, and exclusions. SOM must be constrained by sales capacity, onboarding capacity, support and capital—not a percentage chosen for presentation.

## Competitive categories

| Category | Strength | Gap B_ targets | B_ risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Horizontal CRM | Acquisition/automation ecosystem | Weak transaction/compliance depth | Customers may accept “good enough” |
| Brokerage back office/transaction management | Established workflows and integrations | Local/cross-border/developer configuration | Incumbents can localize |
| Portals/MLS/listing tools | Supply/audience liquidity | Weak controlled workflow after interest | VAULTED cannot beat broad reach early |
| Franchise networks | Brand/playbook/referrals | Limited neutral software proposition | Strong brands outspend Dproperty |
| LMS/course platforms | Efficient delivery | Not tied to operating outcomes | Building Blocks is easy to copy |
| Manual operations | Flexible/local trust | No audit/scale/analytics | Low apparent cost and change resistance |

## Win strategy

Win a narrow workflow: qualified investment/new-development opportunity to documented close. Integrate CRM and LMS, build jurisdiction/configuration packs, make implementation measurable, and use completed transactions as proof. Avoid feature comparisons that ignore switching, data migration, training and legal trust.

## Evidence required before an investor TAM slide

Official/company-registry counts; active/qualified organization filter; 20–30 customer interviews; paid price evidence; sales-cycle data; onboarding capacity; churn/retention; project/developer pipeline; legally viable take-rate opinion; source links and calculations. Until complete, market size remains a methodology, not a claim.
