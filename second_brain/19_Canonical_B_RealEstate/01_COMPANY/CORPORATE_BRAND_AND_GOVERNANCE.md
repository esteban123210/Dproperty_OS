# Corporate, Brand, and Governance Architecture

## Recommended separation

| Layer | Role | Must be protected from |
|---|---|---|
| B_RealEstate holdco/platform company | IP, product, data architecture, shared team, capital | Brokerage liabilities and restricted funds |
| Software operating company | SaaS contracts, engineering, cloud, data processing, AI providers | Franchise brand fund and transaction commissions |
| B_Franchising entity or division | Franchise/partner contracts, standards, support, fees | Product neutrality conflicts and broker client money |
| Dproperty licensed brokerage entity by jurisdiction | Regulated brokerage, local commissions, agents | Platform data/IP ownership ambiguity |
| Building Blocks learning operation | Course/content agreements, instructor rights, certification | Claims of accredited/regulated status without authority |
| VAULTED operating entity/contracting model | Marketplace/distribution agreements and attribution | Custody, escrow, pooled investments unless separately licensed |

Exact entities, tax residence, IP ownership, intercompany agreements, transfer pricing, and regulated responsibilities require counsel. The principle is separation of liability, clean IP chain of title, arm's-length service agreements, and transparent data roles.

## Brand logic

- **B_RealEstate** is the company and neutral ecosystem.
- **B_Franchising** is the commercial gateway for operating partnerships.
- **Dproperty** is a premium brokerage/franchise brand, not the owner of neutral partner data.
- **BluePrint**, **BlankCRM**, **Building Blocks**, and **VAULTED** are modular products.
- `bfranchising.com` is acquisition/qualification; authenticated work occurs in product workspaces.

## Decision rights

| Decision | Recommends | Approves |
|---|---|---|
| Annual budget/financing | CEO/CFO | Board |
| New country or regulated activity | CEO + counsel | Board |
| Product boundary/pricing | Product/finance | CEO; board if material |
| Data use/cross-tenant benchmark | Data/privacy | CEO + counsel; board for policy |
| AI permission increase | Product/security/privacy | CTO + CEO |
| Franchise territory/exception | Franchise lead | CEO; board for master rights |
| Security incident response | Security lead | Incident commander; board notified |
| Related-party transaction | CFO/counsel | Disinterested board members |

## Minimum board pack

Cash/runway; budget variance; ARR/revenue bridge; logo/revenue retention; pipeline attribution; onboarding hours; support demand/capacity; product reliability/security; franchise cohort economics; VAULTED funnel/GMV; evidence register; top risks; decisions required.
