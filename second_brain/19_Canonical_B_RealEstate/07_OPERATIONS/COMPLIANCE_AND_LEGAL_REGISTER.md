# Compliance and Legal Register

This register identifies workstreams; it is not legal advice.

| Domain | Core question | Owner | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Corporate/cap table | Who owns entities/equity; authority; related parties? | CEO/counsel | Financing |
| IP | Who owns code, content, brand, templates, data rights? | Counsel/CTO | Vendor/customer contracts |
| SaaS/data | Controller/processor roles, DPA, subprocessors, transfers, retention, security? | Privacy/security | Production |
| Franchise | Classification, disclosure, earnings claims, territory, fees, termination, renewal? | Franchise counsel | Offer/sale by country |
| Brokerage | Entity/person licensing, referral/commission rules, advertising and supervision? | Local brokerage counsel | Transactions |
| AML/KYC | Subject-obliged status, CDD/beneficial owner, reporting, records, training? | Compliance/counsel | Customer/transaction launch |
| Marketplace/investment | Listing/allocation/referral vs regulated activity; advice/securities/crowdfunding? | Specialist counsel | VAULTED production |
| Consumer | Marketing claims, cancellation/refund, unfair terms, complaints? | Counsel/ops | Public offer |
| Tax/accounting | VAT/ITBMS, withholding, PE, revenue allocation, intercompany, entry fee recognition? | Tax/CFO | Contract/model |
| Labor/contractor | Employer jurisdiction, benefits, IP/confidentiality, contractor classification? | HR/counsel | Hiring |
| Payments | Who receives funds; safeguarding, processor, chargebacks? | CFO/counsel | Any money flow |
| AI | Data use, automated decisions, notices, vendor terms, human review? | Privacy/product | AI feature |
| Education | Certification claims, instructor/content rights, accreditation implications? | Learning/counsel | Paid program |
| Insurance | Cyber, E&O/professional, D&O, crime, general liability | CFO/broker | Scale |

## Jurisdiction pack minimum

Approved source/date; legal owner; applicability decision; required licenses/registrations; customer disclosures; contract clauses; workflow/checklist; evidence and retention; reporting/escalation; prohibited claims/actions; review/expiry. Copilot uses only approved current packs and cites them.

## Immediate counsel questions

Panama and Colombia entity/data roles; franchise/operating-partner classification; collected-GCI royalty definition; developer success fees; referral chains; VAULTED access and qualification; AML obligations; cross-border data transfers; trademark strategy; cancellation/refunds; restricted funds; independent-contractor agents; tax allocation among software, services, education and franchise rights.
