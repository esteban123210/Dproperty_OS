# Reporting and Management Cadence

## Weekly operating review

Pipeline by source/stage; design partners; paid conversions; implementation plan/hours; active usage; transaction blockers; support/security incidents; developer/inventory freshness; hiring/vendor blockers; decisions due. End with owners and dates.

## Monthly business review

Budget vs actual; cash/runway and 13-week forecast; ARR/revenue/deferred/restricted funds; customer/franchise cohorts; churn/retention; CAC/payback; gross margin; product reliability; support capacity; onboarding hours; VAULTED funnel/GMV; compliance register; evidence gaps; roadmap gates.

## Quarterly board review

Strategy and risk; financing/capital allocation; product/market evidence; annualized revenue/retention; portfolio contribution; security/privacy; legal expansion; hiring/compensation; related parties; cap table; next-quarter gates; kill/pivot decisions.

## Decision discipline

Every review distinguishes actual, model, target and forecast. Variance >10%, runway change >2 months, material scope expansion, new regulated activity, cross-tenant data use, or missed gate creates a decision record. Decisions include owner, evidence, alternatives, effective date, downstream updates and reversal criteria.

## Metric owners

Finance owns cash/revenue definitions; product owns adoption/workflow; sales owns pipeline attribution; customer success owns activation/retention/support; franchise lead owns office economics/quality; developer lead owns supply/freshness; security/privacy owns incidents/access; learning owns completion/competence; CEO resolves cross-functional metric conflict.
