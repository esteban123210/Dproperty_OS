## Design Principles (Keep This In Mind)

1. **Consistency:** Every design must feel like it's part of the same brand. Use the same fonts, colors, spacing rules across all materials.
2. **Professionalism:** This is the first franchisee's first impression. Make it look premium, not DIY.
3. **Usability:** Templates must be easy for franchisees to use. If they're hard to edit, they won't use them.
4. **Narrative:** Your designs should tell the story of premium, scalable, professional franchise. Not casual. Not corporate-sterile. Premium.
5. **Speed over perfection:** You have 11 weeks. Good-enough-great is better than perfect-and-late.
