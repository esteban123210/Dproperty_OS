# Dproperty Select — sourcing network growth projections

## Logic and ownership

HQ owns and curates the Select inventory and developer relationships/contracts; branded Dproperty franchises originate vetted projects and sell units network-wide. For each collected eligible **distributable** Select commission: **30% HQ**, **20% registered project originator**, **50% selling partner(s)**. Same partner source + sale = 70% partner / 30% HQ. HQ source + partner sale = 50% HQ / 50% seller. Signed contracts and legal restrictions override modeled allocations. This is distinct from B_ platform revenue and not interchangeable with VAULTED marketplace take rate.

## Cohort conversion and ramp controls [H]

Use by project-vintage and by originator franchise: (1) active franchises; (2) proposals per franchise; (3) HQ Select accepted projects (not just introductions); (4) unique projects with signed commission agreements; (5) eligible salable unit inventory; (6) buyer registrations; (7) reservation→contract→completion conversion; (8) unit closings by quarter; (9) developer commission invoiced and received; (10) refunds/clawbacks and gross-to-net deductions; (11) cash distributed to originator and sellers; (12) HQ direct servicing and acquisition costs.

Scenario stress examples, using mature annual source pipeline **2.5 projects per franchise**, assuming 40 units is *potential* rather than guaranteed sold:
- 25% unit realization: HQ $112,500 per franchise/cohort-equivalent;
- 50% realization: HQ $225,000;
- 75% realization: HQ $337,500;
- 100% realization: HQ $450,000.

**Timing:** project sourcing in year t does not imply closing/payment in year t; apply actual sell-through across t/t+1/t+2 and potentially longer, and recognize commissions only when earned and collectible according to contracts and accounting. No automatic 100% realization.

## Reconciliation to existing plan

The canonical 60-month `B_RealEstate_Launch_Budget_and_Projections.xlsx` continues as the existing financed base case until its formulas are updated and tested. This **does not** silently replace prior Y1–Y5 revenue/EBITDA or historical assumptions.

Legacy Select payout in `05 - Commission Split.md` was 2.5% sale price for branded originator, 1.5% for B_ Partner; modeled new 30/20/50 is a proposed economic replacement for eligible Dproperty Select network transactions, not additive. Do not claim HQ receives both legacy retained commission and the 30% new allocation, or another 6% ordinary royalty on the same Select distribution by default.

**Commercial risk:** On one $300k / 5% unit, HQ new share $4.5k vs legacy retained $7.5k on branded sales (a $3k decrease), which can be offset only if total network completed sales increase enough. Holding all else equal, HQ must generate **1.67×** as many eligible sales to maintain the old $7.5k per-unit contribution before cost differences. New split offers growth incentives but is not unconditionally superior economically.

**Open decisions:** eligibility and termination of 20% source rights; treatment of HQ-created projects; external agents and B_ Partner splits; territory and cross-border referral law; allocation of paid marketing costs; multi-originator disputes; project cancellations; attribution and payout escrow/timing; developer margins. Run market pilots before treating 2–3 projects × 40 sold units as repeatable.
