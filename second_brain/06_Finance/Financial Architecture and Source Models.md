# Financial Architecture and Source Models

## Model hierarchy

1. **Integrated launch model:** `B_RealEstate_Launch_Budget_and_Projections.xlsx` — canonical 60-month capitalization, revenue, team/cost, cash flow, franchise economics, pricing and diligence review.
2. **Franchise isolation:** `Modelo_B_Franchising_20_Franquicias_ES_v3_costos_realistas.xlsx` — tests a capped 20-office franchising front over 10 years.
3. **Standalone ecosystem isolation:** `Modelo_B_Standalone_Proyecciones_ES_v2_costos_realistas.xlsx` — tests BluePrint + BlankCRM + Building Blocks + VAULTED without franchise fees/royalties.
4. **Business plan venture case:** strategic upside hypothesis only; not the operating budget.

When models conflict, use the integrated model for the current raise and portfolio pricing; use isolation models to understand business-line economics. Reconcile rather than add their revenues, because shared customers and costs can overlap.

## Scenario discipline

All projections are management scenarios, not forecasts or history. Each investor-facing number needs file, sheet, scenario, period and definition. No free franchise software is recognized as software revenue. Restricted brand/network funds are excluded from revenue/EBITDA available to shareholders. Entry cash, deferred revenue, revenue recognition, and cash flow are separate schedules.

The workbook's review reports zero or immaterial differences for revenue components, entry cash/release/deferral, EBITDA/working-capital cash flow, opening/closing cash, marketing fund, LMS prepaid expense, license allocation, support capacity, license horizon and use of funds. ## Architecture cost allocation — 2026-09-29 [D]

BlankCRM carries configuration/support and third-party costs for the full commercial lifecycle, including legal/approval/signature/payment/commission integrations. BluePrint carries evidence ingestion, financial reconciliation, CRM/process oversight, management governance and executive AI costs. Existing prices, rates, forecasts and formulas are unchanged assumptions, not validation of the revised scope. Requote both delivery scopes before committing budgets; do not fund a BluePrint transaction-execution engine or require BluePrint for standalone BlankCRM.
