# Funding Plan

## Recommended Structure

This should be positioned as an internal venture budget funded by the existing Dproperty owners.

## Baseline Budget

Headline frame: **$650,000 over 24 months**. Actual 24-month cost base including the
Miguel salary placeholder is **~$734,000** (see Business Plan §15). The model (v0.6)
shows cumulative EBITDA is negative through Year 2 (~-$358k Y1, ~-$192k Y2), so the
build-phase funding requirement is roughly this cost base net of early revenue — around
**$550k–$650k of net cash need across Years 1–2** before the business self-funds from
Year 3. Confirm against `Dproperty_OS_Financial_Model.xlsx v0.6`.

## Tranche 1 — $350,000

Purpose:

- Build MVP.
- Draft legal structure.
- Create franchise manuals.
- Create onboarding package.
- Build pitch materials.
- Run pilots.
- Pay venture lead salary.
- Build CRM/software foundations.

## Tranche 2 — $300,000

Released after milestones:

- MVP live.
- 2 white-label pilots signed.
- 1 developer pilot signed.
- Franchise package ready.
- First franchise pipeline active.
- Legal agreements reviewed.

## Why Not External Seed First?

The existing owners already bring capital, brand, relationships, and business context. External investors may complicate governance too early.

## Architecture cost allocation — 2026-09-29 [D]

BlankCRM carries configuration/support and third-party costs for the full commercial lifecycle, including legal/approval/signature/payment/commission integrations. BluePrint carries evidence ingestion, financial reconciliation, CRM/process oversight, management governance and executive AI costs. Existing prices, rates, forecasts and formulas are unchanged assumptions, not validation of the revised scope. Requote both delivery scopes before committing budgets; do not fund a BluePrint transaction-execution engine or require BluePrint for standalone BlankCRM.
