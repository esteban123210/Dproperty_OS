# Developer Sales OS Economics

## Example Project

- 50 units.
- $300k average price.
- Total sales value: $15M.

## Fee Scenarios

Developer-team sales at 0.5%:

- 50 units all developer-team: $75k Dproperty revenue.

Mixed scenario:

- 40 developer-team sales at 0.5% = $60k.
- 10 Dproperty-sourced sales at 2.5% = $75k.
- Total = $135k.

Dproperty-sourced all sales at 2.5%:

- $375k.

## Interpretation

Developer Sales OS can create meaningful upside even before franchising scales.

## Collection Trigger (OPEN, added 2026-07-02)

Fees are priced on gross sales value, but the collection trigger is 
undefined. If developers pay on buyer payment plans, the 0.5%/2.5% 
fees inherit the same deferral problem as commissions. Proposed: fees 
invoice on developer's receipt of buyer down payment reaching a defined 
threshold, with the monthly managed-desk fee ($3-5k) as the cash-flow 
bridge. Needs decision before first developer agreement.
