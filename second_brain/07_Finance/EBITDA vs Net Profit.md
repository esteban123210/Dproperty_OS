# EBITDA vs Net Profit

## EBITDA

EBITDA shows operational profitability before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization.

It is useful for showing whether the business model works operationally.

## Net Profit

Net profit is closer to what remains after accounting costs and taxes.

## Distributable Cash

Distributable cash is not the same as EBITDA or net profit. It depends on:

- Taxes.
- Reinvestment.
- Working capital.
- Product upgrades.
- Cash reserves.
- Debt or financing obligations.

## Year 5 Example

- EBITDA: about $601k.
- Estimated net profit: about $395k.
- Potential distributable cash: $250k–$400k.
