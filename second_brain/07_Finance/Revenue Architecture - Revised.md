# B_RealEstate Revenue Architecture — Revised v2.0

## Six Revenue Streams

B_RealEstate generates revenue through six distinct, non-mutually-exclusive streams:

### 1. BluePrint Subscriptions (Foundation)

**Model:** Recurring SaaS subscriptions, tiered by organization size.

**Pricing:**
- Solo: $99–149/month
- Growth: $299–499/month
- Pro: $750–1,500/month
- Enterprise: custom

**Unit Economics:**
- Gross margin: 85%+ (software only, minimal support)
- CAC: $200–500 (warm leads, very low)
- LTV: $3,500–8,000+ (18–36 month retention minimum)
- Payback: 1–2 months

**Revenue Projection:**

| Year | Organizations | Avg Price | ARR |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 50 | $300 | $180k |
| 2 | 180 | $350 | $630k |
| 3 | 450 | $380 | $1.71M |
| 4 | 1,000 | $420 | $5.04M |
| 5 | 1,800 | $450 | $8.1M |

**Key driver:** Adoption through software pricing (not franchise partnerships).

---

### 2. Copilot & AI Usage (Expansion)

**Model:** Included allowance + overage pricing.

**How it works:**
- Included: 100–500 Copilot queries/month (tier-dependent)
- Overage: $0.10–0.50 per query above allowance
- Premium AI features: Unlocked at Pro/Enterprise tiers

**Revenue Projection:**

| Year | Active Users | Avg Copilot Revenue | Annual |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 150 | $5/mo | $9k |
| 2 | 540 | $15/mo | $97k |
| 3 | 1,350 | $25/mo | $405k |
| 4 | 3,000 | $35/mo | $1.26M |
| 5 | 5,400 | $45/mo | $2.43M |

**Key driver:** More organizations = more Copilot power users = higher usage fees.

---

### 3. VAULTED Transactions (Network)

**Model:** Marketplace commission on deal value.

**How it works:**
- VAULTED facilitates off-market deal.
- Developer lists $400k property.
- Agency closes deal.
- Developer pays 5% commission = $20,000.
- B_ captures 0.75% of deal value = $3,000.
- Agency gets remainder ($17k).

**Revenue Projection:**

| Year | Annual VAULTED GMV | Transaction Count | Commission % | Annual Revenue |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | $75M | 250 | 0.75% | $562k |
| 2 | $250M | 900 | 0.75% | $1.875M |
| 3 | $500M | 1,800 | 0.75% | $3.75M |
| 4 | $900M | 3,200 | 0.75% | $6.75M |
| 5 | $1.5B | 5,000 | 0.75% | $11.25M |

**Key driver:** Network adoption (more agencies + developers = more inventory = more liquidity = more transactions).

---

### 4. Developer Programs (Supply Integration)

**Model:** Monthly platform fee + transaction-based distribution fee.

**How it works:**

| Fee Type | Amount | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Platform fee | $1k–5k/month | Transaction infrastructure, reporting, integration |
| Distribution fee | % of deals originated through B_ network | Success-based; developer only pays if using the network |
| Optional services | Separate pricing | Content, training, market analysis |

**Developer Project Economics (Per Project, 12-18 months):**

Assume developer project: $15M value (50 units × $300k avg).

| Scenario | Team Sales | Network Sales | Monthly Fee | Annual Revenue |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Conservative | 35 units @ 0.5% | 15 units @ 2.5% | $3k/mo | $525k + $180k = $705k |
| Moderate | 30 units @ 0.5% | 20 units @ 2.5% | $4k/mo | $450k + $300k = $750k |
| Aggressive | 25 units @ 0.5% | 25 units @ 2.5% | $5k/mo | $375k + $375k = $750k |

**Revenue Projection (Concurrent Projects):**

| Year | Active Projects | Avg Revenue/Project | Annual |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | $700k | $700k |
| 2 | 3 | $700k | $2.1M |
| 3 | 6 | $720k | $4.32M |
| 4 | 10 | $750k | $7.5M |
| 5 | 15 | $800k | $12M |

(Note: Projects are 12-18 months, so "active" means overlapping project cycles.)

### 5. Franchise Royalties (Vertical Integration)

**Model:** Launch fee (one-time) + recurring platform fees + variable royalties.

**Franchisee Economics:**

Assume franchisee: 50 units/year @ $350k avg.

| Component | Calculation | Annual |

| Platform fee | $2,500 × 12 | $30,000 |
| Select deals (3/yr @ 2.5%) | 3 × $350k × 2.5% | $26,250 |
| **Total HQ Revenue** | — | **$62,812** |

## Investment Thesis

**To reach $1M:** $400–500k capital.
**To reach $5M:** $800k–1.2M cumulative capital (mostly reinvested).
**To reach $10M+:** Likely self-funded from Y3 forward (60%+ EBITDA margins).

**ROI for investors:**
- $500k seed at Year 1 ($1M revenue) → $15–20M valuation (15–20x EBITDA multiple) = $7.5–10M company value.
- $1M Series A at Year 2 ($5M revenue) → $30–50M valuation = 15–25x EBITDA.
- $10M Series B at Year 4 ($21M revenue) → $100–200M valuation = 5–10x revenue.

**This is a compelling venture return profile.**

## Sensitivity Analysis

**Key assumption: BluePrint adoption rate**

If BluePrint grows 2x faster:
- Year 5 BluePrint revenue: $16.2M (instead of $8.1M)
- Year 5 total revenue: $42M (instead of $34M)
- EBITDA: $28M+ (instead of $22M)

**Key assumption: VAULTED transaction volume**

If VAULTED GMV grows 1.5x slower:
- Year 5 VAULTED revenue: $7.5M (instead of $11.25M)
- Year 5 total revenue: $29M (instead of $34M)
- Still highly profitable

**Key assumption: Developer project success**

If developer projects have longer cycles or lower success:
- Year 5 developer revenue: $8M (instead of $12M)
- Year 5 total revenue: $30M (instead of $34M)
- Model still works

**Conclusion:** The model is robust to slower adoption in any single stream. This is why diversified revenue is strategically valuable.
