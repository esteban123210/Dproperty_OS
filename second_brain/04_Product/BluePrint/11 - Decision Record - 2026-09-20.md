# Decision Record — 2026-09-20

## Decision

Retire the prior concept of BluePrint as the operational transaction spine owning properties/projects/units, transaction execution, compliance and commission workflows.

BluePrint is redefined as the **chat-first management operating system / management-control layer for sales-led real-estate SMEs**.

## Why

1. GHL and other CRMs should remain front-office systems.
2. Specialist accounting software should remain the financial ledger.
3. Rebuilding CRM or ERP functionality creates unnecessary scope and weak differentiation.
4. The strongest observed pain is management reliability: data fragmentation, founder time, weak process control, unreliable reporting, recurring operational failures and back-office skill gaps.
5. The desired user is often a non-expert administrator who needs the system to impose standards automatically.
6. The product must be valuable even when the customer uses a strong CRM.

## Core hypothesis

Customers will pay for a system that knows how a real-estate agency should be managed, reconciles reality across systems, converts natural language into governed structured work, and lets a small administrative team operate at a higher institutional standard.

## Consequence

B_Franchising becomes a distribution/customer channel for BluePrint, not the product definition. BluePrint must remain independently sellable and CRM-agnostic.
