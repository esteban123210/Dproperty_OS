# ICP and Jobs To Be Done

## Beachhead ICP

A **sales-led independent real-estate agency with roughly 5–50 people** that has outgrown founder-led administration but is not large enough—or does not want—to hire a full COO/CFO/operations layer.

Typical reality:
- founders/principals generate revenue and relationships;
- CRM may exist, but management information is unreliable or fragmented;
- accounting exists separately;
- documents live in Drive/SharePoint;
- operating knowledge lives in WhatsApp, spreadsheets and people’s heads;
- one secretary/admin person becomes the de facto coordinator;
- founders spend too much time checking, correcting, chasing and reporting.

## Economic job

> “Let me hire one strong administrator and use BluePrint to run the back office instead of hiring several expensive managers or spending my own selling time doing management.”

## Buyer

Owner, managing broker, CEO, principal or partner.

## Primary operator

Administrator / executive assistant / office coordinator.

## Secondary users

- finance/accountant: verification and finance views;
- department manager: process, incidents and actions;
- owner/CEO: health, reports, approvals, decisions.

Sales agents generally remain in the CRM and do not need full BluePrint seats.

## Jobs to be done

### Owner
- know what is actually true, not only what the CRM says;
- know cash, verified revenue, receivables, budget variance and risks;
- identify recurrent operational failures;
- prepare management/board reports without spreadsheet archaeology;
- reduce hours spent coordinating administrative work.

### Administrator
- upload/find/file documents by asking;
- record expenses/incidents/tasks without knowing taxonomy;
- prepare reports through conversation;
- know what is missing and who must act;
- follow standard processes without memorizing the standards.

### Finance
- reconcile management view to accounting source;
- validate financial events;
- control exceptions and approvals;
- close periods and preserve historical truth.

## Poor-fit customers initially

- solo agents with very low operational complexity;
- large enterprises already deeply implemented on enterprise ERP/BPM suites;
- property-management businesses needing tenant/maintenance operations;
- customers primarily seeking CRM/marketing software.
