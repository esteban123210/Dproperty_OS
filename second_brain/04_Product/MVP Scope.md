# MVP Scope

> **Naming and scope update (2026-08-16):** The platform is **BluePrint**, part of **B_RealEstate**. GoHighLevel remains the CRM, Open edX the Academy technology, and VAULTED the off-market marketplace. The canonical boundaries live in [[../18_Ecosystem/00 - Ecosystem Master Map]] and [[../18_Ecosystem/12 - System of Record and Integration Matrix]].

## MVP Must Do

- Track clients.
- Track brokers.
- Track projects.
- Track units.
- Track deals.
- Generate or index documents.
- Support basic projections.
- Track commissions.
- Support Private Collection workflow.
- Provide franchise dashboard.
- Provide HQ dashboard.
- Track training completion.
- Provide reporting.

## MVP Can Be Manual/Semi-Manual

- Legal review.
- Final document signing.
- Advanced AI.
- Deep integrations.
- Automated payments.
- Client portal.
- Multi-language polish.

## MVP Success Criteria

The first franchise can run for 90 days without relying on scattered documents and WhatsApp memory.
