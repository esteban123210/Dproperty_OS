## Naming

Parent **B_RealEstate / B_** · platform **BluePrint** (wordmark: B_ + underlined "luePrint") · front office **BlankCRM**, powered by GoHighLevel · network **VAULTED** · enablement **Building Blocks**, powered by Open edX · flagship **Dproperty** · curated inventory **Dproperty Select** · own-brand channel **B_ Partner**.

## Public claim rules — binding

- Say **"BlankCRM is powered by GoHighLevel."** Never "CRM propio" or proprietary CRM.
- **Never** claim "one shared database" / "una sola base de datos." Say: *una identidad, una operación conectada y fuentes de verdad definidas.*
- **Never** imply VAULTED liquidity or GMV that is not proven, or that inventory/commission/availability are live in real time.
- **Never** claim BluePrint is full accounting or ERP.
- Dproperty Select is **HQ-curated**; franchisees may propose, never approve. Do not imply broad network availability.
- **DpropertyLiving is parked** — remove from all active navigation and economics.
- Physical hub is **not** a near-term headline promise.
- Attribute +$200M / +700 operations explicitly to **Dproperty** wherever used.
- Reframe dated country-expansion claims as directional unless formally approved and resourced.
- Add legal pages: privacy, terms, cookies, investment/franchise disclaimers.

## Current state

`bfranchising.com` is strategically outdated. **Preserve the visual system** — rewrite the information architecture and claims first. The site must lead with the product story (BluePrint · BlankCRM · VAULTED, supported by Academy), with the four channels presented as ways to adopt.
