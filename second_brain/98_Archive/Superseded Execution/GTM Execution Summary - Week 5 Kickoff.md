## 💡 Key Principles to Keep in Mind

### 1. Agents Seeking Independence First

They have the highest close probability (50%+) and fastest decision timeline (4-6 weeks). If you close 1-2 of them by Sept 15, you've won. Everything else (sales pros, youth) is bonus or Phase 2.

### 2. Minimal Budget = Maximum Authenticity

€2-5k budget means you're lean. That forces you to use your personal network, organic outreach, and authentic storytelling. That's actually your competitive advantage. No fancy ads = real humans talking to real humans.

### 3. Quality Over Volume

Don't chase 100 low-quality leads. Focus on 50-60 high-quality leads (agents with existing pipeline, sales pros with proven track record). One good franchisee is worth 10 leads that stall.

### 4. Three Markets, One Lead

You don't need to succeed equally in all three markets. If Panama delivers 1 franchisee + Bogotá delivers 1 franchisee, you've hit your Sept 15 goal. Medellín can be bonus/Phase 2.

### 5. Use the Playbooks, Don't Improvise

You have discovery call scripts, objection responses, and messaging frameworks. Use them word-for-word in Week 6. By Week 7-8, you'll know what's working and can adapt. But Week 6 is about testing the playbook.

By the middle of Week 10, you'll know if Sept 15 is achievable:

❌ **Cold status:** <5 LOIs issued, 0 franchises in process, pivot to Phase 2 recruiting (Oct+)

**If Hot or Warm:** Keep pushing; you'll make it.  
**If Cold:** Don't panic. Refocus on Agents Seeking Independence; test different messaging.
