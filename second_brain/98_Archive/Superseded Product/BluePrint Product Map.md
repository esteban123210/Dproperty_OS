## Current product definition

BluePrint is a **chat-first AI management operating system for small and growing real-estate agencies**. It sits above CRM, accounting and document systems to create verified management truth, process assurance, financial/operational control and management execution.

## Retired scope

BluePrint no longer owns property/project/unit inventory, CRM, generic sales pipeline, end-to-end transaction execution, full accounting, LMS or marketplace functions.
