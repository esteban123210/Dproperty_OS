## 2. Decisions Made (full text in Decision Log)
1. **Rename:** Private Collection → **Dproperty Select** (commercial name). Folders/filenames not yet renamed (would break wikilinks) — content updated.
2. **Franchise pricing restructure:** Launch fee **$30k founding / $40k after 5 successful franchises** (was $15k). Royalty 6% + **Network & Brand Fund** 1.5% (renamed). Dproperty Select moved from 50/50 gross split to a **fixed % of sale price** (2.5% branded / 2.0% white-label). Select modelled at 5%; upside above 5% kept by HQ.
3. **Branded vs white-label differentiation:** branded carries investment priority + higher Select payout. Rule: *the Dproperty brand goes where there is investment potential; the system can go anywhere.*
4. **Commission waterfall + royalty base:** 5% charged → 50/50 external-advisor co-broke → **2.5% into company** → 35% seller / 10% director / 55% franchise net. **Royalty charged on gross-into-company (2.5%), not net** (un-gameable). HQ ~$562 per $300k local unit.
