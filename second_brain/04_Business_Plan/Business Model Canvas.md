## Economic flywheel

Operating customers create structured workflows and demand; Building Blocks improves activation; Dproperty and partners supply controlled cohorts; developers add inventory; VAULTED adds attributable opportunities; BluePrint captures execution; verified outcomes improve templates, reporting, and acquisition. Each step must work independently before claiming a network effect.

## Non-negotiable tests

1. BluePrint customers pay without requiring a franchise.
2. Retention and workflow use do not depend on founder intervention.
3. Implementation hours fall by cohort.
4. Franchisee incremental profit covers fees and produces acceptable payback.
5. VAULTED closes remain attributable and contribution-positive after partner shares and labor.
6. Data and neutrality commitments make non-Dproperty partners comfortable.
