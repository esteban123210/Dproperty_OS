## 3. Key Strategic Findings

- JV framing surfaced: "bringing the companies together" means the venture is
  [Likely] a joint venture between Dproperty and Simon's entity, not a simple
  spin-out. Slide 12 (contributions per party) is BLOCKED until defined.
- Commission-rate conflict (validation item #1): franchise model assumes 5%
  local GCI; company handoff documents 0.75% deferred advisory rate. Sensitivity
  shows the venture survives even at 0.75% (Y5 revenue $1.19M vs $1.41M) because
  ~76% of revenue is rate-independent.
- 24-month cost base with Miguel placeholder is ~$734k vs the $650k frame.
  Deck slide 11 discloses this and frames tranche gating as the owners'
  downside cap.
- Model outputs (conservative, collected basis): Y1 $129k, Y3 $673k, Y5 $1.41M
  revenue; EBITDA positive Year 3 (~$324k); Y5 EBITDA presented as a range
  ($600k-$1.0M) because the Y3-5 staffing plan does not exist yet.
- Presentation language (ES/EN) is an open decision; deck content drafted with
  Spanish on-slide copy and English speaker notes.
- Brand palette contradiction unresolved: company handoff defines a 2026 palette
  (off-white/graphite/matte gold, Playfair + Montserrat); Esteban stated no
  visual identity exists. Design is blocked until answered.
