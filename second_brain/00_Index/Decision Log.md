# Decision Log

This note records decisions that should not be re-opened accidentally in future chats.

## 2026-07-01 — Obsidian as Source of Truth

**Decision:** Obsidian will be the project brain and long-term memory.  
**Reason:** AI chat memory is not reliable enough for a large multi-document project.  
**Implication:** Every meaningful deliverable, decision, assumption, and open question should be captured in Obsidian or the Deliverables Tracker.

## 2026-07-01 — Obsidian Is Not the Asset Warehouse

**Decision:** Heavy/final assets should live in SharePoint/Drive/Figma/Canva/PowerPoint/Excel, while Obsidian holds context and links.  
**Reason:** Obsidian is best for Markdown knowledge, not large videos, brand packs, signed contracts, or final design files.  
**Implication:** Each major asset needs an Obsidian index note with file links and status.

## 2026-07-01 — Three Business Lines

**Decision:** Dproperty OS will be structured around three business lines:
1. Dproperty branded franchise.
2. White-label OS for boutique agencies.
3. Developer Sales OS.

**Reason:** This creates multiple revenue channels and uses Dproperty's capabilities beyond only franchising.

## 2026-07-01 — Private Collection Must Be HQ-Controlled

**Decision:** Dproperty Private Collection remains controlled by HQ.  
**Reason:** It includes sensitive, curated, premium, and investor-oriented inventory where quality control matters.  
**Implication:** Franchisees may access it under rules, but not independently modify materials, negotiate directly, or create unapproved projections.

## 2026-07-01 — Private Collection Commission Baseline

**Decision:** Private Collection baseline economics: 50/50 gross commission split between HQ and franchise.  
**Additional Rule:** Franchise pays reduced royalty/network fee on its half.  
**Reason:** HQ brings and controls the inventory/process; the franchise brings client access or origination.

## 2026-07-01 — White-label Does Not Automatically Receive Private Collection

**Decision:** White-label clients do not automatically get Private Collection access.  
**Reason:** White-label keeps its own brand and should not receive the same strategic inventory advantage as Dproperty-branded franchises.

## 2026-07-01 — Developer Sales OS Pricing

**Decision:** Developer Sales OS should not be priced only as 10% of salesperson payout.  
**Reason:** If developer salesperson payout is low, Dproperty upside collapses.  
**Preferred Logic:** Price based on gross sales value:
- 0.5% on developer-team sales when Dproperty trains/manages the system.
- 2.5%–3% on Dproperty/network-sourced sales.
- Optional $3k–$5k/month managed desk fee.

## 2026-07-01 — Conservative 5-Year Target

**Decision:** Conservative five-year target:
- 5 branded franchises.
- 20 white-label agencies.
- 15 developer partnerships/projects.

**Reason:** This is more credible for an internal Dproperty owner pitch than an overly aggressive venture-scale forecast.

## 2026-07-01 — Internal Venture Funding

**Decision:** Funding should be framed as internal venture budget from existing Dproperty owners, not external seed round.  
**Baseline Amount:** Approximately $650k over 24 months in tranches.  
**Reason:** The existing owners bring brand, capital, assets, and relationships.

## 2026-07-01 — Esteban's Role and Compensation

**Decision:** Esteban should be positioned as Co-founder & Venture Lead / Product & Strategy Lead.  
**Baseline Ask:** At least €5,000/month salary, plus 10%–15% equity in NewCo.  
**Reason:** The role requires building the venture, product, manuals, pitch, strategy, operations, and execution infrastructure.

## 2026-07-01 — First Franchise Target User

**Decision:** The first franchise package should be built for a single-agent owner-operator with money/network but limited real estate operating experience.  
**Reason:** This forces the package to be complete, practical, and easy to follow.

## 2026-07-01 — GoHighLevel Role

**Decision:** GoHighLevel is a CRM/marketing automation layer, not the core Dproperty OS.  
**Reason:** It handles leads and communication, but not deep project/deal/legal/document/projection/franchise logic.

## 2026-07-01 — Data First, Documents Second

**Decision:** In Dproperty OS, structured data should drive documents, dashboards, projections, and reporting.  
**Reason:** This prevents scattered files and creates a scalable operating model.
