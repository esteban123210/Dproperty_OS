# B_RealEstate — Current Context Brief

## 1. Company

B_RealEstate / B_ is being built as a **product-led operating-infrastructure company for real-estate businesses**, not primarily as a franchise company.

## 2. Core products

### BluePrint
Proprietary, chat-first AI management OS for small/growing sales-led agencies. Goal: one capable administrator + BluePrint + specialist systems instead of premature COO/CFO/operations headcount.

### BlankCRM
Sellable configured front-office product powered by GoHighLevel. Leads, communications, marketing, follow-up, calendars and sales pipeline. Not core proprietary IP.

### VAULTED
Private marketplace/network. Owns listings/opportunities, access, matches, introductions, attribution, GMV and transaction fees. Potential network effect.

### Academy
Enablement/standards layer powered by Open edX; primarily bundled.

## 3. Channels

Direct SaaS; B_ Partner (managed own-brand package); Dproperty Franchise (branded full-stack); Developer Partnerships (revenue/supply/distribution).

## 4. Strategic assets

Dproperty brand/testbed; Dproperty Select curated opportunities; developer/broker/investor relationships; process library/Second Brain.

## 5. Product boundaries

CRM/front office ≠ BluePrint. Accounting remains ledger. Drive/legal archive owns binaries. VAULTED owns marketplace. BluePrint owns management truth, budgets/variance, process assurance, Glitches, management actions, management reporting, audit and AI interaction.

Trust hierarchy: **Reported → Operationally verified → Financially verified → Closed-period/final.**

## 6. Current beachhead

Agencies roughly 5–50 people, led by strong sellers who have outgrown founder/spreadsheet administration but do not want enterprise ERP/BPM or a heavy management layer.

## 7. Current pricing hypotheses

BluePrint: $299 / $599 / $999 per organization, unvalidated. BlankCRM pricing open. VAULTED take rate open. Old white-label/franchise/developer economics remain channel-specific legacy models requiring reconciliation.

## 8. Retired current guidance

Do not use as current:
- franchise-first definition of company;
- BluePrint transaction/project/unit spine;
- 600k TAM / 100k SAM / 1,500 SOM exploration;
- 5 franchise / 20 white-label / 15 developer SOM;
- $1.59M–$1.89M Y5 forecast;
- automatic $650k funding ask;
- physical hub as near-term company thesis/moat.

## 9. Investor narrative

**BlankCRM helps sell. BluePrint helps run the company. VAULTED connects the network. Academy standardizes. Dproperty/franchise/developers give distribution and cold-start advantages.**

## 10. Immediate proof

Prototype BluePrint → Dproperty pilot → external design partners → paying pilots → production product → BlankCRM attach → narrow VAULTED validation.
