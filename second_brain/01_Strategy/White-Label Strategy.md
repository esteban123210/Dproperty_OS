# White-Label Strategy

The white-label product allows existing boutique agencies to keep their brand while licensing Dproperty's operating system.

## Why It Exists

Some agencies do not want to become Dproperty franchises. They may already have:

- A local reputation.
- Their own brand.
- Their own team.
- Their own client base.

But they may lack:

- Structured data.
- Good reporting.
- Standardized processes.
- Investor projection logic.
- Training content.
- Broker/developer workflow.
- Document control.
- CRM discipline.

## Product Scope

White-label can include:

- Operating workflows.
- Dproperty OS workspace with their branding.
- Document structures.
- Projection framework.
- Training modules.
- CRM setup.
- Dashboards.
- Sales process.
- Implementation support.

## Pricing Logic

Starter:

- $10,000 setup.
- $1,500/month.

Growth:

- $20,000 setup.
- $2,500/month.

## Strategic Restriction

White-label clients should not automatically receive access to Dproperty Private Collection. That access should remain a differentiator for branded franchises or be offered only under special terms.
