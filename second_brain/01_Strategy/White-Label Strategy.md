# White-Label Strategy

The white-label product allows existing boutique agencies to keep their brand while licensing Dproperty's operating system.

## Why It Exists

Some agencies do not want to become Dproperty franchises. They may already have:

- A local reputation.
- Their own brand.
- Their own team.
- Their own client base.

But they may lack:

- Structured data.
- Good reporting.
- Standardized processes.
- Investor projection logic.
- Training content.
- Broker/developer workflow.
- Document control.
- CRM discipline.

## Product Scope

White-label can include:

- Operating workflows.
- Dproperty OS workspace with their branding.
- Document structures.
- Projection framework.
- Training modules.
- CRM setup.
- Dashboards.
- Sales process.
- Implementation support.

## Pricing Logic

Starter:

- $10,000 setup.
- $1,500/month.

Growth:

- $20,000 setup.
- $2,500/month.

Monthly OS fee is positioned **higher than branded**, because white-label builds no Dproperty brand value. GoHighLevel is resold with margin per client.

## Strategic Restriction

White-label clients do **not** automatically receive access to Dproperty Select (formerly Private Collection). That access remains a differentiator for branded franchises. If a white-label partner participates in a Dproperty Select deal, the payout is **2.0% of sale price** (vs 2.5% for branded) — a deliberate strategic gap. Branded gets the brand and the investment upside; white-label gets the system.
