# GTM Market-Specific Tactics — Panama | Bogotá | Medellín

**Scope:** Week 5 – Week 12 (acquisition phase)  
**Markets:** Panama (local, not investment), Bogotá, Medellín  
**Personas:** Agents Seeking Independence (PRIMARY) + Sales Professionals (SECONDARY) + Youth Entrepreneurs (VOLUME)  
**Budget:** Minimal (€2-5k total for all markets, organic-first strategy)  
**Strategy:** Geographic + persona layering

---

## Part 1: Market Context & Audience Profiles

### Market 1: Panama (Local Real Estate, Not Investment Focus)

**Market characteristics:**
- Primary market: Local Panamanians buying/selling property for personal use
- Secondary market: Regional expats + investors (but NOT our GTM focus; we're emphasizing local)
- Commission landscape: 5-6% typical commission, 50/50 agent/broker split
- Competitive landscape: Mix of local brokers + international franchises (RE/MAX, Keller Williams, Engel & Völkers)
- **Dproperty advantage:** Boutique positioning (not mass-market), local know-how, premium brand

**Target franchisee profile in Panama:**
- **Agents Seeking Independence:** Licensed agents in Panama City (or secondary markets: Coronado, Bocas) working for local brokers, want to go independent
- **Sales Professionals:** Insurance agents, telco sales, pharmaceutical reps in Panama City area (current €2-5k/mo → RE €4-5k/deal upside)
- **Youth Entrepreneurs:** College-educated 25-35, bilingual (Spanish-English), entrepreneurial spirit, seek independence

**Local partnership opportunities:**
- Real Estate Association of Panama (Cámara Inmobiliaria de Panamá)
- Business networking groups (YPO, business clubs)
- Accounting firms (referral networks)
- Existing Dproperty contacts in Panama

---

### Market 2: Bogotá (Colombia, Large Urban Market)

**Market characteristics:**
- Largest city in Colombia, growing real estate market
- Mix of local buyers (primary residences, investment), regional buyers, international investors
- Commission landscape: 3-5% typical commission, 50/50 agent/broker split (lower than Panama)
- Competitive landscape: Growing number of boutique agencies + international franchises
- **Dproperty advantage:** Complete operating system, international credibility, Dproperty Select access to premium projects

**Target franchisee profile in Bogotá:**
- **Agents Seeking Independence:** Licensed agents (Inmobiliario) in Bogotá working for Metrocasa, Finca Raíz, or other local brokers; want to scale independently
- **Sales Professionals:** Pharma sales reps (€3-6k/mo), B2B SaaS sales (€4-8k/mo), telco sales (€2-5k/mo) → RE commission €3-4k/deal
- **Youth Entrepreneurs:** Educated professionals 25-35, entrepreneurial, speak English, seek income upside

**Local partnership opportunities:**
- Cámara Colombiana de la Construcción (CAMACOL)
- Real estate professional networks (Sociedad Colombiana de Corredores de Seguros, etc.)
- Accounting & consulting firms (cross-referral)
- Universidad del Rosario, Universidad de los Andes alumni networks (entrepreneurship angle)

---

### Market 3: Medellín (Colombia, Growing Secondary Market)

**Market characteristics:**
- Fastest-growing real estate market in Colombia (after Bogotá)
- Mix of local development + international investment
- Commission landscape: 4-5% typical, 50/50 agent/broker split
- Competitive landscape: Few boutique agencies; mostly regional brokers
- **Dproperty advantage:** First-mover advantage in boutique market, premium positioning, deal access

**Target franchisee profile in Medellín:**
- **Agents Seeking Independence:** Licensed agents in Medellín (Envigado, Sabaneta areas) working for local/regional brokers, want to go independent
- **Sales Professionals:** Insurance agents (€200-500/mo!), telco sales (€2-4k/mo), sales managers in manufacturing → RE commission €3.5-4k/deal (10-20x upside!)
- **Youth Entrepreneurs:** Young professionals 25-35, bilingual, entrepreneurial, eager to build something

**Local partnership opportunities:**
- Cámara de Comercio Medellín
- Real estate professional networks
- Tech startup communities (Ruta N, startup hubs)
- Universidad de Antioquia, Universidad EAFIT alumni networks

## Part 2: Channel Strategy by Market

### Panama — Week 5-12 Execution Plan

**Primary Channels (Week 5-8):**
1. **Direct agent outreach (LinkedIn + WhatsApp)**
   - Search: Licensed agents on LinkedIn in Panama City area
   - Target: Agents with 3-10 years experience, "looking to grow," career progression signals
   - Volume: 30-50 agents
   - Cost: Very Low (organic)

2. **Personal network + warm referrals**
   - Leverage Dproperty's existing Panama contacts (investors, developers, brokers)
   - Ask: "Do you know any good agents who'd want to go independent?"
   - Expected: 5-10 warm introductions
   - Cost: Very Low (personal reach-out)

3. **Real Estate Association of Panama**
   - Contact: Cámara Inmobiliaria de Panamá
   - Tactic: Sponsor a networking event or table; position as "franchise opportunity for independent operators"
   - Expected: 10-20 qualified contacts
   - Cost: €500-1,000

4. **LinkedIn organic content + personal posts**
   - Esteban posts in Spanish/English: "Why real estate agents in Panama should own their own operation"
   - Share: Hypothetical economics ("€30k to start, €4k+ per deal")
   - Expected: 5-15 organic inbound inquiries
   - Cost: Free (content repurposing)

5. **Insurance/telco industry outreach (Sales Professionals)**
   - Identify insurance companies in Panama City (Seguros Universal, Mapfre, etc.)
   - Target sales professionals via LinkedIn (search "insurance sales Panama")
   - Volume: 15-30 leads
   - Positioning: "Insurance commission vs. Real Estate commission" comparison
   - Cost: Very Low

**Secondary Channels (Week 8-12):**
6. **WhatsApp/Telegram real estate communities**
   - Find agent networks in Panama WhatsApp groups
   - Share: Success stories, economics, opportunity
   - Cost: Free (community building)

7. **Broker referral partnerships**
   - Contact: Brokers who have agents wanting to leave ("internal competitor")
   - Proposition: "Pay us €1-2k referral fee for agents we convert to franchisees"
   - Expected: 5-10 referred candidates
   - Cost: €5-10k (if successful)

**Weekly Outreach Targets (Panama):**
- Week 5-6: 20+ agents identified, 10+ first messages sent
- Week 7-8: 30+ total agent leads, 15+ discovery calls scheduled
- Week 9-10: 40+ total leads, 20+ discovery calls completed, 3-5 warm candidates
- Week 11: 1-2 franchisees in closing (preferably 1 agent + 1 sales professional)

## Part 3: Commission Comparison Messaging (For Sales Professionals)

Use these comparisons in outreach to insurance, pharma, telco, SaaS sales professionals:

### Panama

**Insurance Agent:**
- Current: ~€300/month + commission (€2-3k/month typical)
- RE Agent (Dproperty): €4,125/deal × 12 deals/year = €49.5k/year gross
- **Upside: 15-20x**
- Message: "Your insurance commission pays you €200. One real estate deal pays you €4,100. Why not?"

**Telco Sales Rep:**
- Current: €2-4k/month + commission
- RE Agent: €4,125/deal × 15 deals/year = €61.9k/year
- **Upside: 15-30x**
- Message: "Trading 10 monthly bonus targets for 1-2 RE deals? You'd make more in 2 deals than a year of telco targets."

---

### Bogotá / Medellín

**Insurance Agent:**
- Current: COP 500k-1M/month (~€130-260/month in European terms, or ~€1,500-2,500 annual)
- RE Agent (Dproperty): COP 20M/year (~€5,200/year in European terms, or €50-60k annual in local earning potential)
- **Upside: 20-40x local earning power**
- Message: "El comisión de un agente de seguros en Medellín: €150-300/mes. Un deal de bienes raíces: COP 20M (~€5k). Mismo esfuerzo de ventas. 50-100x el comisión."

**Pharma Sales Rep:**
- Current: COP 3-6M/month + bonus (~€800-1,600/month)
- RE Agent: COP 20M/deal × 12 deals = COP 240M/year (~€62k/year)
- **Upside: 30-80x**
- Message: "Pharma sales: great training, mediocre commission. Real estate: same sales skills, 30-50x the commission. We teach you the product."

**B2B SaaS Sales:**
- Current: COP 4-10M/month (~€1-2.5k/month)
- RE Agent: COP 20M/deal × 15 deals = COP 300M/year (~€78k/year)
- **Upside: 30-60x**
- Message: "B2B SaaS trains you to sell complex solutions. Real estate is simpler (the product sells itself). Same sales skills, 30-60x the income."

---

### General Positioning for Sales Professionals (All Markets)

**Headline:** "Your sales skills are worth more in real estate."

**Opening email:**
> "Hi [Name], I saw your background in [industry] sales. Your income is €[X]/month, and you're crushing it. But in real estate, one deal pays what you earn in [Y] months. We're looking for salespeople like you who want to 5-10x their income while building their own business. 15-min call? [link]"

**Discovery call positioning:**
1. "You're an excellent salesperson — that's the hardest part. Real estate is the same: understand the client, match the product, close the deal."
2. "The difference: real estate pays €4-5k per deal. Your current field pays €200-1k per deal (or €[X] monthly)."
3. "You don't need RE experience — you need sales skills (which you have) + our system (we provide)."
4. "Year 1: learn the market, close 12-15 deals, earn €50-60k. Year 2+: with a team, earn €150-200k+."
5. "Cost: €30k to start. Earned back in 7-8 deals. That's 2-3 months of work for someone with your sales ability."

## Part 8: Decision Point: Market Pivot Triggers

**If Panama is underperforming (Week 8):**
- Increase referral reach (brokers, associations)
- Test direct agent outreach via WhatsApp (vs. LinkedIn only)
- Reallocate budget to Bogotá/Medellín (higher volume)

**If Sales Professionals not converting (Week 8):**
- Adjust messaging (emphasize income comparison more)
- Test cold outreach to specific insurance companies (less organic)
- Extend timeline expectations (they need more education)

**If no franchises in LOI by Week 10:**
- Activate referral bonus program (pay €1-2k for successful referrals)
- Extend timeline to Sept 30 (not Sept 15)
- Prepare for Phase 2 recruiting (Oct+)
