# Business Model Overview

Dproperty OS has three business lines.

## 1. Branded Franchise

Revenue sources:

- Launch fee.
- Monthly OS fee.
- Royalty on **collected** local gross commission income (6%).
- Network & Brand Fund (1.5%).
- Dproperty Select: franchisee earns 2.5% of sale price (HQ keeps the balance + upside).
- Optional additional services.

Target customer:

- Owner-operator with capital and network.
- Someone who wants the Dproperty brand, system, and support.
- Not necessarily an experienced real estate operator.

## 2. White-label OS

Revenue sources:

- Setup fee.
- Monthly software/operations fee.
- Implementation fees.
- Training/support fees.
- Optional premium modules.

Target customer:

- Existing boutique agency.
- Wants better systems but wants to keep its own brand.
- Does not automatically access Dproperty Select; if participating, earns 2.0% of sale price.

## 3. Developer Sales OS

Revenue sources:

- Managed sales desk monthly fee.
- Percentage of gross sales value on developer-team sales.
- Higher percentage on Dproperty/network-sourced sales.
- Optional exclusive sales partner fee/commission.

Target customer:

- Developer with a project but weak sales team/process.
- Developer who needs CRM, reporting, broker management, training, and pipeline discipline.

## Business Model Logic

The strongest model is not purely SaaS. It is a hybrid of:

- SaaS-like monthly fees.
- Franchise royalties.
- Implementation/setup fees.
- Revenue share/commissions.
- Consulting/managed service fees.
- Network effects from projects, brokers, and cross-market deal flow.

## Ecosystem Framing Update (2026-07-14)

**Reframe:** The three business lines above are now understood as **Phase 1** of a larger play. The meta-model is an **ecosystem** — "the soul of 19M, the model of Station F, for real estate" — a curated hub where the whole real estate value chain (emerging talent, developers, agencies, capital) gathers. Franchising is not the end game; it is how we **prove the model and fund the ecosystem**.

**Two phases of the business model:**
- **Phase 1 (Yr 0–5) — Franchise system (this note's three lines):** launch/OS fees, royalties, Network & Brand Fund, Dproperty Select commissions, white-label subscriptions, developer revenue. Generates HQ EBITDA from Year 3.
- **Phase 2 (Yr 5–15) — Physical ecosystem/hub:** desk & membership rental, events (hackathons/training/conferences), matchmaking fees, **equity stakes in ventures born in the hub**, franchise pull-through, and proprietary data. Funded by Phase 1 EBITDA + an external capital partner.

**Capital-recycling logic:** franchise fees + royalties + Select commissions → HQ EBITDA → seeds hub, talent programs, equity fund → hub deepens the network → network makes franchises more valuable → more franchises. See [[Pitch Deck Outline]] (Deck 1) and [[Ecosystem Deck Outline]] (Deck 2).
