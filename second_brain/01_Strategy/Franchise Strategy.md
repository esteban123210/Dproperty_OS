# Franchise Strategy

## Role

Dproperty franchising is a **branded distribution/operating channel** for the B_ stack, not the company thesis and not the market-size denominator.

## Why keep it

It can provide:
- high-value customer acquisition;
- branded proof cases;
- full-stack deployments;
- recurring/royalty economics;
- network participants;
- real operating feedback.

## Constraint

The franchise model must not create distrust among independent BluePrint customers. Tenant isolation, contractual data ownership and neutral software governance are non-negotiable.

## Brand

Dproperty remains investment-focused. B_ can serve broader agency types without forcing Dproperty positioning.
