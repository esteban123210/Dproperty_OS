# Dproperty OS & Network — Conversation Summary

## Executive summary
Dproperty should evolve from a boutique real estate agency into a scalable operating platform with three connected commercial routes: **Dproperty-branded franchises**, **white-label operating systems for boutique agencies**, and **Developer Sales OS** for developers that need sales process, training, broker coordination, and project sales infrastructure.

The most important strategic shift is that Dproperty OS is not just software. It is the container for the Dproperty method: workflows, documents, broker agreements, projections, training, project data, quality control, dashboards, and access to curated inventory.

## Agreed strategic architecture

1. **Dproperty-branded franchise** — operators use the Dproperty brand, Dproperty OS, training, standards, Private Collection access, and HQ support.
2. **White-label OS** — boutique agencies keep their own brand but license the system and methodology.
3. **Developer Sales OS** — developers use Dproperty to train, systemize, monitor, and partially or fully run their project sales operation.
4. **Dproperty Private Collection** — premium HQ-managed inventory, including Panama projects and future curated investment projects, remains controlled by HQ and is monetized through a 50/50 commission split when sold by franchises.
5. **GoHighLevel** — optional marketing/CRM front-office layer passed through at cost. Dproperty OS remains the execution/document/projection/governance layer.

## Agreed pricing logic

### Branded franchise
- Initial launch fee: **$15,000**.
- OS fee: **$1,000/month**.
- Royalty on local gross commission income: **6%**.
- Brand/network marketing fee: **1.5%**.
- GoHighLevel: passed through at cost.
- Dproperty Private Collection: **50/50 gross commission split** between HQ and franchise, plus 3% royalty and 1% network fee on the franchise half.

### White-label OS
- Starter: **$10,000 setup + $1,500/month**.
- Growth: **$20,000 setup + $2,500/month**.
- Enterprise: **$50,000+ setup + $5,000+/month**.
- No automatic Private Collection access.

### Developer Sales OS
- Developer team sales: **0.5% of gross sale value**.
- Dproperty/network-sourced sales: **2.5%–3% of gross sale value**.
- Managed Sales Desk: **$3,000–$5,000/month minimum**, creditable against success fees.

## Updated five-year target
The realistic five-year target is conservative:
- **5 branded franchises** in Latin America.
- **20 white-label agencies**.
- **15 developer partnerships/projects**.

## Internal funding need
- Recommended internal venture budget: **$650,000 over 24 months**.

- This is not an outside VC round. The two existing Dproperty owners fund the NewCo internally.

## Proposed NewCo logic
The new entity could be called **Dproperty OS & Network**. It would own the platform, training academy, white-label product, franchise operating system, Developer Sales OS, and templates/manuals created for the new venture. Existing Dproperty owners would keep the existing business and would fund/control the NewCo.

## Founder/operator compensation discussion
The proposed personal package discussed privately:
- Role: **Co-founder & Venture Lead / Founding Product & Strategy Lead**.
- Salary: **€5,000/month** as operating cost.
- Equity: **15% vesting equity in the NewCo**, or 10% guaranteed + 5% milestone-based.
- Salary step-ups tied to revenue/EBITDA milestones.
- Optional performance bonus on first-year revenue originated personally.
- Dividend policy once the company is profitable and adequately reserved.
- Protection clauses: vesting, acceleration on termination without cause, change-of-control acceleration, buyback mechanism.

## Main conclusion
The business is realistic if pitched as an internal venture that strengthens Dproperty’s current operations while creating new recurring and commission-linked revenue. The proposal is credible because it does not depend on hypergrowth: only five franchises, twenty white-label agencies, and fifteen developer projects by Year 5.
