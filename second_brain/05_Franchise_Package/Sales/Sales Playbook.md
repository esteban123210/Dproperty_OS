# Sales Playbook

## Purpose

Help a new Dproperty franchise owner start selling through their personal network and structured outreach.

## Sales Philosophy

Dproperty sales should be consultative, trust-based, and curated. The goal is not to push inventory. The goal is to understand the buyer/investor and match them with appropriate opportunities.

## First 100 Contacts Strategy

The first franchisee should map:

- Family network.
- Friends.
- Business contacts.
- Investors.
- Entrepreneurs.
- Expat contacts.
- Brokers.
- Developers.
- Lawyers/accountants.
- Past clients.

## Client Qualification

Ask:

- Are they buying for investment, lifestyle, family, residency, diversification, or income?
- What budget range?
- What country/city preference?
- What risk tolerance?
- What return expectations?
- What timeline?
- Do they need financing?
- Who else is involved in the decision?

## Core Scripts

### Personal Network Launch Message

Hi [Name], I wanted to share something I am building/launching with Dproperty. We are creating a more curated way to guide clients and investors through real estate opportunities, with stronger project analysis, trusted processes, and access to selected developments. I thought of you because you may either be interested directly or know someone who would value this kind of guidance.

### First Follow-Up

Thanks for the interest. The best next step is not to send random listings, but to understand what kind of opportunity would actually make sense for you. We can do a short call and I can explain how Dproperty works.

### Dproperty Select Introduction

Some opportunities are part of Dproperty Select (formerly Private Collection). These are curated by HQ and handled with a more controlled process because of the nature of the projects and investor relationships.

## Weekly Sales Routine

- Monday: pipeline review.
- Tuesday: personal outreach.
- Wednesday: broker/developer follow-up.
- Thursday: client meetings/project presentations.
- Friday: update CRM, send follow-ups, report to HQ.
