# Dproperty Sales Playbook (M4)

> **Who this is for.** The franchisee's **Sales Advisors (SA)** — the people who prospect, qualify, present, negotiate, and close. It is the agent-facing view of Dproperty's commercial processes. Owners read the [[Operations Manual|Franchise Operations Manual]] (M2); this is the selling manual their team uses daily.
>
> **Roles, never names.** Everyone is referenced by role code (SA, SD, OC, OD, CA, PH). See the [[00 - Process Library Index|Role Map]].
>
> **Local-market variables.** Currency is **USD** unless a market overrides it. Amounts, commission %, taxes, deposits, and legal steps marked `[local]` are set per market — never assume Panama.

## 1. Sales philosophy

Dproperty sales are **consultative, trust-based, and curated**. The goal is not to push inventory — it is to understand the buyer/investor and match them to the right opportunity. We present quality, not volume. We never overpromise, and we use data carefully. This is the boutique difference and it is non-negotiable.

## 2. The Dproperty sales system (five processes)

Every sale runs one of five documented processes. Master the one you're in; the full step/RACI/KPI detail lives in the Process Library.

| # | You are selling… | Process | Full detail |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | (any lead, before it's a deal) | Lead capture & qualification | [[01 - Leads]] |
| 2 | Off-plan / pre-construction | Preventa (Lista Cero) | [[02 - Preventa (Lista Cero)]] |
| 3 | An existing property to an end user | Secondary market | [[03 - Secondary Market Sale]] |
| 4 | An investor's contract to a new buyer | Assignment (cesión) | [[04 - Assignment (Cesion)]] |
| 5 | A long-stay rental | Rental | [[05 - Long-Stay Rental]] |

**Golden rules across all five:**
- Every lead is in the CRM within the SLA `[local]`; no lead without a next action.
- **No offer goes out without SD approval** of strategy and terms.
- Everything material is written down — a call is never the record.
- Dproperty Select opportunities follow HQ rules (§8), always.

## 3. Who does what in a sale

| Role | In a sale |
|---|---|
| **SA** | You: prospecting, visits, offer prep, negotiation, close, follow-up |
| **SD** | Approves offers, negotiation strategy, final contract; runs weekly pipeline |
| **OC** | Contracts, documents, billing, signing coordination, commission execution |
| **OD** | Process compliance and exceptions (esp. rentals) |
| **CA** | Approves client profiles / special profiles (esp. rentals) |
| **PH** | Inspection, inventory, handover |

In a small office one person may wear several hats — the **responsibility** still applies (see [[00 - Process Library Index|small-office rule]]).

## 4. Pipeline stages (standard)

`New → Qualified → Presenting → Offer (SD-approved) → Reserved → Contract → Closed → Follow-up`

Keep every deal's stage current in the CRM. The weekly pipeline review (SD) works off these stages.

## 5. Prospecting — the first 100 contacts

Your pipeline starts with your own network. Map and segment 100 contacts:

- Family · friends · business contacts · investors · entrepreneurs · expats · brokers · developers · lawyers/accountants · past clients.

Segment by likely need (invest / lifestyle / residency / income / diversification) and work them with the structured follow-up cadence from [[01 - Leads]] (Day 1 / 3 / 7 / 14).

## 6. Client qualification

Before you present anything, understand the client. Ask:

- Buying for **investment, lifestyle, family, residency, diversification, or income?**
- **Budget** range and what's liquid vs. financed?
- **Country/city** preference?
- **Risk tolerance** and **return expectations?**
- **Timeline?** Financing needed?
- **Who else** is in the decision?

Qualification determines the process (§2) and the priority (Hot/Warm/Cold per [[01 - Leads]]).

## 7. Core scripts

> Adapt tone to be **confident, warm, analytical, premium** (Brand voice). Localize language per market `[local]`.

**Personal-network launch**
> Hi [Name], I wanted to share something I'm building with Dproperty — a more curated way to guide clients and investors through real estate, with stronger project analysis, trusted processes, and access to selected developments. I thought of you because you may be interested directly, or know someone who'd value this kind of guidance.

**First follow-up**
> Thanks for the interest. The best next step isn't random listings — it's understanding what would actually make sense for you. Can we do a short call so I can explain how Dproperty works?

**Discovery call (structure)**
> Motivation → budget/liquidity → location → risk & return → timeline → decision-makers. Log everything in the CRM; identify the process; set the next step.

**Dproperty Select introduction**
> Some opportunities are part of **Dproperty Select** — curated by HQ and handled with a more controlled process because of the nature of the projects and investor relationships. I can introduce one if it fits your profile.

**Objection handling (principles)**
> Never pressure. Acknowledge → clarify the real concern → reframe with data → propose a small next step. If it's price/financing, involve OC/SD early rather than improvising terms.

## 8. Dproperty Select — advisor rules

Dproperty Select (internally "Private Collection") is **HQ-controlled**. As an SA you **may present it** under rules and earn the fixed originator payout `[local: 2.5% branded / 1.5% white-label of sale price]`. You **may not**:

- create or edit projections or materials without HQ approval;
- negotiate directly with the developer;
- bypass HQ on registration or client handling.

Full rules: [[Private Collection Guide]] and the [[Compliance Package|Compliance Manual]] (M5).

## 9. Weekly sales routine

- **Mon:** pipeline review (with SD).
- **Tue:** personal + new outreach.
- **Wed:** broker/developer follow-up.
- **Thu:** client meetings / project presentations.
- **Fri:** CRM hygiene, follow-ups, report to SD.

## 11. Localization notes (multi-market)

This playbook is market-agnostic. Before use in a new market, HQ confirms: currency, commission %, reservation/down-payment norms, KYC/AML document set, legal closing steps, deposit rules, and language. Those are `[local]` variables — the *process* stays the same, the *values* change.
