# Welcome Kit Checklist

## Purpose

Create a premium onboarding experience.

## Physical Items

- Welcome letter.
- Printed manifesto.
- 90-day launch plan.
- Branded notebook.
- Premium pen.
- Business card holder.
- Small architectural object or desk item.
- Certificate/plaque.

- Welcome video.
- Dproperty OS login.

- Training academy link.
- Calendar invites.
- Support directory.
- Launch checklist.
