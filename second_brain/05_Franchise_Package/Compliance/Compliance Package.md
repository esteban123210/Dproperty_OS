# Dproperty Compliance Manual (M5)

> ⚠️ **Draft — not legal advice.** This is a structured operating draft that **must be reviewed by qualified counsel in each market** before it governs a live franchise. Legal specifics resolve from the [[Localization Framework]] per market.
>
> **Who must follow it:** everyone in a Dproperty office — the Principal (PRIN) is accountable; every role complies. **HQ owns and enforces it.**

## 1. Why compliance is a system, not a checklist
Dproperty operates across borders in a **regulated** industry (real estate, money movement, personal data). A breach isn't just brand damage — it can be a **licensing, AML, privacy, or franchise-law event** with legal consequences. Compliance protects the client, the franchisee, HQ, and the network.

## 2. How it works
- **Global core** (this manual) + **local pack** (per-market legal specifics from [[Localization Framework]]).
- Every franchisee completes **compliance certification** before going live and **recertifies** on cadence (see [[Training Academy Outline]]).
- Incidents route through the OS **Cases** engine; no-blame process issues through **Glitch Report** (Decision 2026-07-18).

## 3. Compliance domains

### 3.1 Franchise law & disclosure `[local]`
- Some jurisdictions require a **disclosure document and/or registration** before a franchise may be *sold* (e.g. US FDD & registration states; EU rules; various LATAM regimes).
- **HQ must confirm the regime per market before offering a franchise there.** Selling without required disclosure is a legal event.
- Franchisee: no re-selling/sub-franchising rights except as contracted.

### 3.2 Real-estate licensing `[local]`
- Confirm **who may legally broker** in-market and **under whose licence** the office operates.
- No advisor performs regulated brokerage acts without the required licence/affiliation.
- Local body (e.g. an association such as ACOBIR in Panama) requirements are captured in the market pack.

### 3.3 AML / KYC & sanctions `[local]`
- Real estate is an **AML-regulated sector** in most markets. Each office runs **client due diligence (KYC)**: identity, source of funds where required, and **sanctions/PEP screening**.
- Define the KYC document set and any **suspicious-activity reporting** obligation in the market pack.
- No deal proceeds where KYC cannot be satisfied. Escalate concerns to HQ.

### 3.4 Data privacy & the CRM `[local]`
- The CRM/OS holds **personal data across borders** — this triggers privacy law (e.g. Panama Law 81/2019; Colombia Ley 1581/2012; GDPR if any EU data).
- Rules: lawful basis for holding leads, consent for marketing, data-subject rights, retention limits, and **cross-border transfer** controls.
- Only approved systems store client data; no exporting client lists to personal devices/accounts.

### 3.5 Advertising, marketing & projections
- **No guaranteed-return claims. Ever.** Projections use **HQ-approved assumptions only** and are clearly labelled as projections, not promises.
- No unapproved use of project/developer materials; no edited Dproperty Select materials.
- Comply with local advertising/consumer-protection rules `[local]`.

### 3.6 Anti-bribery & developer relationships
- No improper payments/kickbacks to win listings, developer allocations, or approvals.
- Gifts/hospitality within a defined, modest threshold; log anything material.
- Applies especially to developer and Dproperty Select dealings.

### 3.7 Fair dealing & anti-discrimination
- Serve clients without unlawful discrimination; follow fair-housing-type rules where they apply `[local]`.
- Honest representation of properties; disclose known material facts.

### 3.8 Contracts & document control
- Use **approved templates** ([[Contract Templates Index]]); material changes need HQ/legal approval.
- **Single approved contract version** signed by all parties (see rental version-control rule, [[05 - Long-Stay Rental]]).
- Retain executed documents per retention policy; store in approved systems only.

### 3.9 Dproperty Select controls
- HQ-controlled inventory. Franchisee may present under rules and earn the fixed originator payout `[local: 2.5% branded / 2.0% white-label]`.
- **No** direct developer negotiation, **no** unapproved projections, **no** edited materials, **no** bypassing HQ registration. See [[Private Collection Guide]].

### 3.10 Commission & financial integrity
- Respect the **three-flow firewall** (income vs. agent payout vs. HQ royalty) — [[07 - Commissions]].
- Report **collected GCI** accurately; royalty is computed on gross-into-company (Decision 2026-07-05). No hiding or misreporting deals.
- Accurate invoicing and tax handling `[local]`.

### 3.11 Insurance `[local]`
- Maintain required cover (e.g. professional indemnity / errors & omissions, general liability) at defined minimums before go-live.

## 4. Every franchisee MUST
- Register every client/deal in the OS/CRM; keep it current.
- Complete KYC & sanctions screening before proceeding.
- Use approved materials, templates, and projection assumptions.
- Follow Dproperty Select and commission-reporting rules.
- Hold required licences and insurance.
- Complete and maintain compliance certification.

## 5. Every franchisee MUST NOT
- Make guaranteed-return or misleading claims.
- Modify Dproperty Select materials or bypass HQ on controlled inventory.
- Hide deals, misreport GCI, or game the royalty base.
- Export or misuse client personal data.
- Pay or accept bribes/kickbacks.
- Operate without required licence/insurance.

## 6. Breaches & incidents
- **Client/legal/financial issues** → OS **Cases** engine (confidential, routed, SLA-bound).
- **No-blame process failures / near-misses** → **Glitch Report** (never person-attributed) — Decision 2026-07-18.
- Serious breaches (AML, privacy, licensing, brand-damaging conduct) → immediate HQ escalation; may trigger agreement remedies.

## 7. Review rhythm
- Monthly compliance review during first 90 days; quarterly thereafter.
- Immediate review for any Dproperty Select, AML, or data-privacy issue.
- Annual recertification + refresh when a market pack or law changes.

## 8. Localization
All `[local]` items resolve from the market's pack in [[Localization Framework]]. No market goes live until its compliance column is complete and **signed off by local counsel**. Open items feed [[Open Questions]] (Legal).
