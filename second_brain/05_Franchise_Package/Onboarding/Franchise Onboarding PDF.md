# Franchise Onboarding PDF

## Purpose

This is the first main document a franchisee reads after joining Dproperty. It should feel premium, warm, clear, and operational.

## Draft Structure

### 1. Welcome to Dproperty

Welcome the franchisee into a boutique real estate system built on trust, curation, discipline, and investor-aware guidance.

### 2. What Dproperty Is

Dproperty is a boutique real estate agency and operating system for curated real estate sales. It combines brand, methodology, project access, software, training, documents, and HQ support.

### 3. What Makes Dproperty Different

Dproperty is not mass-market. It is not a generic CRM. It is not an aggressive sales machine. It is a curated, premium, systematized way to guide clients and investors through real estate opportunities.

### 4. What You Are Joining

You are joining a franchise system that gives you structure, not just a logo. You receive a way of operating, selling, reporting, training, and presenting opportunities.

### 5. What Dproperty Gives You

- Brand.
- Dproperty OS.
- CRM setup.
- Sales playbook.
- Operations manual.
- Training academy.
- Templates.
- Project presentation structure.
- Projection logic.
- Dproperty Select access under rules.
- HQ support.
- Reporting rhythm.

### 6. What We Expect From You

- Protect the brand.
- Use approved materials.
- Keep CRM and Dproperty OS updated.
- Follow client registration rules.
- Respect Dproperty Select rules.
- Report pipeline and deals.
- Participate in training.
- Represent Dproperty with professionalism and discretion.

### 7. How the Franchise Model Works

Explain launch fee ($30,000 founding / $40,000 after 5 successful franchises), monthly OS fee ($1,000), royalty (6%) + Network & Brand Fund (1.5%) on **collected** local GCI, local commission, Dproperty Select payout (2.5% of sale price to the franchisee), and reporting obligations.

### 8. How Dproperty OS Works

Dproperty OS is your operating platform for clients, brokers, projects, deals, documents, projections, training, and reporting.

### 9. How Dproperty Select Works

Dproperty Select (formerly Private Collection) is HQ-controlled curated inventory. Franchisees may present it under rules and earn a fixed **2.5% of sale price**; HQ retains the balance of the negotiated commission and keeps any upside above the 5% modelled rate.

### 10. First 30 Days

Setup, training, brand activation, CRM/OS onboarding, first network list, first contacts, first broker list.

### 11. First 60 Days

Meetings, outreach, project presentations, lead campaigns, broker relationships, first opportunities.

### 12. First 90 Days

Pipeline discipline, reporting, first reservations, HQ review, compliance review, next quarterly plan.

### 13. Support Team

List HQ contacts, support channels, review rhythm, escalation rules.

### 14. Launch Checklist

Include a practical checklist from legal signed to first meetings booked.

### 15. Final Message from HQ

Reinforce: the franchisee is not buying a logo; they are joining a disciplined boutique operating system.
