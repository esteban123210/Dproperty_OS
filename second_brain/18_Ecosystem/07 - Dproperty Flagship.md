# Dproperty — Flagship Investment Brand

## What it is

Dproperty is B_RealEstate's flagship consumer and franchise brand for real-estate investment. Its clients buy primarily to profit, not to live. The brand promise is curation over volume, numbers over hype, and advice worthy of a long-term investor relationship.

## What it is not

- Not the parent company.
- Not the name of BluePrint.
- Not a general residential brokerage brand.
- Not the identity a white-label partner must adopt.
- Not proof that B_RealEstate already has a franchise track record; Dproperty's operating history must be attributed honestly.

## Purpose

Provide investors and franchisees with a recognizable, disciplined investment proposition and give B_RealEstate a flagship operation that proves and improves the method.

## Client persona

An investor seeking a defensible real-estate allocation, often cross-border or pre-construction, who values curation, negotiated access, transparent assumptions and an exit/rental thesis more than decorative finishes.

## Franchisee persona

An agent, sales professional or entrepreneur prepared to specialize in investment property and operate under strict brand, product-selection and compliance standards.

## Product-selection rule

Select product that is profitable and reliable, not merely attractive or livable. Between comparable projects, expensive finishes can reduce the investment case by raising the entry price without increasing the relevant return.

## Value proposition

Brand recognition, investment specialization, BluePrint, configured GoHighLevel, B_Academy, Dproperty Select at preferential terms, VAULTED access, templates/manuals, launch support and network reporting.

## Canonical economics

The repository's last approved internal baseline before the live-site divergence was:

- Launch fee: $30,000 founding, rising to $40,000 after five successful franchises.
- Royalty: 6% on collected gross-into-company GCI.
- Network & Brand Fund: 1.5% on the same basis.
- OS/platform fee: $1,000/month.
- Minimum royalty floor: proposed $500/month from month 7, creditable.
- Dproperty Select payout: 2.5% of sale price.

The live site currently publishes $45,000, 6%, 2% and a 12–18 month payback. Those numbers are not reconciled and must not be treated as canonical until approved in [[14 - Unit Economics Registry]].

## KPIs

- Qualified investor relationships and repeat-investment rate.
- Assets/units sold and gross transaction value.
- Projection-to-close and Select conversion.
- Franchise time to launch and time to first transaction.
- Brand compliance and client outcome exceptions.
- Franchise contribution margin and retention.
