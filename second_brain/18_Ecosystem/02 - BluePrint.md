# BluePrint

> **Canonical product folder:** [[../04_Product/BluePrint/00 - README - Product Map|BluePrint v3.0 Product Map]]

## Product definition

**BluePrint is a chat-first, AI-native management operating system for small and growing real-estate agencies.** It connects to the tools the company already uses and turns fragmented operational, financial and human information into structured, auditable management truth.

It is designed for agencies whose founders are strong sellers but do not want to spend hours doing back-office management or build an expensive COO/CFO/operations structure.

## Economic promise

**One capable administrator + BluePrint** should let an agency operate with materially more professional management discipline than the same team using CRM + spreadsheets + Drive + WhatsApp alone.

## What BluePrint owns

- management truth and verification;
- management budgets/forecast/variance;
- management KPIs/reports;
- processes, SOPs, controls and process health;
- Glitches/operational incidents and corrective actions;
- management actions, approvals and decisions;
- governed company knowledge;
- reconciliation/exception queues;
- long-term audit trail and period close;
- management AI.

## What BluePrint does not own

- CRM/leads/marketing automation;
- property/project/unit inventory or MLS;
- general ledger/tax/payroll;
- property management;
- Academy/LMS;
- VAULTED marketplace;
- sales-agent workspace.

## Product relationship to GHL

GoHighLevel is a default/optional **front-office CRM**, not BluePrint’s foundation. An independent agency may keep GHL, HubSpot, Salesforce, Follow Up Boss or another CRM. BluePrint treats CRM data as reported operational data and applies verification/reconciliation before it becomes trusted management information.

## Relationship to accounting

Accounting software remains the ledger. BluePrint consumes verified financial data and turns it into management control and explanation.

## Primary buyer / operator

Buyer: agency owner/principal.  
Primary operator: administrator/secretary/coordinator.  
Executive user: owner/CEO.  
Salespeople primarily remain in CRM.

## Commercial hypothesis

Standalone pricing is currently being validated around organization-level plans rather than agent seats. See [[../04_Product/BluePrint/08 - Pricing and Packaging]].

## Role in the ecosystem

BluePrint is an independently sellable software product. B_Franchising is a distribution/customer channel, not the definition of BluePrint. VAULTED is a separate transaction/network monetization layer. Academy is a capability/standards layer.
