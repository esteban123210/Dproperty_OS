## Canonical calculation boundaries

### Local branded sale

Baseline model: 5% total commission → 50/50 external co-broke where applicable → 2.5% gross into company → 35% seller / 10% sales director / 55% franchise net. Royalty and Network & Brand Fund apply to gross into company, not franchise net.

### Dproperty Select

Separate structure. Branded partner payout 2.5% of sale price; white-label 1.5%. Do not apply the local 35/10/55 waterfall unless a later decision explicitly changes the rule.

### Developer Sales Partner

Separate the developer's total sales commission pool from the amount retained by B_RealEstate and from salesperson/broker payouts. Public copy currently merges these concepts.

## Publication gate

A number may be public only when:

1. The financial model contains the same definition and value.
2. The Decision Log records approval and effective date.
3. Legal/commercial documents use the same calculation basis.
4. BluePrint commission logic is configured from the same registry.
5. Any payback/performance claim has assumptions and an appropriate disclaimer.
