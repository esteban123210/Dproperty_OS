# GoHighLevel CRM

## What it is

GoHighLevel is the ecosystem's white-labeled front-office CRM and marketing automation engine.

## What it is not

- Not proprietary B_RealEstate software.
- Not BluePrint.
- Not the legal, project, unit, projection, commission or document source of truth.
- Not the final record of transaction completion.

## Purpose

Capture demand, manage communication and appointments, qualify leads, automate follow-up and preserve marketing attribution until a real operational opportunity is ready for BluePrint.

## Primary users

Marketing lead, sales advisor, sales manager, franchise principal and HQ growth team.

## Pipeline separation

Maintain separate pipelines for:

1. Dproperty investor leads.
2. Branded franchise prospects.
3. White-label prospects.
4. Developer prospects.

Do not mix partner acquisition with property-buyer leads.

## Handoff to BluePrint

GoHighLevel owns the lead and communication record through qualification. When the user creates an investment case or operational transaction, BluePrint creates the transaction and stores the GHL contact/opportunity ID. BluePrint writes only major milestones back: project matched, proposal sent, reserved, contracted, closed or lost.

The same field must not be manually editable in both systems.

## Unit economics

Third-party subscription, sub-account, communication and automation costs must be included in the relevant B_RealEstate plan. Any resale margin should be explicit in the plan model. Do not describe the CRM as “our proprietary CRM”; describe it as a white-labeled, configured and integrated CRM.

## KPIs

- Leads by source and partner model.
- Response time.
- Appointment booking and show rate.
- Qualification rate.
- Cost per qualified opportunity.
- Contactability and nurture conversion.
- Qualified opportunities handed to BluePrint.

## Website requirement

All bfranchising.com forms and booking flows must write to the correct GoHighLevel pipeline with source, page, model of interest and consent fields. The current live site uses same-page GET forms and a Cal.com booking link; this requires verification or replacement.
