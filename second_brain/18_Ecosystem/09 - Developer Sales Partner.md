# Developer Sales Partner

## What it is

A dedicated commercial partnership for developers: a team, method and operating system that learns the developer's product, buyer and standards and carries that knowledge across projects.

## What it is not

- Not a generic broker distribution agreement.
- Not a temporary team handed a brochure.
- Not automatically exclusive.
- Not simply listing the project in VAULTED or Dproperty Select.
- Not the same as selling a franchise to the developer.

## Purpose

Give developers a professional, measurable sales operation without requiring them to build recruitment, training, CRM discipline, broker activation, reporting and sales governance from zero.

## Primary persona

A developer principal or commercial director with viable product but an inconsistent internal sales operation, fragmented external brokers or insufficient market intelligence.

## Value proposition

Continuity across projects, investment translation, qualified network demand, controlled projections, sales-process discipline, broker activation, live reporting and feedback that improves product/pricing decisions.

## Service levels

1. Sales Enablement: process, CRM, materials and training for the developer's team.
2. Managed Sales Desk: B_RealEstate actively manages the sales operation and reporting.
3. Embedded Commercial Partner: dedicated team and deeper portfolio relationship across projects.

## Canonical economics

The repository's strategic baseline is:

- Approximately 0.5% of gross sale value on developer-team sales where B_RealEstate trains/manages the system.
- Approximately 2.5%–3% on B_RealEstate/network-sourced sales.
- Optional managed-desk fee of $3,000–$5,000/month, potentially creditable.

The live site currently publishes zero entry cost and 4%–6% per unit. That may describe the total developer commission rather than B_RealEstate's retained economics; the distinction must be corrected before publication.

## BluePrint experience

Project dashboard, unit availability, lead/source performance, reservation/close reporting, broker activation, approved materials, recommendations and fee status. GoHighLevel remains the communications/pipeline engine; BluePrint owns governed execution and reporting.

## KPIs

- Sales velocity and projected sell-out date.
- Lead-to-reservation and reservation-to-close rates.
- Sales by source and team.
- Broker activation and productivity.
- Price/discount exceptions.
- Developer renewal across projects.
- B_RealEstate fee revenue and contribution margin.
