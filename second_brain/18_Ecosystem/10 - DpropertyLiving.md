# DpropertyLiving — Decision Required

## Current reality

The live bfranchising.com site presents DpropertyLiving as an end-user/lifestyle side of the Dproperty family and, on the ecosystem page, as one of four routes into the ecosystem. The canonical August 3 brand decision, however, defined three commercial doors and made Dproperty an investment-only brand. No current strategy file defines DpropertyLiving's ownership, audience, economics, franchise rights or relationship to white-label.

## Provisional definition

If approved, DpropertyLiving could be a consumer brand extension for end users buying a home within projects already accepted by the Dproperty curation process, using investment discipline without presenting the purchase primarily as an investment.

## What it is not

- Not currently an approved fourth partner model.
- Not a reason to weaken Dproperty's investment-only positioning.
- Not interchangeable with white-label, which may serve any locally appropriate lifestyle/end-user market under the partner's own brand.

## Potential client persona

A buyer choosing a primary or secondary residence who values quality, livability and long-term value, but is not primarily optimizing yield or exit timing.

## Decisions required before continued public promotion

1. Is DpropertyLiving a real sub-brand, a campaign label or only a service category?
2. Can every Dproperty franchise use it, and does it share territory rights?
3. Does it use the same franchise economics and brand standards?
4. How does it differ commercially from white-label?
5. Which inventory qualifies, and who approves it?
6. Does it have a separate website, CRM pipeline and reporting view?
7. Are trademark and domain assets available?

## Unit economics

None are canonical. Do not promise a fee, royalty, payout or payback for DpropertyLiving until the above decisions are logged and modeled.

## Recommendation

Keep the concept documented but label it “under definition” internally. Remove it as a fourth public ecosystem entry route until the board formally approves the brand and economics. End-user business can continue through white-label partners and broker relationships without creating premature brand complexity.
