# Commission Rules

## Local Franchise Inventory

Franchise earns local commission and pays:

- Royalty: 6% of GCI.
- Brand/network fee: 1.5% of GCI.
- OS fee: monthly platform fee.

## Private Collection

- Gross commission split 50/50 between HQ and franchise.
- Franchise pays reduced royalty/network fee on franchise half.
- HQ controls client/project process.

## Developer Sales OS

- Developer-team sales: Dproperty fee based on gross sale value, e.g. 0.5%.
- Dproperty/network-sourced sales: Dproperty fee, e.g. 2.5%–3%.
- Managed desk fee may be charged monthly.
