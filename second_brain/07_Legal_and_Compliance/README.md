# 07_Legal_and_Compliance — What We Are Bound By

**The question this folder answers:** *What are we bound by, and what has counsel actually approved?*

## What lives here

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `Legal Architecture` | Entity and contract structure |
| `Contract Templates Index` | Template library index |
| `Compliance and Legal Register` | Obligations register |
| `Compliance Package` | Franchise/operating compliance pack |
| `Data and Privacy Notes` | Privacy and data-handling posture |
| `Commission Rules` | Cross-offer commission rules |

Per-offer agreement notes live with their offer: `02_Offers/0X_…/06 - Legal.md`.

## Status — read this before relying on anything here

**No jurisdiction has independent counsel approval.** `[R]` Everything in this folder is internal drafting and reasoning, not legal advice.

**Highest-risk open items:**
- Local franchise, tax, privacy, brokerage, marketplace and AML advice for **every** launch jurisdiction.
- **VAULTED** marketplace legality: brokerage licensing, commission sharing, liability, AML. No `06 - Legal.md` exists for VAULTED at all.
- **Dproperty Select** investment-program exposure: suitability, disclaimers, marketing claims. No legal note exists.
- Trademark ownership and chain of title for software, content, templates and brand — including the name "BlankCRM".
- Franchise royalty and restricted-fund structure: 6% + 1% vs 4% vs flat 6% + 1.5%.

## Rule

Signed and executed documents do **not** live in Obsidian. They live in the secure legal archive; this folder holds reasoning and links.
