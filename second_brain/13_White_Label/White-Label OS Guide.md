# White-Label OS Guide

## Purpose

White-label OS helps boutique agencies professionalize operations while keeping their own brand.

## What It Includes

- Operating workflows.
- CRM setup.
- Dproperty OS workspace.
- Dashboards.
- Document structure.
- Projection framework.
- Training modules.
- Sales process.
- Implementation support.

## Select Access — REVISED 2026-08-03

> ⚠️ The previous blanket exclusion of Private Collection / Dproperty Select access is **retired**.

White-label partners **do** get Dproperty Select access, on **external-partner-broker terms: 1.5% of sale price**. Branded franchises get **2.5%**. The one-point gap, alongside brand recognition, is what justifies the higher branded entry fee. See [[../01_Strategy/Brand Architecture]] §3.2b.

## Focus Freedom — CLARIFIED 2026-08-03

Unlike the Dproperty branded franchise — which operates under a strict **investment-only** mandate (profitable and reliable product, not necessarily pretty and livable) — a white-label partner has **no focus restriction**. They may serve end users, families, livability-driven product, or whatever their local market rewards. This is a genuine benefit of the white-label route, not a limitation.

## Backing Without the Brand

White-label partners are publicly backed and supported, and may use **"powered by Dproperty."** That is **endorsement, not identity** — deliberately distinct from operating *as* Dproperty. They get the know-how and the tools; they do not inherit a decade of brand recognition, and the brand equity they build is **theirs**.

## What It Does Not Include

- The Dproperty brand or its trajectory.
- Investment priority.
- The preferential Select payout (2.5%).
- Unrestricted use of Dproperty IP.
- Direct developer relationship access.
