# White-Label Onboarding

## Steps

1. Agency discovery.
2. Process audit.
3. Data/template intake.
4. Workspace setup.
5. CRM setup.
6. Training.
7. Migration/import.
8. First pipeline review.
9. 30-day optimization.
10. Quarterly review.

## Key Risk

Do not let white-label clients copy the system without proper legal controls.
