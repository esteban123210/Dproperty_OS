# Private Collection Commission Split

## Baseline

Gross commission: 100%.

- HQ: 50%.
- Franchise: 50%.

On the franchise half:

- Reduced royalty: 3%.
- Reduced network fee: 1%.

## Example

Gross commission: $15,000.

- HQ direct split: $7,500.
- Franchise gross split: $7,500.
- HQ royalty on franchise half: $225.
- HQ network fee on franchise half: $75.
- HQ total: $7,800.
- Franchise before local costs: $7,200.

## Reason

HQ contributes curated inventory, developer relationships, project control, and quality assurance. Franchise contributes client origination, local trust, and sales activation.
