# Dproperty Select Commission (formerly Private Collection Split)

> **Revision 2026-07-04:** The 50/50 gross split is replaced by a **fixed percentage of
> sale price** paid to the originator. This preserves HQ negotiation upside and gives the
> franchisee a predictable number.

## Payout Structure

The originator (whoever brings/sells the deal) earns a fixed % of the **sale price**, not a share of commission:

- **Dproperty branded franchise: 2.5% of sale price.**
- **White-label partner: 1.5% of sale price** (external-partner-broker terms, revised 2026-08-03 from 2.0%; the earlier blanket exclusion on white-label access is retired).

HQ retains the balance of the negotiated commission.

## Projection Assumption

Always model Dproperty Select at **5% total commission**. If HQ negotiates 6%–8% on a select project, the originator payout stays fixed and **HQ captures the upside** — projections are never inflated.

## Example (branded, $300,000 unit, modelled at 5%)

- Total commission (5%): $15,000.
- Franchisee payout (2.5% of price): **$7,500**.
- HQ retains: **$7,500**.

## Example (white-label, $300,000 unit, modelled at 5%)

- Total commission (5%): $15,000.
- Partner payout (1.5% of price): **$4,500**.
- HQ retains: **$10,500**.

## Notes

- At exactly 5%, the branded payout equals the old 50/50 ($7,500). Above 5%, the fixed-% structure is strictly better for HQ.
- **Open item:** whether the franchisee pays any royalty/Network fee on Dproperty Select earnings. Default assumption: **no** (2.5% / 1.5% is net to the originator).

## Reason

HQ contributes curated inventory, developer relationships, project control, negotiation, and quality assurance. The originator contributes client origination, local trust, and sales activation.
