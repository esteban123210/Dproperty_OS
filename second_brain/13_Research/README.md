# 13_Research — What We Still Need to Learn

**The question this folder answers:** *What do we still need to learn, and who will answer it?*

## What lives here

`Research Backlog` — open research questions and evidence requests.

## Standing research requirements

These block investor-grade claims and are tracked as `[R]` Required evidence:

**Market**
- ICP-specific firm counts by country — *not* broad brokerage counts
- Agency size distribution in target markets
- First-geography decision evidence: Panama/Colombia vs broader

**Product and pricing**
- BluePrint willingness to pay by agency size at $399/$799 + setup
- Odoo/SAP implementation and comparable economics
- BlankCRM COGS from actual GHL plan, messaging and AI usage
- Founder/administrator time-cost evidence

**Network**
- VAULTED legal, take-rate and comparable marketplace economics per jurisdiction
- Attribution survival rate on off-platform closings

**Proof**
- Pilot usage, retention and value metrics
- Fully-loaded CAC with source attribution

## Rule

All public and investor quantitative claims must distinguish **official data / company data / assumption / management target** — and carry a truth label.

Conclusions do not stay here. Once settled they become canon (`01_Canon/`), strategy (`03_Strategy/`) or finance (`06_Finance/`).
